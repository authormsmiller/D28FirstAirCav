### contact-binh-tuy-1970-09-03-d28cav — tactical analysis (do not publish)
**UPDATED 2026-07-13 — Garvin responded. Wire-setting party theory retired.**

Garvin's account: pitch black, Craig outside the wire alone (reason unknown), tripped a
flare near another squad in the same platoon, fired on. The name of the individual who
fired is known to the researcher and held privately.

The before/after 1420-hour question is now largely moot — a nighttime incident is almost
certainly after the 1420-hour afternoon contact, but the timing relationship is less
significant than originally framed.

~~Wire-setting party theory (2-3 men, late-afternoon window, fragmented witness accounts):~~
Retired. The incident was at night and involved Craig alone outside the wire, not an
organized working party.

The McIntire "coming back from patrol into a remote base" framing is confirmed as a
compressed secondhand reconstruction — the kind that happens across several retellings
before it reaches Iowa.

**What remains open:**
- Why Craig was outside the wire — no one knows
- Grid position — unconfirmed; staff journal (NARA RG 472) is the primary target
- Whether a formal investigation was conducted

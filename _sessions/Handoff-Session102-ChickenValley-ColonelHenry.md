### Part 2 — Identifying "Colonel Henry" / "Stone Mountain 6"

Michael confirmed Captain Gonzales as Charlie Company's CO from his own knowledge, but
wasn't sure who the battalion commander was. A web search turned up **COL Frank Leonidas
Henry** ("Happy Tiger") — independently confirmed by the Vietnam Helicopter Pilots
Association and the Army Aviation Hall of Fame (Quad-A) as CO, 2/8 Cav, in 1968-69, and an
Air Assault pioneer going back to the 11th Air Assault Division (Test) at Fort Benning.

This immediately created a tension with an already-documented fact: `soldiers/gibney-john`
has LTC John V. Gibney as CO 2/8 Cav from 16 Mar 1968, still doing 2/8-affiliated
POW-negotiation work as late as 1 Jan 1969. Surfaced this to Michael rather than guessing;
his read was that Gibney had likely rotated to a 2nd Brigade staff role by then (a battalion
commander personally leading POW talks was always an odd fit) — noted as a working theory,
attributed to him, not asserted as fact.

Built **`soldiers/henry-frank/henry-frank.md`** from that research alone (status:
researching) — full service record from the VHPA/Quad-A bio (1965 Kinnard aide/Air Assault
pioneer → 2/7 Cav XO → 2/8 Cav command 1968-69 → Infantry School 1969-72 → TRICAP at Fort
Hood 1972-74 → 3rd Brigade/Chief of Staff, 101st ABN Div, Fort Campbell → died 1977).
Placed him in the 2/8 Cav command chain directly after Gibney, with an **estimated** October
1968 start based on Michael's stated ~6-month command-rotation pattern for this unit.

### Part 3 — Two clippings from Michael firm up Henry's profile

Michael dropped a `profiles/henry-frank/` folder with a portrait and the saved VHPA page,
then two more images over the course of the session (one initially landed in the wrong
folder):

1. **"Back From Vietnam"** (local Franklin, NC paper, 3 Apr 1969) — reports Henry's home
   visit having "just completed a second tour... as an infantry battalion commander with the
   First Air Cavalry Division," next duty station Fort Benning. **Pins the end of his 2/8
   Cav command to ~March 1969** — a ~6-month tour from the October 1968 estimate fits this
   cleanly. Also gives first family snapshot (wife Emma Lee Higdon, of Asheville; children
   Frank Joseph 13, Samuel Lee 7, Debora 5) and confirms "III" as his suffix and Franklin, NC
   as an actual hometown, not just birthplace.
2. **"New Chief Of Staff Dies"** (obituary, ~mid-Aug 1977) — full itemized decorations
   (Silver Star, 4x Bronze Star, DFC, Vietnamese Cross of Gallantry w/ Palm x2 and w/ Silver
   Star, 15x Air Medal, Meritorious Service Medal w/ 1 OLC, CIB, Master Army Aviator/Senior
   Parachutist badges — supersedes the vaguer "20+ awards" from 1969); cause of death (Berry/
   saccular cerebral aneurysm, 11:55 p.m. Fri 12 Aug 1977); precise later-career dates (3rd
   Brigade CO from 28 Mar 1976, Chief of Staff from 27 Jun 1977, succeeding COL Ted A.
   Crozier); 1977 family (wife "Ema," sons Joe/Frank/Samuel, daughters Debora/Christa).

Both saved as inline `documents:` entries on `henry-frank.md` with full local transcripts
(`henry-frank-back-from-vietnam-transcript.md`, `henry-frank-new-chief-of-staff-dies-
transcript.md`) and copies of the source images, following the Gibney-clipping precedent.
Updated the verbal-account document, the Dec 4 event page, and Gibney's own profile to
reflect the firmer end date.

Two loose threads flagged but explicitly **not** written up as theories, per Michael's
own call:
- Commissioning year conflict (1952 per the 1969 clipping vs. 1954 per the 1977 obituary) —
  left as a plain discrepancy note, no attempt to resolve.
- The 1977 obituary calls the 1976 3rd Brigade command his "third tour of duty at Fort
  Campbell," but only one earlier Fort Campbell period is documented here. Michael floated
  an arithmetic theory (a ~1970-71 second tour) but then **explicitly said not to add it as
  a theory** — it's a post-war detail and could just as easily be an old newspaper typo.
  Trimmed to a single low-priority sentence, no speculative dates attached.

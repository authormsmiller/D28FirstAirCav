## Operational Context

**Operation:** Operation Sheridan Sabre  
**Dates:** 7 November 1968 – 4 April 1969  
**Province:** Binh Long Province, III Corps, South Vietnam  
**Location:** War Zone C (Fishhook area / Saigon River infiltration routes near Cambodian border)  
**Higher HQ:** 2nd Brigade, 1st Cavalry Division, under Operation Toan Thang II (II Field Force, Vietnam)  
**Mission:** Interdiction of PAVN infiltration routes from Cambodia; destroy PAVN forces, facilities, and caches  
**Enemy:** PAVN (primary unit in area: 9th PAVN Division, with the 95C Regiment active Nov 1968)  

**Context of the specific date:** The day before — 3 December 1968 — Company D, 2/7 Cavalry (same 2nd Brigade) had a catastrophic contact northeast of Hớn Quản (Hon Quan District, Binh Long Province). They air assaulted into a landing zone and were hit by B-40 rockets, 82mm mortars, .51 cal machine guns, and command-detonated mines from an estimated battalion-size PAVN force. 24 US KIA, 1 missing; Sgt. John Noble Holcomb received the Medal of Honor posthumously. D/2-8 lost their three men the following day — either a continuation of the same general contact area, or a separate patrol action in the same AO.

**Province discrepancy to resolve:** DCAS/VVMF lists Stoltz's casualty province as **Tay Ninh**. Tay Ninh was the 1st Brigade's AO during Sheridan Sabre, not the 2nd Brigade's. This may be a DCAS error (see Session 63 lesson: DCAS province fields are unreliable). The ORLL for 1st Cav (period ending 31 Jan 1969) would clarify which brigade 2/8 Cav was assigned to and the precise action grid. Use event's reconstructed location as source of truth once confirmed.

## Key operational details

**Radio relay hill (slide 12):** A mountain between FSB Guinn and the rear area at Bien Hoa
blocked direct radio communications. A relay station was required on a nearby hill. Range Platoon
provided security for the relay on 22 Sep; the rest of D Co held the PUC ceremony at the base.
The relay hill's grid is not given but should be searchable in the gazetteer.

**PUC ceremony (23 Sep 1970):** General Putnam presented the Presidential Unit Citation to D
Company. Range Platoon missed the formation (on the relay hill). Garvin says he was also awarded
his CIB that day — which confirms 11B MOS soldiers in Range Platoon were formally recognized for
combat service by late September 1970.

**11th ACR operation (26 Sep – 30 Sep):** D Co was assigned to work with the 11th Armored Cavalry
Regiment. ACR secured the LZ; Range rappelled from a Chinook tailgate. Terrain too thick for
tanks. No-fire zone near a village required battalion permission to return fire — unpopular.

**B-52 strike (Oct/Nov):** Unit observed a B-52 Arc Light strike from Guinn at approximately
6 miles' distance — saw the aircraft, watched the bombs release, observed the shock wave and smoke
cloud, heard the thunder seconds later. Guinn's 105mm guns followed up on the target once smoke
cleared.

**FSB sequence:** Powder Ridge → [gap, 1–17 Sep, Waterman KIA 3 Sep] → Guinn (from 4 Sep) →
Silver. The stub for fsb-guin needs `successor: fsb-silver` added.

**Bien Hoa:** Referenced multiple times as the rear area — water tanks slung there, 105s slung
there for overhaul. Suggests Bien Hoa was the battalion's primary support base during this period.

## ORLL findings — Session 91 (2026-07-09)

Confirmed from AD0516259 and AD0515205 during the Waterman session. These resolve the two major
open questions that existed when this handoff was first written.

**Closing date resolved:** 26 Oct 1970. AD0516259 FSB table, p. 83: "GUIi ZT 100190 26 Oct 2-8 Cav
(qod by 199th)" — relieved by 199th Infantry Brigade. Stub has been updated.

**Successor confirmed:** FSB Silver (YT829043). 2-8 Cav moved to Silver same day, 26 Oct 1970.
AD0516259 Order of Battle (p. 61) confirms 2-8 Cav at Silver, Binh Tuy, 3rd Bde, as of 31 Oct.
Garvin's CLC slide corroborates: "By the time I returned to the field the FSB had moved to Silver."
Stub has been updated.

**Opening day context:** AD0515205, p. 30 — 15 Hoi Chanh ralliers at Guin on 4 Sep 1970, one armed
with a Soviet SS rifle. This is the unit's first full day at the firebase. Documents simultaneous
enemy desertions/defections and confirmed enemy presence in the immediate AO.

**3 Sep hostile contact:** AD0516259, p. 17 — D/2-8 Cav engaged 1 NVA at 1420 hours, grid
ZT076214 (2.4 km west of Guin). This occurred the day before Guin stood up, on the same day CPL
Waterman was killed in a friendly fire misadventure. A light event page has been built:
`contact-binh-tuy-1970-09-03-d28cav`. The key unresolved question is whether Waterman was killed
before or after 1420 hours — see the before/after timing analysis in the main session handoff
(Handoff-Session91-Waterman-Guin-PowderRidge-ORLLs.md). Jim Garvin has been asked this directly
(Michael Miller, 2026-07-09).

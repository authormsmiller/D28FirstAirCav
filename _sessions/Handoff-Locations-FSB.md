## What We Know (Verified or Primary-Source Supported)

### FSB Fanning

- **Named for:** CPT Martin Vincent Fanning, killed April 24, 1971 in the FSB Fontaine crash
- **Location:** MGRS YT 525 155 · 10°59'11"N, 107°18'42"E · decimal: `10.986389, 107.311667`
- **Modern landmark:** Just north of QL-1 / QL-56 intersection, northeast of Xuân Lộc city center. Rubber plantation fringe terrain in 1971.
- **Established:** Early May 1971 — McGrew calendar primary source
  - May 7: "Returned to Fanning" (already operational — implies prior visit)
  - Crash was April 24 → base named within ~2 weeks
- **Active through:** August 11, 1971 — last McGrew entry: "Convoy To Fanning"
- **Transition:** Aug 13 "CA to New Loc" → FSB Jeffries opens ~Aug 14

### FSB Jeffries

- **Named for:** WO1 Gabriel Augustus Jeffries Jr., co-pilot, killed same crash
- **Location:** MGRS YT 545 225 · 11°02'59"N, 107°19'48"E · decimal: `11.049722, 107.330000`
- **Modern landmark:** ~4–5 miles north-northeast of FSB Fanning, on higher ground toward Núi Chứa Chan (Chua Chan Mountain, 2,700 ft). Rural farmland today — no visible trace of base.
- **Opened:** ~August 14, 1971 — McGrew corroborated (Aug 13 CA to New Loc; Aug 14 crossed out)
- **Closed:** December 5, 1971 — **Gemini-sourced, unverified**
- **Relationship to Fanning:** Direct successor — 3rd Brigade relocated from FSB Fanning to FSB Jeffries same day. Both pilots' names stayed on Long Khánh maps until the unit stood down.

## Open Discrepancy

**FSB Fontaine rename vs. new position:**
The existing `fanning-martin.md` KIA entry originally said "FSB Fontaine was subsequently renamed FSB Fanning." The new source material describes FSB Fanning as a *new position established* near QL-1, distinct from Fontaine. These may describe the same event or genuinely different sites. The rename claim has been removed from the timeline entry pending verification. Needs checking against operational maps.

**Tail number discrepancy:**
FSB Fanning source text cites UH-1H tail number 69-15354. VHPA incident record cites 69-15692. One of these is an error. The VHPA record is the primary source — 69-15692 is more likely correct.

# Session Prep — FSB Powder Ridge (Garvin v02)
**Date written:** 2026-07-09
**Deck:** `locations/v02_powder_ridge (1).pptx` — 14 slides
**Stub:** `site/locations/fsb-powder-ridge/index.md` (created this session, gazetteer data only)
**Context:** Jim Garvin, Range Platoon, D Co, 2/8 Cav. Garvin arrived at his unit on 20 Aug 1970
as one of four replacements flown into a Daisy Cutter LZ on Powder Ridge. CPL Craig Waterman
(Range Platoon) was killed 3 Sep 1970 — Garvin had been with the unit for exactly 14 days at
that point. Powder Ridge is the backdrop for their shared service; Waterman never appears in
the Guinn deck.

## The Graphic-Advocate photo

The *Graphic-Advocate* (Rockwell City, May 12, 2021) caption reads:
> "Corporal Craig Waterman (bottom right) is seen during the loading of a log bird in this picture
> from August 1970."
> *Photo credit: Courtesy of Jim Garvin.*

This is almost certainly one of the log day slides (slides 2–7). Slides 3 and 5 are the strongest
candidates — both show men at log sites in August. If Garvin provides a higher-resolution version,
Waterman may be identifiable. **This is the only known photo of Waterman in the field.**

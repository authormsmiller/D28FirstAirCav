### The account

In conversation with Marvin's son, **Larry Fishell** (Cat Platoon, D Co, 2/8 Cav) recalled a Cat Platoon soldier — **William Lyons** — being **wounded in the stomach**. No date, location, or firebase was attached to the memory. This is the only account of the wound currently held, and the wound is **not recorded anywhere else in the archive**.

### Who Lyons is (what we already have)

William Lyons is already a known name in the unit roster, though he has **no profile yet**:

### Candidate dates

Michael's working list of when the wound could have occurred, with what the archive currently says about each:

**20 April 1971 — contact north of FSB Fanning — UNLIKELY.**
The recorded casualties were all **Range Platoon** (Cardwell, Drinkard, Hall KIA; SSgt Dillon WIA). **Cat** was on the **south bank** of the creek and took no recorded casualties. A Cat man wounded here is not impossible but runs against every existing account of that action. (See `contact-north-fsb-fanning-1971-04-20` / profile timeline 20 Apr 1971.)

**2 February 1971 — the "Trotter" contact — POSSIBLE BUT OFF-PATTERN.**
A documented action (`contact-trotter-1971-02-02`), confirmed by McGrew's calendar ("Contact," 2 Feb 71). But it was a **Range 3** fight (Jim Garvin's squad); the recorded WIA is **Sgt Joe Smith** (thumb shot off). For a *Cat* soldier to be hit here, Lyons would have had to be in or near a Range action — possible, but not the platoon in that fight.

**~11 May 1971 — Michael's leading guess — NO DOCUMENTED CONTACT.**
Nothing in the archive records a contact on 11 May 1971. The only event pinned to that date is the **battalion's move to FSB Fanning** — Jim Garvin's first mention of the base, "the battalion had just made another move" (his Fanning chapter runs 11 May – 30 Jun 71; see `locations/fsb-fanning`). So a wound around this date would sit in a **gap**, not a contradiction — consistent with an action simply not yet documented.

**July 1971 (while McGrew was on leave) — CALENDAR-BLIND WINDOW.**
McGrew's field calendar is one of the archive's anchor sources, but it cannot cover a stretch when he was **on leave and not with the unit**. Any action in that window is harder to verify precisely because the most reliable day-by-day source goes dark. Worth flagging as a "could be hiding here" period rather than a positive lead.

**21 October 1971 — the Makowski contact — UNLIKELY (per Michael).**
The contact in which Bill Makowski (Range 71) was killed (`contact-nui-ba-1971-10-21`). Listed for completeness; considered unlikely.

---

### Archivist Notes

- The strongest *positive* documentary lead is **2 Feb (Trotter)** because it is a confirmed contact — but it is a **Range** action, which cuts against a Cat casualty.
- The strongest lead by **process of elimination** is **~11 May**: it has no documented contact, which paradoxically makes it the most likely home for an *undocumented* wound, and it coincides with a battalion move (units in motion, fresh ground).
- **20 Apr** is well-documented and Cat-clear; the absence of a Cat WIA there is meaningful, not just silence.
- The **July leave window** is the genuine unknown — flag it but don't lean on it without a second source.

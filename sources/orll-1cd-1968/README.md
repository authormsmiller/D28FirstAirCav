# 1st Cavalry Division — Official Reports, 1968 (DTIC) — Source Index

Three DTIC documents pulled (July 2026) while investigating the **1LT Harrison
Woehnker Jr** unit conflict: Honor States lists his Unit/Group as "1st Cav
Division, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry, D Company" (the same D Co, 2/8 Cav
designation used for the May 1972 Chinook batch), while Virtual Wall gives his
actual unit as **C Battery, 2nd Battalion, 19th Artillery** — matching his MOS
(1193, Field Artillery Unit Commander) and rank (1LT). Working theory: as an
artillery officer he was serving as a Forward Observer or liaison officer
attached to D Co, 2/8 Cav when wounded, since 2/19 Arty was direct-support
artillery for 1st Brigade (which included 2/8 Cav). See his profile at
`site/soldiers/woehnker-harrison/woehnker-harrison.md` for the full writeup.

Woehnker was wounded 24 May 1968 (Quang Tri Province) and died of his wounds
18 July 1968 — so the correct ORLL quarter is **Period Ending 31 July 1968**,
not 30 April 1968.

Original PDFs are held in the research folder at `locations/` (not copied into
this sources folder): `11_or_hq_1st_acd_pe_30_april_1968_ad391571.pdf`,
`ORLL-30-Apr-68.pdf`, `ORLL-31-Jul-68.pdf`.

## Documents

1. **AD391571** — *Operational Report - Lessons Learned, Headquarters, 1st Air
   Cavalry Division Artillery, Period Ending 30 April 1968* (32 pp.).
   File: `locations/11_or_hq_1st_acd_pe_30_april_1968_ad391571.pdf`.
   **Wrong quarter** — ends three weeks before Woehnker was wounded (24 May).
   Confirmed by subject line via pdftotext; not otherwise useful for this
   research question, though it does contain a generic Lessons Learned item
   on non-divisional artillery battalions providing FOs to base-defense
   companies (Section 2, Personnel/Operations) — background only, not
   soldier- or unit-specific.

2. **AD392381** — *Operational Report - Lessons Learned, Headquarters, 1st
   Cavalry Division (Airmobile), Period Ending 30 April 1968* (74 pp.). This is
   the division-level counterpart to #1 (not DIVARTY-specific).
   File: `locations/ORLL-30-Apr-68.pdf`.
   **Also the wrong quarter**, same reason as #1. Covers Operations PEGASUS,
   JEB STUART II, and the opening of DELAWARE–LAM SON 216 (A Shau Valley,
   19 April on). No mention of 2/8 Cav in a way relevant to this question.

3. **AD393815** — *Operational Report of the 1st Air Cavalry Division
   Artillery for Period Ending 31 July 1968*, RCS CSFOR-65 (RI) (29 pp.).
   File: `locations/ORLL-31-Jul-68.pdf`.
   **This is the correct quarter** — already present in the research folder
   before this pass, not one of the two just added. See excerpt below.

## KEY FINDING — AD393815, 2d Battalion 19th Artillery disposition

Full passage in `EXCERPT-2-19-arty-may-jul-1968.txt`. Summary:

- 2/19 Arty spent the start of the quarter in the A Shau Valley (Operation
  DELAWARE) in direct support of **1st Brigade**.
- Extracted 16 May 1968; continued direct support of 1st Brigade in
  **Operation JEB STUART III**, "base area 101," from 17 May on.
- LZ Barbara established 10 June; B Battery went OPCON to 1/77 Arty
  (2d Brigade) on 26 June.
- By period's end, deployed HHB/LZ Betty, A/LZ Sharon, B/LZ Barbara, C/LZ Anne.

**1st Brigade in this period included 2/8 Cav** — consistent with (but not
proof of) the theory that a 2/19 Arty officer could have been the FO/LnO
riding with a 2/8 Cav company. The document does **not** name Woehnker, does
not mention 2/8 Cav or D Company by designation, and has no personnel-loss
annex. It's supporting circumstantial context, not confirmation.

- If a Division-level (not DIVARTY) ORLL for Period Ending 31 July 1968 turns
  up, it may have a dated contact log like the 1970 example and is worth
  pulling next — this index only has the DIVARTY report for that quarter.

## Follow-up (July 2026) — surrounding D Co, 2/8 Cav KIAs as corroboration

Checked the three nearest-in-time D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry losses
already in the archive for a location/date match against Woehnker's own
casualty record (wounded 24 May 1968, died of wounds 18 July 1968, both
Quang Tri Province per Virtual Wall):

- **ross-robert** (SGT Robert Ross, KIA 1 July 1968, Quang Tri Province, grid
  YD293319) — **closest match**. Same company, same province, and his death
  falls squarely inside Woehnker's wounding-to-death window (17 days before
  Woehnker died). His profile's admin notes already cite the D/2-8 company
  daily journal (see below) for a same-date, single-casualty contact.
- **fast-roger** (PFC Roger Fast, KIA 19 Aug 1968) and **golden-ronald**
  (SP4 Ronald Golden, KIA 20 Aug 1968) — **looser matches**. Same company,
  but Thua Thien Province, near LZ Carol/Hill 927, and about a month after
  Woehnker's death. These corroborate that D Co, 2/8 Cav was still intact and
  in the field through mid-August, and show the company moved out of Quang
  Tri into Thua Thien by then — useful for the unit's broader movement
  history, but they don't bear on Woehnker's specific location the way Ross
  does.

**Bonus find while checking Ross's sourcing:** his profile cites a "D/2-8
company daily journal" — this is `locations/Skipper Journal _ Vietnam War
Daily Log by Angry Skipper.html`, a veteran's blog transcribing the actual
D/2-8 Cav company journal day by day. The saved snapshot covers **3 June –
3 July 1968**, entirely in the YD-grid area (Quang Tri Province) — the same
grid zone as Woehnker's casualty location and Ross's YD293319. On 3 June, D
Co airlifted *from LZ Anne*. LZ Anne (YD 298394) is independently listed in
AD393815 (above) as **C Battery, 2/19 Artillery's** position for this same
quarter — and C Battery, 2/19 Arty is Woehnker's own unit per Virtual Wall.
That's the closest geographic link found so far between his parent battery
and the company Honor States credits him to.

The blog states it continues day-by-day through **22 July 1968** — four days
past Woehnker's death — but only the 3 June–3 July portion has been saved
locally. The live site (author "Angry Skipper") likely has entries for
mid-to-late July, and possibly late May if the author had journal access
predating his own arrival (he says June was "most of" his first month
in-country, so an entry for 24 May specifically is not guaranteed). Not yet
checked against the live site — worth a look if pursuing this further.

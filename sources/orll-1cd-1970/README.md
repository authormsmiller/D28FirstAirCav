# 1st Cavalry Division — Official Reports, 1970 (DTIC) — Source Index

Two DTIC documents pulled (June 2026) to confirm the **18 March 1970 D Co, 2-8 Cav contact**
in the Dog's Head, War Zone C — the action reported in CAVALAIR ("Alpha Blues Surprise NVA
Force," 8 Apr 1970) and the likely action in which **SGT Richard Wiseman** (HHC, attached to
D Co) was wounded and died of wounds.

Original PDFs are held in the research folder at `KIA/wiseman-richard/` (AD0512505.pdf,
AD0509767.pdf). Both have usable text layers; `EXCERPT-2-8-cav-march-1970.txt` holds the raw
pdftotext OCR of the two relevant sections.

## Documents

1. **AD0512505** — *Operational Report – Lessons Learned, Headquarters, 1st Cavalry Division
   (Airmobile), Period Ending 30 April 1970* (107 pp., CONFIDENTIAL / since declassified).
   DTIC: https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/tr/pdf/AD0512505.pdf
   Contains a dated daily contact log. The 2-8 Cav entries run early–late March 1970 and
   include the 18 March D Co contact (entry **(n)**, transcribed below).

2. **AD0509767** — *Senior Officer Debriefing Report, 1st Cavalry Division (Airmobile),
   period 23 April 1969 – 5 May 1970* (34 pp.). DTIC: https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/tr/pdf/AD0509767.pdf
   Section **10, "Operations in the Dog's Head,"** gives the commander's narrative: in early
   March 1970 the division moved into the Dog's Head west/south of Tay Ninh; first heavy
   fighting 26 March, then battalion-size contacts 29 Mar (FSB Jay), 1 Apr (Illingworth),
   16 Apr (Atkinson). ~700 enemy killed; 83 US KIA, 234 WIA across these actions.

## KEY ENTRY — AD0512505, 2-8 Cav contact log (cleaned transcription)

> **(n) 181110 Mar XT045833:** [D Co 2-8] received SA [small arms], AW [automatic weapons]
> and B-40 [RPG] fire from an est[imated] plat[oon]-size en[emy] force from the N, NE and S
> of their position. Eng[aged] w/org[anic weapons], Arty and A/S [air strike]. **Contact
> broke at 1125 resulting in 14 US WIA.** The following day at 1516 hrs a search of the
> contact area … revealed **2 fresh graves containing a total of 19 NVA KIA** [from] the
> contact the previous day. **Total casu: 14 US WIA and 19 NVA KIA.**

- DTG `181110 Mar` = **18 March 1970, 11:10 hrs**. Contact broke 1125 → **15-minute fight**.
- Grid **XT 045 833** (western War Zone C / Dog's Head).
- In March 1970, **2-8 Cav was under 1st Brigade** (AD0512505: "the 1st Bde (with the 2-8 Cav,
  2-7 Cav and 1-11 [ACR])…"; "1st Brigade AO … Western War Zone C (Dog's Head Area)").
- Battalion commander: **LTC Michael J. Conrad** (per HistoryNet "Rescue at Dog's Head").
- Enemy: **NVA 272nd Regiment** (identified in POW/document captures in the same AO, AD0512505).

## Cross-checks

- **Matches CAVALAIR** ("Alpha Blues Surprise NVA Force," 8 Apr 1970) on date (18 Mar), unit
  (D Co 2-8), weapon (small arms + B-40), the **15-minute** duration, and enemy dead found in
  graves the next day. Enemy count differs: **ORLL 19 NVA KIA** (confirmed by graves) vs.
  **CAVALAIR 39** (paper likely inflated, or conflated the 18 Mar D Co fight with the 19 Mar
  1/9 "Blues" series).
- **Wiseman:** the entry records **14 US WIA and no KIA**. SGT Wiseman is classified "Died of
  Wounds" 18 Mar 1970, Tay Ninh — i.e. one of the wounded who died of his wounds. The daily
  journal does not name individuals, so this is **near-certain, not name-confirmed**. A named
  confirmation would require the **2-8 Cav daily staff journal (NARA RG 472)** for 18 Mar 1970.
- **Jackson** (CPL Michael C. Jackson, D Co 2-8, KIA 17 Mar 1970, Tay Ninh): the ORLL contact
  log shows **no 17 March action** (it jumps 14 → 18 → 26 Mar), so his death is **not**
  corroborated here. Treat as a separate, unconfirmed action.

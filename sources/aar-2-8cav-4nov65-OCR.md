## Document identity

- Unit: **2nd Battalion (Airborne), 8th Cavalry, 1st Cavalry Division (Airmobile)**, APO San Francisco 96490.
- **Prepared 6 Nov 65, concerning the action of 4 Nov 65 (1015–1900), vicinity coordinates 979049.**
- Signed: **Capt. [G.] Linton, Infantry, Commanding** (CO, Co B).

## Clean summary of what the AAR establishes

- The action began as **Recon #14 patrol (Lt Ward + 17 men)**, departing **"Cavalair" (the 2/8th
  forward base)** ~0400(?) on **azimuth 030°**, mission to locate/destroy a reported village/camp
  ~600–900 m out. Captured a sick VC (~1025), then took fire from a small hill (point #3) and a
  camp (point #2). Lt Col **Hix** (bn cdr) ordered contact maintained.
- Elements committed in sequence: **Recon (Ward)** → **"Buckeye" (FSG Marshall + Sgt Julien, ~10 men,
  attached to Co B; Capt Linton CO Co B)** → **"Flanker Right" (Lt Trapnell, XO Co B; 2nd Plt Co A,
  a medic, Capt Slifer + interpreter, Lt Wiest)** → **"Right Guard" (Capt McIlrey/"Mellrey", CO Co A,
  + 1 plt Co A)** → **Co C (Capt Kercison)** guided in late by Lt Ward (~1835). PSG **Carver** (Plt Sgt,
  2nd Plt Co A) assumed command of the platoon after Lt Parrack was hit.
- Heavy sniper + B-40 fire on the hill from ~1250; Right Guard pinned ~100 m SSE of point #3; friendly
  artillery from Cavalair adjusted in. Camp and enemy equipment burned (could not be evacuated); 4
  friendly KIA had to be left on the hill and were recovered the next afternoon by Sgt Cantes, Co C
  mortar squad.
- **Enemy assessment (verbatim sense):** "one enemy **company-plus sized PAVN unit** located in a
  **rest area**, was surprised and abandoned [it] in a disorganized fashion; snipers and small groups
  resisted until ~1700, then withdrew to the north and east."

- **Confirms** all three American KIA already in `pleiku-campaign-1965-11-04` (Coffey, Hill, Hamill),
  and confirms each was recommended for the **Bronze Star w/V (posthumous)** — upgrading the
  "Bronze Star (unconfirmed)" notes on their profiles to a primary-source recommendation.
- **Adds a 4th KIA:** a South Vietnamese **interpreter ("Sgt Be," name uncertain)** — not a 2/8 Cav
  trooper, so not an archive soldier, but part of the same action.

  (cited on the event page) describes THIS action.
- **Location:** the AAR fixes the fight at **vicinity 979049, the "Cavalair" position** (matches the
  Pleiku Campaign AAR grid ZA978050) — the patrol moved ~600–900 m on azimuth 030 to an unnamed
  camp/hill. This is ~13 km from **Hill 732 (YA885106)**, the hill the page is currently titled for
  (a framing drawn from secondary accounts). The primary AAR supports relabeling the action to the
  **Cavalair-area patrol fight**, not Hill 732.

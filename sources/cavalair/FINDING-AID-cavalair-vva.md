# Finding Aid — *The Cavalair* & 1st Cav Unit-History Sources at Texas Tech (VVA)

**Purpose:** locate and harvest primary-source material for the **full D Co / 2-8 Cav unit history, 1965–1972** — not just Marvin's 1971 window. Built for Michael to search the Texas Tech Vietnam Virtual Archive (VVA) directly while parallel searches run elsewhere.

*Last updated: 18 Jun 2026. Item/collection IDs below should be confirmed on arrival — VVA records get re-numbered.*

---

## 1. What *The Cavalair* is (and isn't)

- The **1st Cavalry Division's weekly newspaper in Vietnam**, produced by the Division Information Office (APO SF 96490), printed by Pacific Stars & Stripes, Tokyo.
- Masthead from the 8 Apr 1970 issue: **CG** MG E.B. Roberts · **Information Officer** MAJ **J.D. Coleman** · **Press Officer** CPT V.M. Vukelich. (Coleman is why the surviving run sits in his donated collection.)
- The "**CAVALAIR**" masthead was introduced **9 July 1969**. Issues are numbered by Volume/Number (e.g., *Vol. 4, No. 14*, 8 Apr 1970; *Vol. 4, No. 15*, 15 Apr 1970 — the one we have).
- **Editorial bias to keep in mind:** it was a morale paper — upbeat body counts, downplayed US losses. Treat it as the Army's contemporary narrative, useful for **dates, places, unit movements, named soldiers, and company-level actions**, not for casualty accuracy.

**Coverage reality for our 1965–72 goal:**

| Period | Best source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1965 – mid-1969 | **Not** the Cavalair (predates the masthead). Use ORLLs/AARs, the *Memoirs of the First Team* book, Coleman Collection textual records. | 1st Cav arrived Vietnam Sept 1965 (Ia Drang Nov 1965). |
| Jul 1969 – ~Apr 1971 | **The Cavalair** (weekly). Core target. | Division HQ at Phuoc Vinh. |
| ~Apr 1971 – Jun 1972 | 3rd Brigade (Separate) era — the division's bulk left 29 Apr 1971; **3rd Bde stayed** (Marvin's command). Look for a **separate 3rd Bde publication** and ORLLs. | The Cavalair likely ceased when the division departed. |

## 3. How to search (recipes)

Run these in the VVA search box. Combine a unit term with a date range when possible.

**Find Cavalair issues:**
- `Cavalair` (quoted) → filter to the Coleman Collection / serials.
- Browse the Coleman Collection resource record (§2) for the **box/folder list of issues** — this is the fastest way to see exactly which dates survive.

**Find 2-8 Cav content (any era):** the OCR is inconsistent, so try several spellings —
- `"2nd Bn, 8th Cav"` · `"2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry"` · `"2-8 Cav"` · `"2/8 Cav"` · `"8th Cavalry"`
- Company-level: `"Delta Company" "8th Cav"` · `"D Company" "2nd Bn, 8th"` · likewise Alpha/Bravo/Charlie/Echo.
- **Caution:** "Delta Company" alone pulls in 1/5 Cav and 2/12 Cav too (this bit us already). Always pair with **8th**.

**Find places in our timeline (cross-checks the soldier record):**
- FSBs: `"FSB Mace"` · `Fontaine` · `Silver` · `Fanning` · `Jeffries/Jeffreys` · `King` · `"Relay Mountain"` · `"Song Be"` · `"Phuoc Vinh"` · `"Tay Ninh"` · `"Long Khanh"`.

**Find the pre-1969 history:**
- `"1st Cavalry" "Ia Drang"` · `"2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry" 1965..1968` · ORLL / "Operational Report Lessons Learned" 1st Cavalry.

## 4. Worked example — what a single issue yields (8 Apr 1970, Vol. 4 No. 14)

This one issue alone gave us **D Co 2-8 Cav** material (all pre-Marvin, all good unit history):
- **"Alpha Blues Surprise NVA Force"** — 19 Mar 1970, Dog's Head area, War Zone C. **Delta Co 2-8 Cav** inserted, linked up with the 1/9 Cav "Blues"; D Co stayed overnight, killed 1 NVA with a Claymore. The day prior (18 Mar) **D Co 2-8** had killed enemy in a 15-minute fight; **39 NVA found dead** the next day.
- **Photo:** "Delta and Echo Companies, 2nd Bn, 8th Cav disembark at Bien Hoa" for a 3-day VIP Center stand-down.
- **"Medevac Flies Despite Heavy Ground Fire"** — recon platoon, **Echo Co 1-8 Cav**, heavy contact near FSB Buttons.
- **Rice cache (Tay Ninh):** **Company C, 2-8 Cav** spent ~5 days loading a 126.5-ton cache.

**Takeaway:** expect ~1 in 2–3 issues to carry a usable 2-8 Cav item. A systematic sweep is worth it.

## 7. How to file what you find (so it slots into the archive)

- Drop downloaded issues in `sources/cavalair/` named by date, e.g. `cavalair-19700408.pdf` (or keep the site's `8-APRIL-70` style — just be consistent).
- For each 2-8 Cav item worth surfacing on the site, note: **date, Vol/No, headline, companies named, location, named soldiers, 1-line summary**. Those become unit-history entries / event records.
- Flag any issue naming a **rostered soldier** (cross-check the search index) — that's a direct profile hit.

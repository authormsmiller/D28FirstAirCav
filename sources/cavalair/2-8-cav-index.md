# CAVALAIR — 2-8 Cav Content Index (mirrored issues, Nov 1969 – Aug 1970)

Extracted from the ~20 issues mirrored at medicinthegreentime.com (J.D. Coleman Collection, Texas Tech). Scope: every item naming the **2nd Bn, 8th Cav** (or its companies A/B/C/D/E) — for the 1965–72 unit history. All pre-Marvin (he arrived 4 Dec 1970).

**Legend:** Co = company · ⭐ = roster name hit (cross-checked against site roster)

---

## 1970-04-08 · Vol. 4 No. 14

- **"Alpha Blues Surprise NVA Force"** — 18–19 Mar 1970, Dog's Head area, War Zone C. **D Co 2-8** inserted, linked with 1/9 Cav "Blues"; D Co stayed overnight, killed 1 NVA w/ Claymore. On 18 Mar a 15-min fight; **39 NVA found dead** the next day, killed by D Co 2-8. Backed by ARA/light fire team.
- **Photo:** "**Delta and Echo Companies, 2nd Bn, 8th Cav** disembark at Bien Hoa" for a 3-day VIP Center stand-down (line-company R&R rotation).
- **"Medevac Flies Despite Heavy Ground Fire"** — recon platoon, **E Co 1-8 Cav** (note: 1st Bn), heavy contact near FSB Buttons; multiple urgent medevac sorties under fire.
- **Rice cache (Tay Ninh):** **C Co 2-8** spent ~5 days loading a 126.5-ton cache (2nd-largest of the war) onto Hueys/Chinooks; perimeter + arty guarded it at night.
- **Photo:** RTO SP4 Carl Battaglina, **C Co 2-8**, crossing a stream "100 mi N of Saigon."

## 1970-04-15 · Vol. 4 No. 15

- **"2/8 Blasts Enemy"** (lead) — week 22–28 Mar 1970; div killed 175→343. **7-hr battle 26 Mar**, ~22 mi NNW of Tay Ninh, **88 NVA dead**. **C Co 2-8** on ground recon hit bunkers at 11:45 a.m.; **A Co 2-8** also engaged. Quote: Sgt **J. Hobson** (C Co); Lt **Jack Hugele** (Blues plt ldr, recurring). 3rd Plt leader cut down NVA at the B-40s.
- Incidental: **Bravo Co 2-8** mention; **E Co 2-8** book-ad (Sgt James Lackey).
- (Note: "Delta" items elsewhere in this issue = 1/5 Cav and 2/12 Cav — NOT 2-8.)

## 1969-11-26 · Vol. 3 No. 48

- **"NCOC School Training Pays Off"** — SSG **Clarence M. Self**, **B Co 2-8 Cav**. Action ~1 mi NW of **FSB White**: two NVA had his squad pinned down after a resupply; Self eliminated both in their bunker and pulled out a wounded squad leader (frags). Awarded the **Silver Star**, plus an **Oak Leaf Cluster** for a later period of continual contact near **FSB Ike**, where **B Co 2-8** was engaged almost daily by NVA estimated at battalion size. Mentions 1SG **David H. McNerney** (Medal of Honor recipient) in the same fight. Context: early/mid-Nov 1969.
- **Battalion correspondents** list names the 2-8 stringers: SP5 **Bob Robinson** and SP4 **Gerald Somerday**.
- (Adjacent but NOT 2-8: Capt Rocco Alexander, **E Co 1-8 Cav**, at FSB Ellen during the 4 Nov 69 fire-base attacks.)
- No roster name hits.

## 1969-12-03 · Vol. 3 No. 49

- **"Mom Keeps Mail Coming"** (Tay Ninh) — SP4 **Tony DeFillippo**, squad leader, **C Co 2-8 Cav**; human-interest piece (his mother had written 1,000+ letters in his ~year in-country, so he arrived ~Dec 1968). Light, but a named C Co 2-8 soldier.
- **2-8 correspondents** this issue: SP5 Bob Robinson, SP4 Gerald Somerday, SP4 Robert Fetters.
- (Adjacent, NOT 2-8: "Recon Platoon Catches NVA Napping" — **D Co 1-8 Cav** killer team near FSB Ellen / Serges Jungle Highway, PFC Michael Alongi, 1SG Earl Haney; and **C Co 1-8** flamethrower-sapper fight at FSB Ellen.)
- Thin issue for 2-8; no combat action, no roster name hits.

## 1969-12-10 · Vol. 3 No. 50

- **No 2-8 content.** Every "8th Cav" reference this issue is **1st** Battalion: B Co 1-8 (contacts NW of FSB Ellen, week ending 21 Nov), E Co 1-8 (cache N of Quan Loi), and a 1-8 "Buddies on patrol" photo spread near FSB Ellen (PFCs Paul Oliver, Robert Copeland, Don Hoesman).
- Standing 2-8 correspondents still listed (Robinson, Somerday, Fetters). No roster hits.

## 1969-12-17 · Vol. 3 No. 51

- **No 2-8 content.** Week/month-end combat roundup (Nov total 1,808 enemy dead — highest since Tet '68) features 1/9 Cav ("Rat Patrol" D Co 1/9), Trp A 1/9, Co D 229th AHB, 1/12, 2/7, 2/20 Arty ("Blue Pecker"). No A–E Co 2-8 items.
- Standing 2-8 correspondents only (Robinson, Somerday, Fetters). No roster hits.

## 1969-12-24 · Vol. 3 No. 52 (Christmas issue)

- **No 2-8 content.** Mostly holiday/human-interest. The "8th Cav" references are all **1st** Battalion: month-end recap notes "elements of 1-8 Cav" near Song Be (15 Nov, 14 NVA), and an S-1 human-interest piece on SFC Charles Shannon, **1-8 Cav** personnel sergeant.
- Standing 2-8 correspondents only. No roster hits.

## 1971-03-02 · Vol. ? — Mar 2 1971  *(TTU file `2mar71.pdf`; NOT in mirror — Marvin-era)* ⭐ relevant

- **"FSB FONTAINE" dateline, 2d Bn, 8th Cav:** the battalion added a **"Vietnam-style '500'"** to its athletics/recreation program — Skytroopers of the 2/8 racing **bicycles** one lap around the inside of the firebase berm, broken into five classes by rank (E-4 and below, etc.). Light feature, but it's **2-8 Cav at FSB Fontaine in early March 1971** — Marvin's unit, location, and timeframe (cf. his 9 Mar 71 letter "moved to a new base called Fontaine"). Candidate detail for the unit history / Marvin's profile.
- (NOT 2-8: a battalion surgeon item is **1-8 Cav** at FSB Buttons; "Echo Recon" feature is **1-7 Cav** near FSB Noble.)
- No roster name hits, but high contextual value for Marvin's window.

## 1971-04-14 · Standdown special, 12 pp.  *(TTU file `14apr71.pdf`; NOT in mirror — Marvin-era)* ⭐⭐ unit history

CG this issue: **MG George W. Putnam Jr.** (Roberts era over); Info Officer MAJ Barry L. Winzeler. A division-standdown special edition.

- **Division standdown ceremony — 26 March 1971, Bien Hoa** (Cav Rear HQ; site moved from Phuoc Vinh that morning). Article "Standdown ceremony typifies First Team." Attendees: **Gen. Creighton Abrams** (COMUSMACV, himself a former F Troop/7th Cav member, Oct 1936), **Lt. Gen. Michael Davison** (II Field Force), **Lt. Gen. Nguyen Van Minh** (III Military Region CG), **MG George W. Putnam Jr.** (div cdr). 1st Cav Band; **posting of the Division colors** closed the ceremony.
- **Colors → Ft. Hood, Texas**, to be flown for the **1st Cavalry Division (TRICAP)**, assuming the assets of the 1st Armored Division.
- **3d Brigade (Separate) — "Garry Owen"** remains in Vietnam; issue includes guidance for troops staying vs. redeploying, a "Garry Owen tradition" history piece, and division-history retrospective (Cav arrived Vietnam **Sept 1965**).
- **Confirms the 26 Mar 1971 standdown date** cited in Marvin's 26 Mar 71 letter note.
- **Use:** strong primary source for the existing `3rd-brigade-separate-garryowen-1971` event and the Unit History page. (No specific 2-8 company item in the passages scanned; it's division-level.)

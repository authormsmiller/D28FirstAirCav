An oral history interview with Henry "Rocky" Colavita, recorded 18 Jan 2016 by the Americans in Wartime Experience museum's "Voices of Freedom" oral history project (Vimeo channel: NMAW.org — the museum's former name, National Museum of Americans in Wartime). Title: "Henry 'Rocky' Colavita - Vietnam War - U.S. Army - 011816." Runtime: 45:19.

Michael has hand-transcribed most of this interview (roughly 38 of 45 minutes as of this update), surfacing significant new detail not available in any secondary source: the exact date and circumstances of Colavita's 1966 wounding, his stateside recovery and War College posting, his initial S4 assignment and the October 1968 start of his D Company command (succeeding CPT Rodney Grannemann), the sequence of firebases D Company operated from, his ambush tactics and command philosophy, and — most significantly — a detailed firsthand account of the January 28, 1969 bunker-complex fight that is very likely the same action in which SP4 Carl Pipher and CPL Warren Eskridge were killed. See colavita-henry.md's timeline and service record, and the colavita-henry-verbal-account-jan28-1969 document for the full transcript of that account.

Roughly 7 minutes of the interview remain untranscribed as of this update — further detail may still surface.

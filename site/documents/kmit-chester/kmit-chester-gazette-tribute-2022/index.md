Paraphrased from Emily Thurlow's Daily Hampshire Gazette article on a Haydenville-led
effort to restore sunken and overgrown military memorial plaques at St. Mary of the
Assumption Cemetery in Leeds, MA. Among the volunteers was Russ Warriner, a Vietnam
veteran, who pointed out several childhood friends and classmates memorialized there,
including Chester Jon Kmit.

Warriner recalled crossing paths with Kmit unexpectedly, in-country, on the very day
Kmit died — he did not learn of the death until he returned to his own unit. In his own
words:

> "I ran into him in Vietnam the same day he was killed." — Russ Warriner, quoted in
> the Daily Hampshire Gazette, Oct. 16, 2022

This is the first personal, named recollection on file of anyone who crossed paths with
Kmit in-country. It does not describe the circumstances of Kmit's death itself, and it
does not confirm whether Kmit has a memorial marker at this specific cemetery — only
that Warriner, a fellow Hampshire County native, considered him among the friends being
honored that day.

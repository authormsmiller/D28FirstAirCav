*Paraphrased from a "1st Cav Div Association" newsletter memorial column by James
Glenn "Top Gun" Dotson. The column marks Chester Jon Kmit's birthday (April 11) with a
short remembrance: it recaps his birth date, hometown of Williamsburg in Hampshire
County, Massachusetts, and his Wall listing at Panel 33W, Line 65. It states his MOS as
an infantryman ("11-B," described in the column as "grunt") and places him with D
Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry, 1st Cavalry Division, operating in Tay Ninh
Province at the time of his death on February 5, 1969.*

*Dotson, writing on behalf of the unit's veterans, describes Kmit in the column as an
unusually good soldier who was well liked by those who served with him, and closes with
a brief expression of remembrance and respect. The full clipping is preserved in this
folder for provenance; see the discrepancy notes above regarding the tour-start date and
MOS, neither of which is treated here as overriding the primary casualty-record
sources already on file for Kmit's profile.*

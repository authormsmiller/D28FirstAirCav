I met Craig on June 6, 1970 at Delta 2/8 company rear in Phuc Vinh. We were fresh out of the 1st Team Academy and headed to the field. The next morning a Huey takes us, John Blair, Fred Baumann, Craig and myself, to Cambodia. Craig and Fred go to Range platoon, and John and I to Cat Platoon.

Nearly three months later we leave Ben Hoa, where we were a quick reaction force, and head for an area near Fire Base Guinn surrounded by many mountains. The area was very wet and triple canopy. The mountains themselves has many steep cliffs. We had a new medic, Doc Walker.

On September 3rd we start up a very rugged area, it is pouring down rain and we set in for the night and set up our perimeter. Doc Walker sets up with me. Late in the night somewhere about 10 or 11 PM a trip flare pops and a night defense position open fire. Lots of scurring and we hear the call for a medic. Doc picks up his medical bag and takes off. A long time passes before he returns to our night position. He said very little when he came back. "Don’t say nothin man, I’ve never seen anything like that in my life, let me calm down."

The next morning we find out a Huey can not make it in to take Craig out. The whole net day Range carries him out wrapped in a poncho. Cat and Skull walk flank to Range. About 5 to 6 Pm we get a place along side of a small river across from a large field. About 8 Pm and darker than hell John Blair, myself and six others take him across the river to the large field. We expect an attack and a very shot time passes before a medivac arrives. No enemy activity occurs and we load Craig onto the chopper. I remember saying God bless you Craig. It had only been three month earlier that Craig, John Blaiir, Fred Baumann and myself had landed in Cambodia. After the chopper takes off we cross back over the river. We spend the night shaking, more from nerves than from being wet.

The next day was long and we continued our mission. I don’t remember anyone talking. There are many days I remember what happened to him and wonder why it was him and not me. I guess this is survivors guilt and we all suffer from it.

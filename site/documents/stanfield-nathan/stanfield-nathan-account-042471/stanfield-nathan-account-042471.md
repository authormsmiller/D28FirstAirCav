*Nathan Stanfield was the door gunner (E3, A/229 AVN) aboard UH-1H 69-15692 when it crashed on April 24, 1971. He posted this account as a Facebook comment in the D 2/8 CAV 65-72 group in response to a photo identification thread. It is reproduced here verbatim.*

---

Yes, I can I D the Firebase.....it was FB Fontain........I was the Gunner on the bird that Crashed on 4/24/71 just outside that FB....Part of D 2/8 came out and secured the crash and got me out from under it. Several of the men on the bird died in the crash, both pilots and two men that we was giving a ride to Bien Hoa. The CrewChief got the transmission in the back on was unable to walk. One of the men that jumped off the Bird helped him put out a small fire on the engine. I owe my life to the men that came out....how they got me out from under I have no idea....and the other can't tell me, they was watching for "Charlie"

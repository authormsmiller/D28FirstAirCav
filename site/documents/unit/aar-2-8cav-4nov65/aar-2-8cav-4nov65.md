The **battalion-level after-action report** for the **2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry's** action of **4 November 1965** — the company-and-platoon companion to the [division Pleiku Campaign AAR](/documents/unit/pleiku-campaign-aar-1965/), and the primary record behind the [casualty event](/events/pleiku-campaign-1965-11-04/). Prepared 6 November 1965 and signed by **Capt. Samuel Linton**, commanding officer of **Company D**.

## What it documents

The action began as **Reconnaissance patrol #14** (Lt Ward, 17 men) departing **Position "Cavalair"** on azimuth 030°, with the mission of locating and destroying a reported camp 600–900 m out. The patrol captured a sick prisoner (~1025), then took fire from a small hill (point #3) and an enemy camp (point #2). Lt Col Hix ordered contact maintained, and the battalion fed in successive elements through the afternoon:

- **"Buckeye"** — FSG Marshall and Sgt Julien (~10 men), attached to Co B
- **"Flanker Right (-)"** — Lt Trapnell (Co B XO), with 2nd Platoon, A Co, a medic, Capt Slifer + interpreter, and Lt Wiest
- **"Right Guard"** — Capt McIlrey, CO A Co, with one platoon of A Co
- **Company C** — Capt Kercison, guided in late (~1835) by Lt Ward

The enemy was assessed as a **company-plus PAVN unit in a rest area**, surprised and scattered; snipers and small groups resisted until about 1700, then withdrew to the north and east. The camp and the enemy equipment that could not be evacuated were burned.

## Casualties (per the report)

Consolidated friendly casualties for the Recon, Buckeye, and Flanker Right (-) elements: **4 killed** — **SGT Richard Coffey**, **CPL Eddie Hill**, **PFC Wright Hamill**, and a South Vietnamese interpreter ("Sgt Be") — and **9 wounded**, named in the report: Lt Parrack, Sgt Wilson (platoon sergeant), Sgt Baker, [Pvt/PFC] Frolleny, SP/4 Spencer, **SP/4 David "Doc" Wilson** (senior medic), SP/4 Patterson, Sgt Taylor, and PFC Ortiz. A mid-battle figure for the separate "Right Guard" (A Co) element — "5 KIA and 17 WIA" at ~1430 — does not reconcile with the final consolidated count and reads as an unrevised field report (see the casualty event for the reconciliation).

The report recommends decorations including the **Silver Star** (SSG Jones) and the **Bronze Star with "V"** for several troopers — among them posthumous awards for **Coffey, Hill, and Hamill**, and a Bronze Star with "V" for **SP/4 Wilson**.

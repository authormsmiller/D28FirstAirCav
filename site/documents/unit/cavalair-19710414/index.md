A twelve-page **standdown special edition** of *The CAVALAIR* — the 1st Air Cavalry Division's weekly command newspaper — marking the division's departure from Vietnam and the stand-up of its 3rd Brigade (Separate), the "Garryowen Task Force." Commanding General as of this issue: **MG George W. Putnam Jr.**

**The standdown ceremony.** The lead account, *"Standdown ceremony typifies First Team,"* reports that the division formally stood down on **26 March 1971** in a brief ceremony at the Cav's rear headquarters area at **Bien Hoa** — relocated from the long-planned Phuoc Vinh site only that morning. Present were **Gen. Creighton Abrams** (commander, U.S. forces in Vietnam, and himself a former member of F Troop, 7th Cavalry), **Lt. Gen. Michael Davison** (II Field Force), **Lt. Gen. Nguyen Van Minh** (III Military Region), and division commander **MG George W. Putnam Jr.** The posting of the division colors closed the ceremony; the colors were to be shipped to **Fort Hood, Texas,** and reflagged for the **1st Cavalry Division (TRICAP)**, which assumed the assets of the 1st Armored Division.

**The transition.** Other pages guide troops on whether they would remain with the **3rd Brigade (Separate)** or redeploy, recount the origin of the regiment's "Garry Owen" tradition, and review the division's history from its arrival in Vietnam in **September 1965**.

This issue independently **confirms the 26 March 1971 standdown date** referenced in SGT Marvin Dale Miller's letter home of 26 March 1971.

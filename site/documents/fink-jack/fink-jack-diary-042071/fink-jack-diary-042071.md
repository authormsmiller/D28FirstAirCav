*Jack Fink served with Range Platoon in 1971 but was stationed on Relay Mountain, apart from the platoon, at the time of the April 20 contact — likely on a communications relay position. The strikethrough text below reproduces words Fink himself wrote and then crossed out in the original diary; it is preserved here because it shows, in real time, how incomplete and mistaken word from the battlefield reached the rear and was later corrected. One detail in particular — who, exactly, had died of his wounds — was wrong in the moment and is addressed in the archive notes rather than corrected here, in keeping with preserving the diary as written.*

**Tuesday, April 20**

I feel pretty good this morning. It's now afternoon & I just heard that Range PLT. got into a bad contact. They humped into a bunker complex which was waiting in ambush. Results: 3-4 men dead & 8 wounded. Med is working out & there are 2 air strikes going on. I'm watching it all from the top of Relay Mountain. All it could have been me down there if I wasn't called up here.

*Pages 56–57*

**Wednesday, April 21**

It's hard to believe that buddies who I've been with for a long time are dead & wounded. Alpha Company was rushed into the area. And I'm getting my stuff ready in case I have to leave Relay Mountain to be with Range PLT. again. The gooks are still in the area. Range is unable to recover the 3 dead men or their heavies. There was another air strike.

**Thursday, April 22**

Range cut a log pad to get resupplied. Range recovered the 3 bodies & the rucksacks. Just found out one more man died of wounds. I'm still on Relay Mountain & haven't been called down yet. Note: Man did not die of wounds. Stan Dillion is still in critical condition. Delta Company is still humping, the mission must continue no matter what.

**Friday, April 23**

Last night everyone on Relay Mountain had a mad minute firing their M-16's. This morning 2 red cross donut dollies dropped by & entertained us. We got resupplied with clothes & hot food. It's hot today & I'm just soaking up the sun & writing some letters. I've been taking a shower every day. No word today about Range Platoon.

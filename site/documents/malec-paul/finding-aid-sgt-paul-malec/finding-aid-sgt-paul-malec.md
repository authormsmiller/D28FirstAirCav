A downloadable research packet on **SGT Paul William Malec** (D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry,
1st Cavalry Division) — assembled for his family as a starting point for their own research, not as
a finished biography.

It gathers what the archive holds and can reasonably infer about Paul: his identity and service; a
careful reconciliation of his 21 March 1966 wounding (the official "hostile / fragmentation wounds"
record set alongside his family's account, with the primary sources that reconcile them); the
operations his battalion conducted during his tour; the firebases and landing zones it held within
his service window (tiered by confidence, for matching against family photographs); the men around
him; everything the archive holds on him; and concrete leads for continuing the research.

**[Download the finding aid (PDF)](https://angryskipperarchive.org/media/photos/soldiers/malec-paul/finding-aid-sgt-paul-malec.pdf)**

*Photographs of Paul Malec courtesy of David Malec (great-grandson). The finding aid is a research
aid, not an official military record.*

Within a month of the time Jim Garvin left the field, his platoon was ambushed. Several members of
the squad were wounded badly enough to be medevaced. One of the men killed would have gone home
**the next log day**, having already qualified for a hardship discharge.

Garvin's photograph from afterward shows **Bill Holtzclaw**, arm bandaged, holding the radio he had
been carrying at the time of the ambush.

A question would haunt Garvin long after his own discharge — whether he could have prevented the
ambush if he had stayed in the field with his platoon.

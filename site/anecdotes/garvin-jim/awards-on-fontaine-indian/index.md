Because of the activity surrounding the ambush, several men won awards for valor, and on
**25 April 1971** an awards ceremony was held on **FSB Fontaine**.

Among those decorated was a man Jim Garvin knew as **"Indian"** — **Gary Collins** — a very good
friend, credited with bringing the squad back to safety.

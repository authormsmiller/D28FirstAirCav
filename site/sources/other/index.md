- year: 1966
  name: AD0833869
  type: caar
  unit: 1st Brigade (Airborne), 1st Air Cavalry Division
  start_date: 25 Mar 66
  end_date: 8 Apr 66
  pages: ~55
  file: AD0833869-caar-lincoln-1st-bde-1966.pdf
  source: DTIC
  references:
    - operation-lincoln-1966
  notes: 1st Brigade-level Combat After Action Report for Operation Lincoln only (25 Mar–8 Apr 1966), Pleiku and Darlac Provinces. CO 1st Brigade was Col. John J. Hennessey. Companion document to the Division CAAR (AD0824627) which also covers Mosby I. Key 2/8 Cav data — March 25: convoy from An Khe to Oasis (ZA115275), closing at 1521h; March 30: three companies moved from Oasis to LZ Typhus (YA855198), LZ Boil (YA950255), LZ Pox (YA975291); March 31: D Company and battalion CP trucked from LZ Pox to Oasis (1230h), A and C companies airlifted to LZ Dime (YV916815), B Company from LZ Boil to Dime; April 2–3: 2/8 Cav patrolling from LZ Quarter (YV876766); April 4–7: 2/8 Cav at LZ Cotton (YV843936)/Cloth (YV832942) with battalion CP at LZ Wool (YV856962); Lincoln terminated with 2/8 moving to Pleiku (Lake Bien Ho). OCR quality is poor (heavily degraded scan) but key unit data is legible. Moved from locations/new/ on 2026-07-10.

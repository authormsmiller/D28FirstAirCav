## What this document teaches about the 1967 gap generally

1. **This is the richest of the three 1967 documents by a wide margin**, and the first to
   approach the level of company-level detail found in the AD0506273 (1969) golden example.
   The difference appears to be genre, not chance: this quarter's operations (Song Re Valley
   recon, Bolling, Wallowa) were smaller, more dispersed, multi-brigade/multi-unit efforts
   that generated more individually-narratable actions than the larger set-piece contacts
   (Tam Quan, An Lao Valley screen) that dominated the Feb-Apr and May-Jul narratives.
2. **2/8 Cav's OPCON history for 1967 is now fully sketched across all three documents**:
   OPCON to the division's own 3rd Brigade for Pershing (Feb–Apr, AD0385642); an unresolved
   gap with no task-org table at all (May–Jul, AD0386215); grouped under 3rd Brigade again
   for the Song Re Valley recon (1–20 Aug) and then formally OPCON to the 173rd Airborne
   Brigade for Bolling (17 Sep–14 Oct, this document). Four different command relationships
   across three quarters — this is exactly the kind of history Task #11's timeline needs to
   capture explicitly, since "2/8 Cav" alone is not a stable brigade-affiliation label for
   any part of 1967.
3. **The Aug 9 Song Re Valley fight (A/2/8 Cav) is worth surfacing prominently** even though
   it doesn't tie to a KIA — it's the only sub-unit-named 2/8 Cav combat action found in the
   entire 1967 backfill, and useful context for anyone researching the battalion's actual
   operational tempo this year, separate from the KIA-cluster research.
4. **Verdict:** highest operational yield, lowest KIA yield of the three 1967 documents —
   the mirror image of AD0386215 (strong KIA lead, weaker task-org clarity). Together, the
   three documents give a genuinely complete OPCON picture for 1967 even though none of them
   resolved a single D Company KIA to a specific action. That remains a job for the 2/8 Cav
   Daily Staff Journal or an equivalent unit-level record — the same conclusion reached at
   the end of both prior reviews, now confirmed a third time.

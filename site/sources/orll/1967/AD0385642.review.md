## What this document teaches about the 1967 gap generally

1. **The March 1967 cluster (7 of the year's 21 KIA) needs a source below division
   level.** This ORLL confirms the operational frame (Pershing, 2/8 OPCON to 3rd
   Brigade) but narrates nothing at the company/battalion level for any of the nine
   in-range dates. A 2/8-specific Daily Journal (if one surfaces, per the spec's stated
   limitation) or the primary NARA 2/8 Cav DSJ (RG 472) are the sources that would
   actually resolve these.
2. **The OPCON swap is a real trap for future research on this quarter.** Any future
   pass that assumes "2/8 Cav = 1st Brigade" for Feb-Apr 1967 specifically will
   misattribute; 2/8 was with the division's own 3rd Brigade for Pershing during this
   window, not 1st Brigade, even though 1st Brigade is its organic administrative
   parent (confirmed separately by the Dazzlem-operation task org, which does show 2/8
   back under 1st Brigade for area-security purposes).
3. **"1st Brigade" and "3d Brigade, 25th Infantry Division" are two different things**
   throughout this document's prose, and the attached 25th ID brigade's actions
   (6 March Dam Tra-O contact) should not be read as 1st Cav's own 3rd Brigade (2/8's
   Pershing-period OPCON headquarters) without much closer reading than a division ORLL
   permits.
4. **Verdict:** honest low-yield-for-KIA, useful-for-operations outcome. Exactly the
   kind of result Step 4 of the spec anticipates ("a low-yield document may produce only
   a digest + manifest entry, no page -- a valid, honest outcome") -- except here the
   digest itself is valuable for the OPCON finding even though no KIA candidate resolved.

  notes: II FFORCEV semi-annual ORLL (parallel to IIFFV period covered by AD0515205, Oct 70). Covers post-incursion return through 1st Cav Div stand-down (Mar 1971) and 3d Brigade (Separate) stand-up. Key findings — 1st Brigade stood down 24 Mar 71; 2d Brigade stood down 12 Mar 71; 3d Brigade became 3d Bde (Separate) on 30 Mar 71, OPCON to II FFORCEV on 10 Apr 71; overall 1st Cav Div (-) OPCON passed to USARV 14 Apr 71. FSB table confirms 2-8th Cav at FSB Fontaine (YT 901119), Long Khanh Province, under 3d Bde (SEP) 1st Cav Div (AM). 2d Squadron, 11th ACR operated OPCON to 3d Bde until 16 Apr 71. Cover letter dated 24 September 1971. Moved from locations/new/ on 2026-07-10.

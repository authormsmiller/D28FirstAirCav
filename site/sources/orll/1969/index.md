- year: 1969
  name: AD0505650
  type: orll
  unit: 1st Cav Div Artillery (Airmobile)
  start_date: 1 May 69
  end_date: 31 Jul 69
  pages: 25
  file: AD0505650-orll-1cav-arty-jul69.pdf
  source: DTIC
  references:
    - fsb-carolyn
  notes: >
    1st Cav Div Artillery ORLL — companion to AD0506273 (divisional HQ, same period).
    Pulled for FB Carolyn source verification (2026-07-13). Key finding: LZ Carolyn
    overrun on 6 May 1969. Attack began ~0200, heavy volume of incoming (200+ rds
    incl. 107mm rockets, 75mm RR, 82mm/60mm mortars) followed by heavy SA/AW fire
    from a regimental enemy force. Firing batteries B/2/19 and B/1/30 returned fire;
    9 sections A/2/20 ARA committed (864 HE rockets, 239 flechette rockets). Ammo
    and POL dumps destroyed by direct hits. Attack suppressed at 0600. Casualties:
    9 US KIA, 64 US WIA, 101 NVA KIA, 29 NVA WIA. Enemy weapons captured/destroyed:
    42 AK-47s, 6 SKSs, 11 RPG-2s, 3 RPG-7s, 9 RPG-MGs, 2 x 60mm mortars, 2 radios.
    B/1/30 suffered 25 WIA within the battery. No D Company 2/8 Cav KIA incidents
    by name — artillery ORLL tracks unit-level engagements, not infantry casualties.
    Same night (6 May): LZ JOE also attacked — 4 US KIA, 25 US WIA, 8 NVA KIA.
    12–13 May: LZ JAMIE attacked by regimental force — 7 US KIA, 35 US WIA; LZ
    GRANT simultaneously — 5 US KIA, 40 US WIA, 45 NVA KIA. Note: DTIC page
    shows "AD505650" (6 digits); correct accession number is AD0505650 (7 digits).

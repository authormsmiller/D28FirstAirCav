# ORLL Sources — 1968

## Gaps

The 1st Cav Div HQ ORLLs for periods ending **30 Apr 1968**, **31 Jul 1968**, and
**31 Oct 1968** are not in the collection. The Jul 1968 artillery ORLL (AD0393815) is present.
The Oct 1968 HQ ORLL (estimated AD0499xxx–AD0502xxx range) is the primary research gap —
it covers the period of LZ Carol and related operations.

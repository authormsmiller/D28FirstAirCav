**FSB Saint Barbara** gets substantial new operational detail (see digest locations). Combined
with the existing `operation-montana-scout-1969` page — which places the 1st Brigade (2/8 Cav's
brigade during this window) at "LZs Grant, Carolyn, St Barbara, Ike, Jamie, and White" — this
chapter's own timing (Saint Barbara Feb-March 1969, then an April move to the LZ Carolyn area)
lines up almost exactly with Montana Scout's official 1 April 1969 start date. Two follow-on
implications worth Michael's attention:

- **The Feb 5, 1969 casualty-record "Tay Ninh Province" entry may simply be correct this time** —
  unlike the Dec 4/Jan 28 clusters (where Tay Ninh was very likely a DCAS error for the 2nd
  Brigade's actual Binh Long AO), 2/8 Cav appears to have already shifted to the 1st Brigade's
  Tay Ninh/War Zone C AO by early February, consistent with operating off Saint Barbara. If so, the
  existing event page's unresolved "2nd Brigade" Wriston remembrance is likely just outdated
  information reflecting the unit's earlier Rita-era assignment, not a live contradiction.
  **This is an inference from strong circumstantial fit, not a stated fact in the book** — flagged
  for Michael's judgment, not asserted as resolved.
- **The Saint Barbara-to-Carolyn move in April 1969 is a strong candidate for the actual
  transition into Operation Montana Scout**, with the Feb-March Saint Barbara period likely still
  falling under the closing weeks of Operation Sheridan Sabre (which existing sources already date
  to 7 Nov 1968 – 4 Apr 1969 — a near-exact handoff to Montana Scout's 1 Apr start).

## What this chapter teaches about mining this source going forward

1. **A "research stub, not confirmed" event page is exactly the kind of gap this pipeline is
   built to catch.** The Feb 5, 1969 event page's own blunt disclaimer made it an obvious target —
   worth specifically checking future chapters against any other event pages carrying similar
   stub/disclaimer language, not just KIA-list entries with no page at all (ch. 9's lesson) or
   thin `researching`-status profiles (ch. 8's lesson).
2. **Not every capture gap is harmless.** Ch. 9's missing "p6" turned out to be nothing; this
   chapter's missing "p14" is a real, acknowledged content gap (per Michael). Worth continuing to
   verify each gap on its own terms (content continuity + percentage counter) rather than assuming
   either outcome by default.
3. **Battalion-level personnel (commanders, S-3s) keep surfacing at a steady rate.** Wood and
   Bramlett bring the running count of named field-grade/battalion-staff officers across three
   chapters to Henry, Wood, Bramlett, and Gonzales (Charlie Co) — worth keeping half an eye on
   whether this archive wants a standing "battalion staff, 2/8 Cav, 1968-69" cross-reference page
   given how often these names now recur across chapters.

## What this chapter teaches about mining this source going forward

1. **Not every chapter is "all new" the way ch. 8 was.** Ch. 9 overlaps heavily with material
   Michael had already excerpted directly (Dec 4) or sourced from a separate interview (Jan 28) —
   the full-chapter read still earns its keep by surfacing an entirely new KIA (Velez-Rodriguez)
   that neither prior source touched, plus a fully independent second account of the Jan 28 fight.
2. **Photo captions can outrank existing citations.** The Karr "White Skull 6" caption is a
   stronger primary source than the secondhand roster currently backing that profile's platoon
   assignment — worth actively checking photo captions against existing "probable roster error"
   or similar hedged notes on other profiles as more chapters are read.
3. **Photo pages don't reliably sit at their narrated moment.** The photos.jpg group photo's own
   %-position would place it mid-chapter, but its content (Karr, confirmed arrived 12 Mar 1969)
   proves it depicts a moment weeks after this chapter's own last narrated date. Treat photo-page
   position as even less reliable than body-text page position, which itself already required the
   filename-collision caution learned in ch. 8.
4. **A KIA already on the roster list can still be a "new" find.** Velez-Rodriguez shows the KIA
   list and the event-page/document layer can drift out of sync — a name can sit on the roster for
   who-knows-how-long with `profile_status: —` and no narrative anywhere, until a source like this
   supplies one. Worth a periodic sweep comparing the KIA list against which entries actually have
   an event page, independent of chapter-by-chapter digesting.

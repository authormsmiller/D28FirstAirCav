# Chinook Crash KIA Checklist
**Event:** `chinook-crash-1972-05-10`
**Aircraft:** CH-47A tail number 64-13157
**Unit (aircraft):** 362nd Assault Support Helicopter Company (362 ASHC), 1st Cavalry Division
**Date:** May 10, 1972
**Time:** 1023–1025 hours
**Location:** Near Long Thanh North Airfield · UTM YT168032 · en route Biên Hòa → Vũng Tàu (R&R)
**Cause:** Material failure of a blade retaining pin
**Total killed:** 34 (5 crew + 29 passengers)

**VHPA Incident Record:** https://www.vhpa.org/KIA/incident/720510031ACD.HTM

## Cross-Reference Notes (2026-05-28)

Three sources cross-referenced: VHPA incident record · Wall D Co. data · D Co. unit honor roll.

- **21 confirmed D Co. organic** — the unit honor roll lists exactly 21 D Co. members, matching Robin Woo's count precisely.
- **7 Wall-listed names absent from honor roll** (Carr, Dunning, Hayes, Henaghan, Lahner, Lydic, Rivera-Agosto) — these are almost certainly the 7 non-D Co. passengers from A/1-12 Cav and 11th ACR, listed as D Co. on the Wall but not claimed by the unit. Moved to the non-D Co. section below pending confirmation.
- **SGT Mike John Aguilar** — in VHPA manifest but not on the D Co. Wall list. Confirmed non-D Co.
- **PVT James Douglas Groves** — confirmed D Co. KIA (Wall + honor roll). VHPA "NOT, A" is a data error. Wall spelling: **GROVES** (one O).
- **Wall panel data** — all 21 D Co. entries confirmed at Panel 1 West. See individual entries below.

## 1. Overview

FSB Hall was a **US artillery fire support base established to support Operation
Overlord** in early June 1971. It is named in a 1st Australian Task Force (1 ATF)
operation-level account of Overlord, alongside the note that **Headquarters 2/8
Cav remained at FSB Fanning** while three of its companies held blocking positions
along the **Suoi Luc**.

**Primary corroboration (Howard McGrew calendar):** McGrew's calendar records
**"June 5 - build FSB Hall"** -- matching the 1 ATF account's D-Day of 5 June 1971
date-for-date, and independently confirming the base name from inside D Co, 2/8 Cav.
The surrounding calendar entries place the company staging through Fanning and
combat-assaulting through the AO across the rest of the month (see Section 4).

---

## 2. What the source says

From the 1 ATF Operation Overlord account (D-Day, 5 June 1971):

> "2/8 Battalion 3 Brigade (US) 1 Cavalry Division (Air Mobile), deployed in
> blocking positions along the Suoi Luc with three companies, with Headquarters
> 2/8 Battalion remaining at FSB FANNING and a new US artillery FSB HALL
> established to support the operation."

Key points:
- **New** base — stood up specifically for Overlord (~early June 1971).
- **US artillery** role — fire support for the operation.
- Associated with **2/8 Cav** (3rd Bde, 1st Cav Div) and the Overlord
  hammer-and-anvil against the **3/33 NVA Regiment** in **AO Gold** (Long Khanh /
  Phuoc Tuy border area).

The 1 ATF account is **secondary** — compiled from Peter Scott (*Command in Vietnam*),
Mike English (*The Rifleman*), and Jerry Taylor (*Last Out*) — so the base name
and role are corroboration-grade, not primary.

**Likely battery (added Session 76).** Col Peter Scott's Overlord order of battle
(*Call to Remembrance*, 50th-anniversary address, 7 Jun 2021) lists **US Army Battery
C, 5th Battalion, 42nd Artillery**, armed with **155mm guns**, as the operation's US
artillery. Hall was *the new US artillery base* stood up for Overlord, and the 155mm
matches the heavy guns D Co photographed at **FSB Judy** on 4 Jun — so **C/5-42 FA is
the strongest candidate for Hall's battery** (it may also have manned Judy). Still a
lead, not a documented fact: confirm against the 2/8 Cav / 1st Cav journals (NARA RG 472).

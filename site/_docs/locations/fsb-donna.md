## 1. What Is Known

FSB Donna was a 1st Cavalry Division (3rd Brigade, Separate) fire support base
in Military Region 3. Unlike the Long Khánh Province outposts (Fanning,
Jeffries, Silver, Relay Mountain), Donna was positioned significantly further
west, closer to the defensive rings around Saigon — a different operational
role in the same brigade's area.

McDonald, Norm ("Range") was at Donna per his personal calendar (primary
source). No other confirmed D Co. personnel at this time.

## 3. Strategic Role

⚠️ *Gemini-sourced — treat as plausible, unverified.*

Donna served as an artillery platform in the counter-rocket defensive ring
around Tan Son Nhut Air Base and Long Binh Post. Primary mission was two-fold:

- **Counter-rocket shield:** Intercepting NVA 122mm rocket launch teams before
  they could set up against Tan Son Nhut and Long Binh
- **H&I fire:** Harassment and Interdiction missions into surrounding swamps
  and river approaches to block sapper infiltration toward Saigon

Artillery units cited by Gemini: 7th Battalion, 8th Artillery (7/8 Arty) and
Alpha Battery, 2nd Battalion, 12th Artillery (A/2-12 Arty).
⚠️ *Unit assignments unverified.*

## 5. Working Hypothesis — May 1971 Transit Context

⚠️ *Working hypothesis — not yet documented in primary sources. Recorded here
for investigation.*

The early May 1971 posting at Donna may reflect a unit movement tied to two
events that converged in late April / early May:

1. **The April 20, 1971 contact** (contact-fsb-fontaine-1971-04-20): Cat Platoon
   was ambushed approximately four days before the April 24 crash. Four soldiers
   were killed in the initial action and at least six more wounded. After a
   three-day engagement, Delta Company extracted the KIA and returned to FSB
   Fontaine on April 23.

2. **The April 24 crash** (crash-fsb-fontaine-1971-04-24): A UH-1 returning to
   Biên Hòa from Fontaine crashed on departure, killing the pilot, co-pilot, and
   Sgt. Richard Colburn. McGrew's calendar records "CA to Fontaine" on May 2 —
   the unit was still operating from Fontaine eight days after the crash.

3. **Vũng Tàu R&R pattern:** After a combat action at this scale, a company-level
   or element-level R&R rotation to Vũng Tàu would be consistent with 1st Cav
   practice. Vũng Tàu is approximately 90 miles southeast of Biên Hòa. A transit
   from Fontaine to Biên Hòa to the coast — or a staging stop at Donna's position
   in the peri-Biên Hòa defensive ring — fits the geography.

**Donna as transit point or brief posting:** At ~40 miles west/southwest of
Fontaine, Donna is geographically situated between the forward operational area
(Long Khánh) and the Biên Hòa rear base. A brief assignment to Donna in early May
could represent: a rotation off the forward line after heavy losses; security duties
in a less hostile environment during unit reconstitution; or a stop during transit
to/from Vũng Tàu. The presence of Range (McDonald) specifically — an individual
rather than a full company entry — may indicate a smaller detachment or a single-soldier
assignment rather than a full company rotation.

**Investigation needed:** Confirm McGrew calendar entry date; check whether other D Co.
soldiers appear at Donna in May; cross-reference Kint's Vũng Tàu trip timing (he
describes two in-country R&R trips from the bush, both after contacts).

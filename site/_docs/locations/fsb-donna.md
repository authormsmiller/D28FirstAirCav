## 1. What Is Known

FSB Donna was a 1st Cavalry Division (3rd Brigade, Separate) fire support base
in Military Region 3. Unlike the Long Khánh Province outposts (Fanning,
Jeffries, Silver, Relay Mountain), Donna was positioned significantly further
west, closer to the defensive rings around Saigon — a different operational
role in the same brigade's area.

McDonald, Norm ("Range") was at Donna per his personal calendar (primary
source). No other confirmed D Co. personnel at this time.

## 3. Strategic Role

⚠️ *Gemini-sourced — treat as plausible, unverified.*

Donna served as an artillery platform in the counter-rocket defensive ring
around Tan Son Nhut Air Base and Long Binh Post. Primary mission was two-fold:

- **Counter-rocket shield:** Intercepting NVA 122mm rocket launch teams before
  they could set up against Tan Son Nhut and Long Binh
- **H&I fire:** Harassment and Interdiction missions into surrounding swamps
  and river approaches to block sapper infiltration toward Saigon

Artillery units cited by Gemini: 7th Battalion, 8th Artillery (7/8 Arty) and
Alpha Battery, 2nd Battalion, 12th Artillery (A/2-12 Arty).
⚠️ *Unit assignments unverified.*

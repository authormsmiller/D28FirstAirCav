## 1. Overview

FSB Guinn was Delta Company 2/8 Cav's primary operational base in the autumn
of 1970, immediately before Miller, Marvin Dale arrived in-country. The company
operated from Guinn from approximately September through November 1970, then
shifted south to establish FSB Silver. When dad touched down in early December
1970, the company was just settling into Silver — the mud of Guinn was still
fresh on the rucksacks of the veterans he was joining.

Guinn is the earliest confirmed position in the D Co. operational sequence for
this deployment cycle.

## 4. Strategic Role

⚠️ *Gemini-sourced.*

Guinn sat near the transport corridors surrounding Xuân Lộc, positioned to
bring 1st Cav artillery within range of jungle trails running out of War Zone D
and the May Tao Secret Zone. Full battery of 105mm howitzers provided fire
support for D Co. platoons on week-long clearing operations in the surrounding
brush.

## 5. Life on the Base (Autumn 1970)

⚠️ *Gemini-sourced — attributed to "Jim Garvin + 1" (Garvin, James and at
least one other D Co. veteran). Garvin's photos and accounts are the primary
documentary source for this period.*

The company operated at Guinn through the tail end of the monsoon season.
Veteran accounts describe:

- Thick, ankle-deep red clay mud throughout the base interior
- Subterranean sandbagged bunkers roofed with timber and corrugated tin
- Entirely airdropped resupply — water, C-Rations, artillery shells via
  CH-47 Chinooks
- Sharp contacts with local Viet Cong units during jungle patrols

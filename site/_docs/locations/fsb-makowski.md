## 1. Overview

FSB Makowski was named for PFC William John Makowski, Delta Company 2/8 Cav,
killed in action on October 21, 1971 during a contact in Long Khánh Province.
It follows the same naming tradition as FSB Fanning and FSB Jeffries — a base
established to honor a fallen member of the unit, on the same ground where they
operated.

Miller, Marvin Dale was with the CP (Command Post) when Makowski was killed,
a few hundred yards from the contact. He departed Vietnam on December 2, 1971 —
four days after FSB Makowski opened.

## 5. Miller, Marvin Dale — CP Position

Miller, Marvin Dale was with the Command Post during the October 21 contact
in which PFC Makowski was killed. The CP is the tactical nerve center of a
company-level engagement — radio operators, platoon leaders, the company
commander, artillery coordination. The contact was happening a few hundred
yards from his position.

This is one of the final major combat events before his December 2 departure.

*Note: This detail was shared directly and should be treated with appropriate
care in how it is eventually surfaced on the public site.*

The existing archive event record `contact-nui-ba-1971-10-21` documents this
engagement. Gemini describes the operational context as:

- Dense, booby-trapped jungle corridors in Long Khánh Province
- Near the Song Dinh river area
- Hidden enemy positions and heavy explosive devices

## 7. Naming Tradition

Makowski follows the pattern established by Fanning and Jeffries:

| Base | Named for | KIA date | Base opened |
|---|---|---|---|
| FSB Fanning | CPT Martin V. Fanning | April 24, 1971 | ~Early May 1971 |
| FSB Jeffries | WO1 Gabriel A. Jeffries Jr. | April 24, 1971 | August 14, 1971 |
| FSB Makowski | PFC William J. Makowski | October 21, 1971 | November 28, 1971 |

In each case, the base was established within weeks to months of the soldier's
death, in the same operational area where they fell.

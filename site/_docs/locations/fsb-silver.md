## 1. What Is Known

FSB Silver was a 1st Cavalry Division fire support base in Long Khánh Province,
Military Region 3 (III Corps). Miller, Marvin Dale was stationed there; he
references it as "Silver" in his letters.

The base was dismantled prior to his garrison rotation at Relay Mountain
(estimated March–April 1971). Its former location was visible from the summit
of Relay Mountain on a clear day.

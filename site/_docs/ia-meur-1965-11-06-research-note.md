# Research Note — Ia Meur River action, 6 November 1965

**Status:** Ruled OUT for the archive. **No published event page.** Filed as a research note only.
**Date researched:** 29 June 2026 (Session 81)
**Decision (Michael):** D Company was not involved, so the action does not belong in a D Company
archive — "as awful as the actual event was."

---

## The question

Was **D Company, 2/8 Cav** involved in the 6 Nov 1965 battle on the Ia Meur River?

## Finding — NO. It was a **B and C Company** action.

On 6 November 1965, on the **west bank of the Ia Meur River**, two companies of the 2/8 Cav —
**Company B and Company C** — moved out from the battalion's overnight position. A **B Company
platoon** made first contact with an NVA squad, pursued it, and ran straight into an **entrenched
NVA battalion** (a battalion of the 33rd Regiment; the **6/33 Battalion was nearly annihilated**
in the exchange). The rest of **B Company** reinforced the pinned platoon; **C Company** was the
other committed company. **26 Americans were killed.** The action is described as a prelude to the
LZ X-Ray and LZ Albany fights.

Context: part of **Operation All The Way** (27 Oct – 9 Nov 65), the Chu Pong phase of Operation Long
Reach / the Pleiku Campaign. The 2/8 Cav (1st Air Cavalry Brigade) had been pursuing the NVA 33rd
Regiment since its 19 Oct attack on the Plei Me Special Forces camp.

**D Company is not named in any account of the 6 Nov action.**

## Why D Co is not in it (and the limit of that claim)

- Secondary accounts (Coleman, *Pleiku*; the Pleiku Campaign After Action Report) name **only B and C**
  as the maneuver companies on 6 Nov.
- The one primary doc already on file — the **2/8 Cav AAR for 9–10 Nov 65** (filed under
  `events/operation-all-the-way-1965/sources/AAR-2d-8th-Cav-9-10Nov65.pdf`) — is **three days later**
  and a **separate event**: by 9 Nov the battalion was securing base STADIUM (YA201342) with **D Co on
  the north perimeter, B Co south, C Co reserve, A Co detached** to the 1/8 Cav airfield. It says
  nothing about D Co on the 6th.
- So the proper statement is "D Co is **not mentioned** in the 6 Nov narrative," not "D Co is
  documented elsewhere on 6 Nov." To pin D Co's actual location/role on 6 Nov 65 to a primary-source
  standard you'd need the **2/8 Cav daily staff journal (NARA RG 472)** — the same pull already queued
  in the S79/S80 handoffs for the Jan 67 and Nov 68 actions. Worth bundling into one RG 472 request.

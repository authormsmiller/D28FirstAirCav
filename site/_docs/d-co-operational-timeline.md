# D Co, 2/8 Cav — Operational Timeline (Coverage Tracker)

**Purpose:** the operational counterpart to `d-co-kia-list.md`. This tracks **everything we can
evidence D Company, 2/8 Cav doing in Vietnam — casualty or not** — and how much of it the site
has captured yet. It is the backbone for Tier 3 of the `coverage-model.md`: the complete picture
of how the unit's mission evolved across the war.

**How to read the status column:**
- ✅ **covered** — an operation context page (Tier 2) and/or event page (Tier 1) exists and is published.
- 🟡 **partial** — some pages exist but the operational frame is thin or hedged inside an event page.
- ⬜ **gap** — KIA and/or known activity for this period, but no page yet.
- 🔎 **candidate** — a likely operational frame from general 1st Cav Division history, **not yet
  confirmed for 2/8 Cav** against unit records. Confirm before asserting on a public page.

**KIA counts** are from `d-co-kia-list.md`. **Next step for confirmation** is almost always the
2/8 Cav daily staff journal / AARs at **NARA RG 472**, and the division **ORLLs** at the Texas
Tech Vietnam Center & Archive / DTIC — see `primary-records-finding-aid.md`.

Division arrived in Vietnam **mid-September 1965**; AO progression: **Central Highlands (II Corps,
1965–early 1968) → I Corps (1968) → III Corps / War Zones C & D (late 1968–1971) → War Zone D /
Saigon–Long Binh defense (1971–72)**. The division stood down in Vietnam **26 Mar 1971**; the
**3rd Brigade (Separate), "Garryowen Task Force"** (with 2/8 Cav) stayed until **late June 1972**.

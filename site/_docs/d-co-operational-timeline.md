# D Co, 2/8 Cav — Operational Timeline (Coverage Tracker)

**Purpose:** the operational counterpart to `d-co-kia-list.md`. This tracks **everything we can
evidence D Company, 2/8 Cav doing in Vietnam — casualty or not** — and how much of it the site
has captured yet. It is the backbone for Tier 3 of the `coverage-model.md`: the complete picture
of how the unit's mission evolved across the war.

**Includes non-combat deaths.** Disease (e.g. malaria), non-battle injury, accident, and
self-inflicted deaths are part of the unit's experience and belong on this timeline alongside
combat actions — recorded honestly per `coverage-model.md` (Tier 1, "Non-combat deaths count
too"). The KIA counts below are hostile-death clusters from `d-co-kia-list.md`; that roster now carries
a casualty-type **Cause** column (added 2026-06-16) so non-combat deaths no longer hide inside a "KIA" list.

**How to read the status column:**
- ✅ **covered** — an operation context page (Tier 2) and/or event page (Tier 1) exists and is published.
- 🟡 **partial** — some pages exist but the operational frame is thin or hedged inside an event page.
- ⬜ **gap** — KIA and/or known activity for this period, but no page yet.
- 🔎 **candidate** — a likely operational frame from general 1st Cav Division history, **not yet
  confirmed for 2/8 Cav** against unit records. Confirm before asserting on a public page.

**KIA counts** are from `d-co-kia-list.md`. **Next step for confirmation** is almost always the
2/8 Cav daily staff journal / AARs at **NARA RG 472**, and the division **ORLLs** at the Texas
Tech Vietnam Center & Archive / DTIC — see `primary-records-finding-aid.md`.

Division arrived in Vietnam **mid-September 1965**; AO progression: **Central Highlands (II Corps,
1965–early 1968) → I Corps (1968) → III Corps / War Zones C & D (late 1968–1971) → War Zone D /
Saigon–Long Binh defense (1971–72)**. The division stood down in Vietnam **26 Mar 1971**; the
**3rd Brigade (Separate), "Garryowen Task Force"** (with 2/8 Cav) stayed until **late June 1972**.

## 1967 — 18 organic KIA (+2 attached) — ✅ covered (Tier 2 frames complete; Tier 1 clusters pending)
- Operation pages (all Tier 2): `operation-thayer-ii-1966` (runs to 12 Feb 1967) frames the
  `contact-binh-dinh-1967-01-28` cluster (Keller, Yates, 28 Jan); `operation-pershing-1967` (NEW,
  12 Feb 1967 – 19 Jan 1968) is the year UMBRELLA — 2/8 Cav role confirmed via the firebase-by-year
  data (companies at ~26 Binh Dinh LZs across the year); `battle-of-tam-quan-1967` (NEW, 6–20 Dec,
  sub-op of Pershing) frames the `1967-12-11` cluster. That cluster now has its own Tier 1 event
  `contact-tam-quan-1967-12-11` (D Co 22:15 night ambush of PAVN escaping south, per the 1st Bde
  Combat AAR / DTIC AD390613) and both soldier profiles are built (`follett-allan`, `paulson-john`).
- **Tier 1 clusters still to build** (each wires up to Pershing): 1967-03-01 (Burton, R.E. Johnson) ·
  1967-03-18 (M.N. Johnson, Woodall; +Willis, Middleton att.) · 1967-05-30 (D.I. Nelson, Sutt) ·
  plus singles 3/12 Van Gieson, 4/2 V. Williams, 5/23 Bohmer, 5/31 Krueger, 6/1 Ehlers, 6/5 McComb,
  6/13 Houser. Each needs the 2/8 Cav daily staff journal (NARA RG 472) for the day-of action.
  **Santoroski note:** PFC Michael Paul Santoroski (9/26 casualty date) is a **DOW case, NOT a
  Bolling casualty**. His wounding incident was September 6 — before Operation Bolling began (Sep 19).
  He stepped on a land mine in the Pershing AO (Binh Dinh), was evacuated, and died at a U.S. Army
  hospital in the Philippines on September 26. Profile built: `santoroski-michael`. Province "Unknown"
  on the Wall is consistent with death outside Vietnam. The 9/26 casualty date falls inside the Bolling
  window but the action was Pershing.
- **19 Sep – 14 Oct 1967 — Operation Bolling (173rd Airborne OPCON, Tuy Hoa):** Battalion alerted
  mid-September. IFFORCEV directed 173d Abn Bde to conduct the operation with two battalions west of
  Tuy Hoa — 4th Bn 503d Inf (173d organic) and TF 2/8 Cav (OPCON from 1st Cav Div, effective 17 Sep
  67). Staged at SF camp west of Qui Nhon Sep 17–18; assault into the Bolling AO 19 Sep 67; returned
  to Pershing AO 14 Oct 67.
  **ORLL CONFIRMED (AD0387543, 2026-07-07):** Results — Enemy KIA 21, PW 12, SA WPNS CAPT 8;
  Friendly KIA 0, WIA 7. Characterized as "scattered contacts." Zero friendly KIA closes the D
  Company question definitively. Enemy: 95th NVA Regiment (HQ, 5th NVA Division) — same unit in the
  AO from 1967 through mid-1968 per AD0394510. Operation Bolling II (173d Abn Bde's continuation of
  the same AO mission after 2/8 Cav departed) commenced 17 Sep 1967 and ran into 1968.
  Sources: AD0387543 (1st Cav Div ORLL, period ending 31 Oct 1967) ✅; AD0394510 (173d Abn Bde ORLL,
  period ending 31 Jul 1968, Operation Bolling II section) ✅; B Co memoir, eagerarms.com ✅.
  Page: `operation-bolling-1967` (Tier 2, updated 2026-07-07 with primary sources).
- **July 1967 — Camp Radcliff (An Khe):** Battalion spent the month on special duty at Camp Radcliff
  providing base security and conducting operations in the An Khe area. Coincided with change of command:
  **LTC Jack Dashiell relieved by LTC John Stannard** (July 1967; both profiles updated). Source: B Co
  memoir, eagerarms.com. The July ORLL (AD0386215, period ending 31 Jul 67) shows 3d Brigade had just
  returned from Operation Greely (Kontum, under 4th Inf Div OPCON through 25 Jul) and was re-entering
  the Pershing AO — consistent with the battalion drawing down at Radcliff before the Song Re push.
- **1–19 Aug 1967 — Song Re Valley:** Battalion placed under 3d Brigade OPCON for the first major recon
  in force into the Song Re Valley, southern Quang Ngai Province, I Corps (no formal operation name —
  designated "3d Bde Reconnaissance in Force into the SONG RE Valley" in the ORLL). Valley was a known
  VC stronghold; recon aircraft had already taken heavy AA fire before the operation opened.
  2/8 Cav assaulted north of the valley 3 Aug. On 9 Aug: B Co assaulted **LZ Lou** at 0737H to secure
  the battalion CP and firebase for **C Battery, 2/19th Arty (105mm)**; C Bty operational ~0900H;
  A Co then assaulted **LZ Pat** (BS330477, Hill 450) at 0935H under arty prep — took heavy contact
  (automatic weapons, mortar, 57mm RR from est. 2–3 NVA companies), pulled back, 46 TAC AIR sorties.
  13 Aug: 2/12 Cav moved to LZs north of 2/8 Cav. 17 Aug: D Co staged at LZ Jane (BS362596).
  18 Aug: D Co extracted from LZ Tom (BS347482 / PZ) — NOT a direct return to Pershing; the
  battalion was airlifted to **LZ Moberly**, a Special Forces camp south of the combat area in
  the Song Re Valley, where they spent the night (small airstrip). Morning of 19 Aug: battalion
  airlifted by Caribou from LZ Moberly to **LZ English** (Bong Son, Binh Dinh). Closed Pershing
  AO ~20 Aug.
  Results: 42 enemy KIA, 2 POW, 242 detainees; US 7 KIA, 34 WIA (brigade-wide).
  Sources: 1st Cav ORLL AD0387543 (period ending 31 Oct 1967); B Co memoir, eagerarms.com;
  B Co account of 18–19 Aug staging sequence (Moberly → LZ English by Caribou).
  Location pages built: `lz-lou` (BS400425, confirmed), `lz-jane`, `lz-tom`, `lz-moberly`
  (Ba To AF, BS558321, confirmed). Operation page: `song-re-valley-1967` (Tier 2).
- **Next:** build the cluster event pages; Pershing context page once 2/8 Cav role is documented.

## 1970 — 10 organic KIA (+1 attached) — ✅ covered (Tier 2 frame built; Tier 1 clusters pending)
- **Cambodian Incursion** (1 May – 30 Jun 1970; officially Toan Thang 43/45/46) — the year's
  defining 1st Cav Division operation and the first large-scale U.S. airmobile assault outside
  South Vietnam. **2/8 Cav role now confirmed at published-history level:** the CMH airmobility
  volume (ch. XI) names **D Company, 2/8 Cav** as the unit that discovered an enemy
  communications-supply depot in Cambodia (25 May–9 Jun), with **A Company** finding an
  automotive-parts cache; the firebase data places 2/8 Cav at FBs inside Cambodia (Gonder,
  Picatinny East) and notes FB Eunice as the "last 3rd Bde unit to leave Cambodia." 2/8 Cav was a
  **3rd Brigade** battalion (Task Force Shoemaker) for the incursion.
- Page: `operation-cambodian-incursion-1970` (Tier 2 UMBRELLA, NEW). Frames the year's losses in
  three phases — the winter–spring War Zone C border buildup (incl. the **Battle of FSB
  Illingworth**, 1 Apr 70: C & E Cos 2/8 Cav, SGT Peter Lemon's Medal of Honor — battalion
  context, not a D Co action), the May–Jun Cambodia phase, and the autumn return to III Corps.
- **Tier 1 pages:** `gonder-mine-1970-05-19` (NEW — incident, non-combat: CPL Kenneth Gonder
  killed by an Allied land mine in Binh Duong, 19 May 70; his profile, the FB Gonder location page,
  and a sister's-tribute document were built alongside it) · `rava-friendly-fire-1970-02-18` (NEW
  — incident, hostile DOW / friendly fire: PFC Henry Tony Rava, artillery/rocket/mortar misadventure,
  18 Feb 70; sourced from AD0512505; no named operation, 1st Brigade interdiction in War Zone C;
  wires to operation-cambodian-incursion-1970 pre-incursion phase). Still to build (each wires up

  (19 May, same date as Gonder — "hostile" tag still to be re-checked) · Waterman (3 Sep,

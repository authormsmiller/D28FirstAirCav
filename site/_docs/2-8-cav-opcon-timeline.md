# 2/8 Cav — OPCON & Task-Organization Reference Timeline

**Purpose:** a quick-reference for figuring out which higher headquarters 2/8 Cav (and,
where it matters for reading a document's prose, the division's own 1st and 3rd Brigades)
was actually under at a given date — **before** requesting NARA records or citing a brigade
in a page. "2/8 Cav = 1st Brigade" is **not** a safe assumption for any part of 1967 covered
so far. This doc exists because that assumption is exactly the kind of trap that would send
a NARA request (RG 472) to the wrong unit's records, or misattribute a division-ORLL
narrative passage to the wrong battalion.

**Created:** 2026-07-15 (Session 98), after the digest-and-cross-reference pass on all three
1967 division ORLLs (AD0385642, AD0386215, AD0387543). Companion to
`orll-digest-and-cross-reference-spec.md` and `primary-records-finding-aid.md`. Each row below
is sourced to a specific document's `.review.md` — follow the citation back to the full
reasoning and page numbers before relying on this table alone for a NARA request.

**Coverage so far: 1967 only** (Feb–Oct, three quarters). 1965–66 and Nov 1967 onward are not
yet reviewed against a source document for OPCON purposes — see "Open windows" below. Extend
this table as more ORLLs get the same digest-and-cross-reference treatment; don't assume the
pattern from 1967 carries into other years.

---

## How to read this table

- **2/8 Cav's status** is the column that matters most for a NARA RG 472 request — it tells
  you which brigade's (or other headquarters') records would actually contain 2/8 Cav's
  daily staff journal cross-references for a given window, and which brigade's ORLL/AAR
  narrative would plausibly describe 2/8's actions if the battalion itself isn't named.
- **Confidence** follows the same convention as the digest passes: `high` = an explicit
  task-org table or an unambiguous OPCON sentence in the source; `medium` = inferred from
  narrative prose that doesn't use the word "OPCON" but is operationally unambiguous;
  `low` / `open question` = genuinely not stated in the document, flagged rather than guessed.

## The mirror image: 3rd Brigade's own OPCON departures in 1967

Worth tracking separately, because narrative passages in these documents often say simply
"3rd Brigade" — and during two separate windows this year, the division's own 3rd Brigade was
itself operating away from the division under someone else's OPCON. A document that
mentions "3rd Brigade" in passing during either window is NOT describing a division-internal
action.

| Dates | 3rd Brigade's status | Operation | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24 Jun – 25 Jul 1967 | OPCON to the **4th Infantry Division** (Francis Marion), Kontum Province — a genuinely different theater (Central Highlands / II CTZ interior) from the division's normal Binh Dinh coastal AO. A separate battalion task force also went OPCON to the 173rd Abn Bde at Dak To one day earlier, 23 Jun. | GREELEY | `orll/1967/AD0386215.review.md`, Pass 3 | high |
| **1 Oct 1967 – continuing past 31 Oct** | OPCON to the **Americal Division**, deployed over 100km to **I Corps Tactical Zone** (Chu Lai / Tam Ky, then Hill 63/LZ Baldy) — a full brigade move (3 battalions: 1/7, 2/7, 5/7 Cav, plus support), the highest-casualty operation of the Aug–Oct quarter (46 US KIA / 480 WIA as of 31 Oct). Still ongoing at the end of the document reviewed; end date not yet known. | WALLOWA | `orll/1967/AD0387543.review.md`, Pass 3 | high |

Two OPCON departures for 3rd Brigade in a single year (GREELEY, then WALLOWA) — this is a
recurring pattern for this brigade in 1967, not a one-off, and WALLOWA's open end date is
itself an open question for whatever ORLL comes next.

## Practical implications for NARA requests

- **There is no single "2/8 Cav = [Brigade X]" designation that holds for all of 1967.** A
  NARA RG 472 request for "2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry" daily staff journal records should
  specify the exact date range being sought, since the battalion's operational parent (and
  therefore which brigade's records might cross-reference it) changed at least four times
  this year (see table above).
- For the **Pershing/Feb–Apr window specifically**, records search should account for BOTH
  1st Brigade (2/8's organic administrative parent) AND the division's own 3rd Brigade (2/8's
  actual Pershing-period OPCON headquarters) — a document filed under either could be
  relevant.
- For the **Bolling window (17 Sep–14 Oct)**, "TF 2/8 Cav" records may be cross-referenced
  under **173rd Airborne Brigade** records rather than 1st Cavalry Division records — worth
  checking both when requesting.
- The **May–Jul 1967 gap** (2/8's brigade assignment genuinely unknown) and the **two
  short undocumented gaps** (20 Aug–17 Sep; 14–31 Oct) are exactly where the 2/8 Cav daily
  staff journal itself (not a division or brigade ORLL) would be needed to establish the
  actual operational control for those specific weeks.
- 2/8 Cav's own commanding officers across this year, for cross-checking any record by CO
  name: **LTC Thomas H. Tackaberry** (through 7 Feb 67) → **LTC John C. Dashiell** (7 Feb – 30
  Jul 67) → **LTC John E. Stannard** (from 30 Jul 67, continuing through the end of the
  October document's window).

## First 1969 data point (2026-07-20) — unverified, single secondary source

**5 February 1969** (KIA date of `edmonds-james` and `kmit-chester`, both D Co, 2/8 Cav,
Tay Ninh Province): a VVMF Wall of Faces remembrance for James Edmonds, posted by **Dennis
Wriston** (dated 7.12.2026 on the page — a modern tribute, not a wartime document), states:

> "Corporal James Thomas Edmonds, Served with Company D, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry
> Regiment, **2nd Brigade**, 1st Cavalry Division, United States Army Vietnam."

This is the first explicit brigade attribution found for 2/8 Cav anywhere near 1969. If
accurate, it means battalion DSJs filed under **1st Brigade** for this date are very likely
the wrong brigade's records — 2nd Brigade's files (or wherever 2nd Brigade's cross-references
to 2/8 Cav landed) would need to be checked instead. **Confidence: low** — this is a single
named remembrance-comment source, not a task-organization table or primary military record
(the same standard that earns "high" confidence elsewhere in this doc). No corroboration
found in Chester Kmit's own Wall of Faces remembrances (personal/family tributes only, no
unit detail). Worth checking against AD0502597 (1st Cav Div HQ ORLL, Nov 68–Jan 69, already
on hand but not yet digested) or AD0504766/AD0504857 (candidate but unconfirmed AD numbers
for the Feb–Apr 69 quarter) before relying on this for a NARA request.

## 1 August – 31 October 1969 — 2/8 Cav under Division Artillery (high confidence)

**TAB A, Task Organization**, in the 1st Cavalry Division (Airmobile) Operational Report —
Lessons Learned for the quarterly period ending 31 October 1969 (DTIC AD0508303, PDF pp. 96-97 /
internal pp. 91-92), is an explicit, formal task-org table. It places **2-8th Cav under "e. Div
Arty"** — alongside HHB Div Arty, 2/19th Arty, 2/20th Arty, 1/21st Arty, 1/77th Arty, Btry E
(Avn) 82nd Arty, and 1/30th Arty — **not** under 1st Bde (2-5th Cav, 2-7th Cav, 1-12th Cav), 2nd
Bde (5-7th Cav, 2-12th Cav), or 3rd Bde (1-5th Cav, 1-7th Cav, 1-8th Cav) for this quarter.

This is a genuinely surprising placement for an infantry/cavalry battalion, verified against the
table image itself (not just OCR) to rule out a transcription error — the table plainly lists
"2-8th Cav" as the first line under the Div Arty heading. **Confidence: high** (clean, explicit
task-org table, the same standard that earns "high" elsewhere in this doc) for the *fact* of the
placement; the *operational meaning* of an infantry battalion being OPCON to Div Arty for this
period is itself an open question — possibly artillery-support-coordination role, possibly a
document quirk, not yet explained by anything else in this source.

**Tension with the adjacent quarter:** the previous quarter's ORLL (AD0506273, 1 May – 31 Jul 69,
already digested for `operation-toan-thang-iii-1969`) describes 2/8 Cav as organic to the 1st
Brigade, operating in War Zone C under Montana Raider / Montana Scout / Kentucky Cougar. Within
the Aug-Oct 69 quarter's own narrative, the actual day-to-day contact log (11 and 16 August, FSB
Becky / War Zone C — see `contact-tay-ninh-1969-08-12` and `contact-tay-ninh-1969-08-16`) is fully
consistent with a normal 1st Brigade War Zone C posture, not anything Div-Arty-specific. So the
task-org table's placement does not obviously match the battalion's documented combat activity for
the same quarter — flagged as an open question rather than resolved either way. Does not
corroborate or contradict the Session 103 Wriston "2nd Brigade" data point (5 Feb 1969, a different
quarter, different question — organic brigade vs. OPCON-to-arty are not necessarily the same
question).

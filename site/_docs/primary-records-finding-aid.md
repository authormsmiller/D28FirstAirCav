# Primary Unit Records — Finding Aid (Texas Tech, DTIC, NARA)

**Purpose:** locate the primary records that establish what D Co, 2/8 Cav, 1st Cavalry
Division was *doing* operationally during its 1969 casualty concentrations — the May 1969
spike and the October 8 river crossing — **before** drawing connections or building profiles.
**Created:** 2026-06-15 (Session 67). Companion to `may-1969-casualty-cluster.md`.

**Reading order:** start with the free, online **Division ORLLs (Section 1)** to fix the
operation name and area of operations, then go to **battalion/company daily journals and AARs
(Section 2)** for day-by-day tactical detail.

---

## 1. 1st Cavalry Division Operational Reports — Lessons Learned (ORLL)

Quarterly division reports. They give the **operation names, area of operations, narrative
summary, and statistics** — not per-platoon detail, but they fix the operational frame. All
are free and online at DTIC (apps.dtic.mil); most are also mirrored, OCR'd and full-text
searchable, at archive.org.

| Report | Period covered | Why it matters | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AD0506273** | **ending 31 Jul 1969** (May–Jul) | **The May 1969 cluster.** DTIC abstract already states the division operated in **Tay Ninh, Binh Long, Binh Duong, Phuoc Long** — matches George Brown & Dunkle (Tay Ninh). **Start here for May.** | https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/html/trecms/AD0506273/index.html |
| **AD0508303** | **ending 31 Oct 1969** (Aug–Oct) | **The October 8 river crossing.** Covers the Song Be crossing window. **Start here for Oct 8.** | https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/tr/pdf/AD0508303.pdf |
| AD0509767 | 23 Apr 1969 – 5 May 1970 | Senior Officer Debriefing Report (division CG's retrospective over the command tour) — high-level context for all of 1969. | https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/tr/pdf/AD0509767.pdf |
| AD0509007 | (Div Artillery, ~1969–70) | Division Artillery ORLL — supporting-fires context. | https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/tr/pdf/AD0509007.pdf |
| *(to confirm)* | ending 30 Apr 1969 (Feb–Apr) | Context for the quiet Mar–Apr before the May spike — locate the AD# via DTIC search. | apps.dtic.mil (search) |

Caveat: an ORLL is a **summary**. It will name the operation and AO and may describe notable
contacts, but it will rarely name an individual soldier or a single platoon's day.

## 2. Battalion / Company tactical detail (daily staff journals + AARs)

This is where a specific day (May 2, 12–13, 25; Oct 8) is reconstructed.

- **2/8 Cav daily staff journal / duty officer's log + after-action reports → NARA RG 472**
  (Records of the U.S. Army, Vietnam / USARV; unit records of the 1st Cav Div, 2nd Bn 8th Cav).
  These are the primary day-by-day records. **Not** held as a unit collection at Texas Tech —
  request from NARA (College Park) under RG 472, by unit and date range.
- **Sister-battalion context at Texas Tech — 1/8 Cav "Jumping Mustangs" Association Collection
  (#369).** Roughly 7 linear feet, **daily staff journals 1965–1971** (Boxes 1–4), plus a CD,
  **369EM0221 — "1/8 Cavalry Association Daily Staff Journals 1965–1969."** 1/8 and 2/8 Cav
  were sister battalions in the same division; the 1/8 journals are useful for the **adjacent
  operational picture** in the same AO and dates. Finding aid:
  https://vva.vietnam.ttu.edu/repositories/2/resources/346
- Note: **no dedicated 2/8 Cav association collection was located at Texas Tech** (the 1/8
  "Jumping Mustangs" donated theirs; the 2/8 apparently did not). For 2/8-specific journals,
  NARA RG 472 is the source.

## 3. Texas Tech — Virtual Vietnam Archive: how to search

Portal: **vva.vietnam.ttu.edu** (catalog/finding aids) and **vietnam.ttu.edu/virtualarchive**
(digitized items). Useful tools off the search bar:

- **Operations search** — by operation name, dates, and units involved. Use it to identify the
  operation(s) running in Tay Ninh in May 1969 (candidates: the **Toan Thang** series), then
  pull the associated reports. Operations database table:
  https://www.vietnam.ttu.edu/resources/operations/table.php
- **Advanced search** — search by collection name/number, box, folder, author, date.
- **Maps search** — to resolve grids (e.g., the Oct 8 crossing at XT 921 449 on the Song Be).
- **Reading Room** (readingroom.vietnam.ttu.edu) — for copyrighted/restricted materials.
- **Ask a Reference Question** — https://www.vietnam.ttu.edu/resources/request.php — TTU staff
  will help locate 2/8 Cav material and confirm what's digitized vs. on-site only.
- Search guide (PDF): https://vva.vietnam.ttu.edu/shared/guides/Guide_Using%20the%20Virtual.pdf

Ready queries (run from the search box):
- `"2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry"` and `2/8 Cavalry` — any catalogued 2/8 items.
- `"Toan Thang"` (+ year 1969) — operation reports/AARs for the III Corps offensive umbrella.
- `"1st Cavalry Division" "Operational Report" 1969` — division reports held at TTU.
- Lead to chase: TTU finding-aid resource `/repositories/2/resources/2162` surfaced as a
  1st-Cav ORLL-related record — open and confirm what collection it indexes.

## 7. 1971 records — the 3rd Brigade (Separate) era

By spring 1971 the 1st Cavalry Division had ceased Vietnam operations and redeployed; its
**3rd Brigade (Separate), "Garryowen Task Force,"** was activated **30 April 1971** to carry on,
and **2/8 Cav was one of its maneuver battalions** (with 2/5, 1/7, 1/12 Cav, plus F/9 Cav and
D/229th AHB; brigade HQ at **Bien Hoa**). Its AO was **War Zone D / "Cav Country"** — about
3,500 square miles covering the eastern approaches to Saigon and Long Binh, interdicting enemy
infiltration and supply routes. So for the 1971 D Co events (the **April 1971 FSB Fontaine**
contact and crash in Long Khanh, the May 1971 Chieu Hoi escort, the Oct 1971 Nui Ba contact),
the relevant higher records are the **3rd Brigade (Separate)** records, not the departed
division's.

Division/brigade-level reports located so far (free, online at DTIC):

|---|---|---|---|
| **AD0520447** | Senior Officer Debriefing Report, **CG, 3rd Bde (Separate), 1st Cav Div** — **11 Apr – 13 Dec 1971** (dated 13 Nov 1971) | The commander's reflections on employing the separate airmobile brigade; spans the **April 20–24 1971 Fontaine** window and the brigade's War Zone D mission/AO. Frame, not day-by-day. | https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/AD0520447.pdf |
| **AD0523510** | "1st Cavalry Division, 13 December 1971" | Companion 1971 report — confirm exact scope on open (the 13 Dec date matches the close of AD0520447). | https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/tr/pdf/AD0523510.pdf |

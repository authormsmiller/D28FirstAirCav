# 1st Cavalry Division (Airmobile) — Vietnam Operations: Research Paper Trail

**Purpose:** a year-by-year menu of the **division's** named operations in Vietnam, to use as a
**paper trail** for finding D Company, 2/8 Cav's specific involvement. D Co almost certainly took
part in most of these as an organic battalion of the division (and later the 3rd Brigade
Separate), but this list does **not** assert that — each operation is a lead to confirm against
unit records, not an established D Co action. Pair with `d-co-operational-timeline.md` (our
coverage tracker) and `d-co-kia-list.md` (the casualty clusters that should fall inside these
operations).

**Created:** 2026-06-16. **Primary seed:** TogetherWeServed, "Distinguished Military Unit: 1st
Cavalry Division (Airmobile) Vietnam" (synopsis, staged in the KIA folder) — secondary; confirm
operations and dates against the division ORLLs and CMH histories.

**How to use a lead:** for any operation below, (1) confirm the division's role/AO from the CMH
campaign histories, (2) confirm 2/8 Cav's part from the division **ORLL** for that quarter (two
are already staged in the KIA folder: **AD0506273** and **AD0520447**) and the **2/8 Cav daily
staff journal / AARs (NARA RG 472)**, then (3) match it to the D Co KIA dates for that year.

## 1965 — ✅ covered (3 D Co KIA)
- **Pleiku Campaign** (23 Oct – 26 Nov 1965) → **Operation Long Reach** → **Operation All the Way**
  (1st Bde pursuit) and **Operation Silver Bayonet** (Ia Drang, X-Ray/Albany). II Corps, Central
  Highlands. The division's first campaign and first Presidential Unit Citation.
- D Co: in country Sept 1965; first losses at Hill 732, 4 Nov 1965.
- Pages: `deployment-vietnam-1965`, `operation-all-the-way-1965`, `pleiku-campaign-1965-11-04`.

## 1966 — ⬜ gap (5 D Co KIA)
- **Operation Masher / White Wing** (≈24 Jan – 6 Mar 1966) — Bình Định Province clearing.
- **Operation Crazy Horse** (May 1966) — Vĩnh Thạnh / Bình Định.
- **Operation Thayer I & II / Irving** (autumn 1966 into early 1967) — Bình Định.
  *(The synopsis names Masher, Crazy Horse, and Thayer explicitly; Irving/Paul Revere etc. are
  the division's other 1966 Bình Định operations — confirm which involved 2/8 Cav.)*
- **Next:** cluster the five 1966 KIA dates from the KIA list against these operations.

## 1967 — ✅ covered (Tier 2 frames built; Tier 1 clusters pending) (18 organic D Co KIA — highest early-year loss)
- **Operation Pershing** (12 Feb 1967 – 19 Jan 1968) — large-scale search-and-destroy of PAVN/VC base
  areas in Bình Định, II Corps (~5,400 enemy killed, 2,000 captured; 852 US KIA). The division's
  dominant 1967 operation; almost the entire year. **BUILT (S82):** `operation-pershing-1967` —
  year umbrella; 2/8 Cav role confirmed via firebase-by-year data (companies at ~26 Binh Dinh LZs).
- **Battle of Tam Quan** (6–20 Dec 1967) — 1st Brigade vs PAVN 22d Regiment near Tam Quan/Dai Dong;
  **BUILT (S82):** `battle-of-tam-quan-1967` (sub-op of Pershing), frames the 11 Dec cluster
  (Follett, Paulson; carried in its KIA block, profiles pending).
- **Next:** build the remaining Tier 1 cluster event pages + soldier profiles (Mar–Sep 1967) and wire
  each up to Pershing.

## 1968 — 🟡 partial (16 organic D Co KIA)
- **Operation Jeb Stuart** (Jan 1968, I Corps) — the division had moved north.
- **Operation Pegasus** (1–14 Apr 1968) — the relief of the Khe Sanh Marine base.
- **Operation Delaware / Lam Son 216** (19 Apr – mid-May 1968) — A Shau Valley.
- **Autumn 1968: the move south to III Corps** (Phước Vĩnh Base Camp, NE of Saigon) — a major AO
  shift; the start of the War Zone C/D period. This is the **Operation Sheridan Sabre** era.
- Pages: `operation-sheridan-sabre-1968-12-04` (the 4 Dec 1968 cluster). 
- **Next:** the II→I→III Corps moves are a strong candidate for a Tier 2 context page; tie the
  1968 clusters to Pegasus/Delaware (I Corps) vs the late-year III Corps move.

## 1969 — ✅ covered (21 organic D Co KIA — best-documented year)
- **Operation Toan Thang III** (III Corps offensive umbrella, 17 Feb – 31 Oct 1969) with
  sub-operations **Montana Raider, Montana Scout, Comanche Warrior, Creek II, Kentucky Cougar**.
- Pages: `operation-toan-thang-iii-1969` + the five sub-op pages; `trail-ambush-hau-nghia-1969-05-25`;
  `river-crossing-song-be-1969-10-08`. Primary: division ORLL AD0506273 (qtr ending 31 Jul 1969).

## 1970 — ✅ covered (10 organic D Co KIA)
- **Cambodian Incursion** (1 May – 30 Jun 1970; officially Toan Thang 43/45/46) — the division's
  defining 1970 operation and the first large-scale U.S. airmobile assault outside South Vietnam.
  Task Force Shoemaker (3rd Bde, 1st Cav + 11th ACR + 3rd ARVN Abn) assaulted the Fishhook; the
  campaign overran "The City" and "Rock Island East" and a chain of caches. **2/8 Cav role
  confirmed:** CMH airmobility ch. XI names **D Company, 2/8 Cav** (communications-supply depot
  cache, 25 May–9 Jun) and **A Company** (automotive parts, 25 May); firebase data places 2/8 Cav
  at FBs Gonder and Picatinny East inside Cambodia. 2/8 Cav was a **3rd Brigade** battalion.
- Pages: `operation-cambodian-incursion-1970` (Tier 2 UMBRELLA, NEW).
- **Next:** cluster the ten 1970 KIA dates into Tier 1 pages and wire them to the incursion frame
  (RG 472 to pin which fell inside vs. outside the Cambodia window).

## 1971–72 — ✅ covered (1971: 7 KIA · 1972: 21 KIA)
- Division colors redeployed to Fort Hood, 1971. The **3rd Brigade, 1st Cavalry Division
  (Separate) — "Garryowen Task Force"** (activated 30 Apr 1971) continued in **War Zone D** until
  June 1972; 2/8 Cav inactivated 28 June 1972.
- Pages: `3rd-brigade-separate-garryowen-1971`; the FSB Fontaine cluster; `contact-nui-ba-1971-10-21`;
  `chinook-crash-1972-05-10`; `departure-vietnam-1972`.

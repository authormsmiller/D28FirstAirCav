## June 28, 1968 — 1st Platoon Contact

> **Research stub.** Built from a single secondary source (a veteran-transcribed
> company daily journal). No US casualties are recorded from this action, and
> no grid coordinate is given.

At **1600 hours on June 28, 1968**, **1st Platoon, D Company, 2nd Battalion,
8th Cavalry** engaged four North Vietnamese soldiers with small-arms fire.
One NVA was killed — an AK-47 (serial number 947752) and documents were
recovered from him — and an estimated two more were wounded. No US casualties
were reported, and no grid coordinate is given for the action, unusual for
this source.

The transcriber, a D Company veteran writing in the first person, identifies
this as his own first enemy contact and names the 1st squad's machine gunner
only by the nickname "**White Skull**."

This action is recorded here for completeness of the D Company record for
June-July 1968.

## June 4, 1968 — YD324398 (Friendly Fire)

> **Research stub.** Built from a single secondary source (a veteran-transcribed
> company daily journal). No US fatality is recorded from this incident.

At **1210 hours on June 4, 1968**, an enlisted man of **D Company, 2nd
Battalion, 8th Cavalry** was wounded by shrapnel from friendly (outgoing US)
artillery fire at grid **YD324398**. Medevac ran at 1250 hours.

This incident preceded, by about an hour and a half and roughly 500 meters,
the same company's bunker-complex contact with NVA forces at grid YD324393 at
1345 hours that afternoon (see
**[contact-yd324393-1968-06-04](/events/contact-yd324393-1968-06-04/)**). The
two are recorded separately because the journal gives them as distinct
entries with different causes.

## 1972 — The Last Team out

> **Context page — division level.** The close of D Company, 2/8 Cav's war: how the unit came
> home. Drawn from the official Army record (the 2/8 Cav Lineage and Honors and the 3rd Brigade
> Operational Reports). The exact DA General Order numbers are flagged to pull.

By 1971 the American ground war was winding down, and the **1st Cavalry Division colors redeployed
to Fort Hood, Texas**, where the division reorganized as a "Triple Capability" (TRICAP) division.
But the war was not over for the men who carried its lineage.

### The division goes home — the brigade stays

To continue operations in country, the **3rd Brigade, 1st Cavalry Division (Separate)** — the
**"Garryowen Task Force"** — was **organized and activated on 30 April 1971**. It became one of
the **last two major U.S. Army ground combat units in Vietnam**, operating in **War Zone D** out
of the Bien Hoa area. The brigade **stood down on 20 June 1972** in a ceremony attended by the
COMUSMACV commander, **General Creighton Abrams**, and its colors departed for Fort Hood, arriving
**29 June 1972**; **1st Battalion, 7th Cavalry (Task Force Garry Owen)** stayed on into the summer.

### 2/8 Cav — in the field to the end

**2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** was one of the 3rd Brigade (Separate)'s maneuver battalions. It
**left Vietnam in 1971 and was inactivated on 28 June 1972** — present to the very end of the
brigade's tour. This is why D Company kept taking losses after the division proper had gone home:
the **FSB Fontaine actions (April 1971)**, the **Núi Bà contact (October 1971)**, and the
**Chinook crash of 10 May 1972** — D Company's single costliest day — all fall in this final
period, the Chinook loss coming just **weeks before the brigade stood down**.

### First in, last out

D Company, 2/8 Cav brackets the American ground war about as cleanly as any unit can: **in country
with the first full division in September 1965** (see **Deployment — The First Team Arrives**) and
**still in the field with the last brigade out in 1972**, the battalion inactivating on 28 June
1972. The same battalion designation opened and closed the division's war.

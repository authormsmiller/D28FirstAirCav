## March 6, 1970 — Tay Ninh Province

**CPL Francis "Frank" Ware III**, Range Platoon, D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry, was killed in an ambush in **Tay Ninh Province** on **March 6, 1970**. He was 20 years old. Official records record the cause as hostile, ground casualty, multiple fragmentation wounds.

Ware had been in-country since August 3, 1969 — 215 days at the time of his death. He served in Range Platoon alongside his close friend **Sonny Silva**, who later described the circumstances and wrote to Ware's family: *"I knew him well. He was a great person. He did not suffer."*

The precise location of the contact has not been established. The battalion staged from **FB Mary Gwen** (XT526796) in late February 1970 and was operating west through the Tay Ninh AO in early March. The 1st Cavalry Division ORLL (AD0512505) does not carry a D Company entry for March 6; the significant contacts log records A Company on March 4 and C Company on March 7–8, both in the XT17x grid west of Mary Gwen.

The named-casualty record and precise grid await the 2-8 Cav daily staff journal (NARA RG 472, 6 March 1970).

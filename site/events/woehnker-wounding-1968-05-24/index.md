## May 24, 1968 — 1LT Harrison Woehnker Jr, wounded

> **Research stub.** This page does not describe a confirmed action — it
> records an open question about which unit 1LT Harrison Woehnker Jr was
> actually with when he was wounded. His own casualty-record sources
> disagree on this point.

**1LT Harrison E Woehnker Jr** was wounded on **May 24, 1968**, in **Quang Tri
Province**, by gun or small arms fire. He died of his wounds **55 days later,
on July 18, 1968**, at age 25. He is inscribed on the Vietnam Veterans
Memorial, **Panel 51W, Line 9**. See his full profile at
**[Harrison Woehnker](/soldiers/woehnker-harrison/)**.

### A conflict in the sources

Honor States lists Woehnker's Unit/Group as **"1st Cav Division, 2nd
Battalion, 8th Cavalry, D Company"** — the same D Co, 2/8 Cav designation used
for the well-documented May 1972 Chinook crash batch. But Virtual Wall gives
his actual unit as **C Battery, 2nd Battalion, 19th Artillery**, which matches
his rank (1LT) and MOS (1193, Field Artillery Unit Commander) — an artillery
officer's billet, not an infantryman's.

The working theory is that Woehnker was serving as a **forward observer or
liaison officer attached to D Company** at the moment he was wounded. This is
a plausible, well-precedented arrangement — artillery officers routinely rode
with the infantry companies their batteries supported — and the timing fits:
DTIC record AD393815 (1st Cav Division Artillery's operational report for
this quarter) confirms that 2/19 Artillery was in direct support of **1st
Brigade**, which included 2/8 Cav, for the entire period surrounding this
wounding. But no source reviewed so far names him with D Company directly,
and no primary unit journal for 24 May 1968 has been located.

### What corroborates the location

Three other D Company, 2/8 Cav losses already documented in this archive help
place the company in the same area during this stretch:

- **[SGT Robert Ross](/soldiers/ross-robert/)** was killed July 1, 1968, in
  Quang Tri Province, at grid YD293319 — inside Woehnker's own
  wounding-to-death window, in the same province.
- **[PFC Roger Fast](/soldiers/fast-roger/)** and **[SP4 Ronald
  Golden](/soldiers/golden-ronald/)** were killed August 19–20, 1968, near LZ
  Carol in Thua Thien Province — a month later and a different province,
  showing the company had moved on by then.

Separately, the **Skipper Journal** — a veteran's day-by-day transcription of
the D/2-8 Cav company log — places the company at **LZ Anne** on June 3,
1968. LZ Anne (grid YD298394) is independently listed in AD393815 as **C
Battery, 2/19 Artillery's** position that same quarter — Woehnker's own
battery. It's the closest geographic link found so far between his parent
unit and the company Honor States credits him to, though it doesn't cover May
24 itself.

### Next source needed

A **NARA RG 472 daily staff journal** — for 2/8 Cav or 2/19 Arty, 24 May 1968,
Quang Tri Province — would supply a grid and could name-confirm which unit
Woehnker was actually with. Until then, this remains an open question, not a
finding.

## June 5, 1968 — YD325393

> **Research stub.** Built from a single secondary source (a veteran-transcribed
> company daily journal). The casualty tie to SGT Jacob Weldin below is a
> candidate identification, not a confirmed one.

**D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** received small-arms fire at **1615
hours on June 5, 1968**, at grid **YD325393**. A man was wounded at 1745 and died
of his wounds at 1755. His body was recovered at 1945, and the company pulled
back. The journal gives no enemy unit identification for this contact beyond the
small-arms fire itself.

### The candidate match

**SGT Jacob Robinson Weldin's** roster date of death is June 6, 1968 — one day
after this action. That one-day offset is the pattern seen elsewhere in this
source: the company journal was kept in the rear, off delayed field reporting, so
a casualty's administratively recorded date can trail the actual date of action by
a day or more. This is also the only single-casualty D Company action in the
transcribed journal close to Weldin's date of death, which is what makes it a
plausible, though unconfirmed, match. Virtual Wall independently records his cause
of death as hostile fire, gun or small arms, in Quang Tri Province — consistent
with this action's mechanism and setting, though it does not by itself resolve the
one-day date offset. See **[Jacob Weldin](/soldiers/weldin-jacob/)** for the full
profile.

### An open reconciliation question

The review built from this journal flags both this event and the June 4
bunker-complex contact as open questions: three roster deaths that week (Weldin,
Winner, and — at a wider 7-day offset — Wheeler) sit close enough in time to both
single-KIA events that the correct pairing cannot be determined from date alone.
This page and [June 4](/events/contact-yd324393-1968-06-04/) record the
circumstantial reasoning for each candidate pairing without forcing a resolution.

### Next source needed

The **2/8 Cav daily staff journal** (NARA RG 472, 5-6 June 1968) would confirm the
casualty by name.

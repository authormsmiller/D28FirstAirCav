## Operation Creek II — War Zone D, 19 May – 22 June 1969

> **Context page — division level** (DTIC AD0506273). A **3rd Brigade** operation; **2/8 Cav
> was not in it.** Included to bound the quarter's operations.

Creek II overlapped Comanche Warrior in time and place and was conducted by the same **3rd
Brigade** against the **5th VC Division** in War Zone D; the ORLL discusses the two together
(see the Comanche Warrior page for the shared mission, narrative, and combined results — 132
enemy killed across both operations). Communications support records place 3rd Brigade activity
in the **Blackhorse–Long Giao** area with a relay on **Nui Chua Chan**, in the Long Khanh
sector.

**Relevance to the cluster:** none directly. Documented so the quarter's operational picture is
complete and so no D Co casualty is mistakenly tied to a War Zone D operation 2/8 Cav did not
take part in.

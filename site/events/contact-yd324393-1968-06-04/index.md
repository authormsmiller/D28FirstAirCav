## June 4, 1968 — Bunker Complex, YD324393

> **Research stub.** Built from a single secondary source (a veteran-transcribed
> company daily journal). The casualty tie to PFC Brian Winner below is a
> candidate identification, not a confirmed one — the journal never names
> individuals.

At **1345 hours on June 4, 1968**, **D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry**
engaged North Vietnamese Army forces in a bunker complex at grid **YD324393**,
using automatic-weapons fire; supporting scout helicopters also took fire. Brigade
intelligence had assessed a **10th Sapper Battalion** command post essentially at
the same location, YD323392. The company also took incoming 60mm mortar fire — 13
rounds — during the action. One NVA KIA (a rice carrier) was noted at 1000 hours,
separate from the contact itself.

The company suffered one US KIA, whose body was not recovered that day — he was
carried missing in action — and one US WIA from fragmentation.

### Recovery, three days later

On **June 7**, the journal records that a US KIA was found at grid YD325391
(1105 hours) and identified by the platoon leader as the man missing since June 4;
his remains were partially decomposed. Five additional NVA KIA from the
intervening June 5-6 fighting, and a captured SKS rifle, were found in the same
area.

### The candidate match

**PFC Brian Carl Winner's** roster date of death is June 7, 1968 — matching this
recovery/identification date rather than the June 4 date of the actual contact.
That offset is the pattern this source repeats throughout: the journal was kept in
the rear, and personnel status could lag well behind the tactical action itself.
Virtual Wall independently records his cause of death as hostile fire, died
outright, in Quang Tri Province — consistent with being killed in the contact
itself rather than dying later of wounds, which fits the "carried MIA, recovered
days later" account better than a delayed death would. He had arrived in-country
May 13, 1968, about three weeks before this action. See
**[Brian Winner](/soldiers/winner-brian/)** for the full profile.

### Next source needed

The **2/8 Cav daily staff journal** (NARA RG 472, 4-7 June 1968) would confirm the
casualty by name and resolve the exact circumstances of the recovery.

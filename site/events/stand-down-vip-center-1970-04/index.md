## A three-day stand-down at the Bien Hoa VIP Center

In the spring of 1970, between operations in War Zone C, **D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** was rotated out of the field for a **three-day stand-down** at the **1st Cavalry Division's VIP Center** in Bien Hoa. Two photographs in *CAVALAIR* (8 April 1970) record it: D Company and E Company men stepping off a C-130 at Bien Hoa airport, and D Company men in the VIP Center pool.

Line companies cycled through the center periodically for rest and relaxation -- a swimming pool, miniature golf, ball courts, movies, traveling floor shows, and clubs -- a sharp contrast to the bamboo and bunkers of the Dog's Head a few days earlier.

### From the clipping (verbatim captions)

> "That C-130 may not be the 'Freedom Bird' but it's the next best thing. 1st Air Cavalrymen from **Delta and Echo Companies 2nd Bn, 8th Cav** disembark at Bien Hoa airport for a three-day stand down at the Cav's VIP Center."

> "Plenty of room for everyone in the 1st Cav's VIP Center pool and the water's always warm. Members of Alpha Company, 1st Bn, 12th Cav and **Delta Company, 2nd Bn, 8th Cav** get in the swim of things during the three day stand down."

*Both captions, CAVALAIR (1st Air Cavalry Division weekly), Vol. 4 No. 14, 8 April 1970.*

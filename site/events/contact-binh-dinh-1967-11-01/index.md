## November 1, 1967 — D Company loses its platoon leader

> **Research page — sparse.** Confirmed: 2LT Michael E. Bennett, D Company, 2nd Battalion,
> 8th Cavalry, was killed by small arms fire in Binh Dinh Province on 1 November 1967. The specific
> patrol, grid position, and contact circumstances are not yet established. The 2/8 Cav daily staff
> journal (NARA RG 472) is the primary source needed to locate this action.

On **1 November 1967**, **2LT Michael E. Bennett** — platoon leader, D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th
Cavalry — was killed by **small arms fire** somewhere in **Binh Dinh Province**. He was 21 years
old and had been in-country for 63 days. His name is on the Wall at **Panel 28E, Line 107**.

### Where the battalion was

The 2/8 Cav had returned to **LZ English** at Bong Son on **14 October 1967** following a
three-week attachment to the 173rd Airborne Brigade during **Operation Bolling** in the Song Re
Valley. Back in the Pershing AO, the battalion was conducting combat operations on the **Bong Son
Plain** when Bennett was killed — small arms contacts in the paddies, tree lines, and village
complexes of Binh Dinh were a constant feature of Operation Pershing.

Two days later, around **3 November**, the battalion would deploy to **Dak To** in Kontum Province
for the November battles there before returning to Binh Dinh for the **Battle of Tam Quan** in
December. November 1 is the last confirmed Pershing-AO date before that move; Bennett died in
Binh Dinh, not Kontum, placing him at the tail end of the Bong Son phase.

### 2LT Michael Bennett

Michael E. Bennett was from **Brentwood, New Hampshire** — a Sanborn Seminary graduate, University
of New Hampshire student, commissioned via Officer Candidate School in **February 1967**. He arrived
in Vietnam on **30 August 1967** and was assigned to D Company as a platoon leader. A local
newspaper reported him as the **first recorded KIA from Brentwood** and the **73rd from New
Hampshire** at the time of his death. He is buried at Calvary Cemetery, Portsmouth, New Hampshire.
See his [profile](/soldiers/bennett-michael/).

## June 12, 1968 — Quang Tri Province (circumstances unresolved)

> **Research stub — help needed.** PFC John Wheeler's death is confirmed by
> name, date, and province. The specific incident that killed him is not yet
> known. This page exists to flag that gap, not to propose an incident.

**PFC John Clark Wheeler**, D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry, was killed in
action on **June 12, 1968**, in Quang Tri Province. Virtual Wall records the cause
as hostile fire, died outright, an **explosive device** — a mine, booby trap, or
similar, rather than small-arms fire.

### Why this isn't tied to a journal action

The only other D Company narrative source reviewed for this period, the
veteran-transcribed Skipper Journal (3 June-3 July 1968), has an untranscribed gap
from **June 10 to June 18** — the company was on base defense at LZ Betty and then
LZ Pedro during that stretch, but the journal itself gives no detail for those
days. Wheeler's date of death falls squarely inside that gap.

The nearest documented single-casualty action, [June 5 at grid
YD325393](/events/contact-1968-06-05/), is seven days removed and — now that
Wheeler's cause of death is confirmed as an explosive device rather than
small-arms fire — a different mechanism entirely. That rules the June 5 action
out as a match; it does not merely leave it uncertain.

An explosive device is at least consistent with a base-defense setting — a mine or
booby trap on a perimeter, for instance — but no source yet confirms the specific
incident, location, or circumstances. See **[John Wheeler](/soldiers/wheeler-john/)**
for the full profile.

### Next source needed

The **2/8 Cav daily staff journal** (NARA RG 472, 10-18 June 1968) is the specific
gap every other source reviewed so far leaves open, and the logical next document
to pull. If you have any information about this loss, please use the site's
contribute form.

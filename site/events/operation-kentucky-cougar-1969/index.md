## Operation Kentucky Cougar — initiated 24 June 1969

> **Context page — division level** (DTIC AD0506273). A **1st Brigade** operation that 2/8 Cav
> took part in, but it begins **after** the May cluster and was still in progress when this
> report closed (31 July 1969).

Kentucky Cougar was **initiated 24 June 1969** as the division shifted its weight north. The
**1st Brigade continued operations through July with the 2/5, 2/7, and 2/8 Cav** to interdict
enemy movement through the brigade AO and to destroy major enemy forces, supplies, and
fortifications, with the division concentrating its efforts in Tay Ninh, Binh Long, Binh Duong,
and **Phuoc Long** as the period ended.

**Relevance to the cluster:** it post-dates the May 1969 deaths, so it does not frame them. It
is included to complete this quarter's operations and because it is the likely operational
context for **later** 1969 D Co losses (e.g., the August 1969 entries on the KIA list). Its
conclusion falls in the next quarterly ORLL.

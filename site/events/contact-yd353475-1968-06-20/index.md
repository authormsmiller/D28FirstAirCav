## June 20, 1968 — YD353475 (Booby Trap) and YD347482 (Detention)

> **Research stub.** Built from a single secondary source (a veteran-transcribed
> company daily journal). No US fatality is recorded from this incident.

At **1015 hours on June 20, 1968**, an enlisted man of **D Company, 2nd
Battalion, 8th Cavalry** was wounded by a booby-trapped Chicom grenade rigged
with a trip wire, at grid **YD353475**. Medevac ran at 1035 hours; the booby
trap itself was destroyed.

Later the same day, at **1510 hours**, the company detained one military-age
male at grid **YD347482**, who was turned over to 1st Brigade for
interrogation. This was the month's only enemy prisoner, per the journal's own
end-of-period tally ("captured one POW").

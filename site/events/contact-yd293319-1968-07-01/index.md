## July 1, 1968 — YD293319

> **Research stub.** Built from a single secondary source (a veteran-transcribed
> company daily journal). The casualty tie to SGT Robert Ross below is the
> strongest candidate identification of the four reviewed from this document,
> though still not name-confirmed.

**D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** made contact with an estimated 2-3 North
Vietnamese soldiers at **0930 hours on July 1, 1968**, at grid **YD293319**. A
soldier was wounded in the contact and died of his wounds by 1230. The action
produced one NVA KIA and a captured AK-47 rifle (serial number 51747).

Later the same day, at 1315 hours, a separate NVA KIA was found buried in a bunker
at grid YD296319, roughly 300 meters away — the journal attributes this to a First
Platoon ambush four days earlier, on June 28.

### The candidate match

**SGT Robert James Ross's** roster date of death is July 1, 1968 — an exact match
to this action, with no casualty-count discrepancy to reconcile. Of the four D
Company losses reviewed against this journal, this is the cleanest: same date,
same company, one casualty. Virtual Wall independently records his cause of death
as hostile fire, gun or small arms, in Quang Tri Province — matching this action's
mechanism exactly (wounded 0930, died of wounds 1230). It remains a candidate
rather than a confirmed identification only because the journal never names
individuals, but this is now the best-corroborated of the four candidate matches
from this document.

Robert Ross was born July 1, 1947, and had arrived in-country October 27, 1967 —
over eight months before this action. If this identification is correct, he was
killed in action on his 21st birthday. See **[Robert Ross](/soldiers/ross-robert/)**
for the full profile.

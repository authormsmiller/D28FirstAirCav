## February 28, 1970 — Firebase Mary Gwen, Tay Ninh Province

> **Research stub.** The casualty record is confirmed; the operational circumstances are not.
> This page holds what is known and marks the gap explicitly.

**CPL Kenneth Michael Flashner**, a rifleman with D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry, was killed in action on **February 28, 1970**, in **Tay Ninh Province**. The Army records his death as hostile, died outright — multiple fragmentation wounds, ground casualty. He was 23 years old and had been in Vietnam two months and twenty-one days. He is inscribed on the Vietnam Veterans Memorial, **Panel 13W, Line 68**.

### What is known

The date, province, unit, and cause of death are confirmed across all three primary sources. **Firebase Mary Gwen** (XT526796) was established by the 2/8th Cavalry on **February 24, 1970** — four days before Flashner was killed. He was almost certainly at that position when he died; fragmentation wounds on a ground casualty are consistent with an attack on a newly opened firebase.

After his death, the battalion named a firebase in his honor. The II Field Force Vietnam ORLL (AD0511158) records **FSB Flashner at XT174982** — a position further west, near the Cambodian border, established in the weeks that followed. On **March 8**, the 2/8 Cav made contact northwest of FSB Flashner, losing 3 killed and 15 wounded against an NVA force. The naming of a firebase after a soldier was not automatic — it marked someone whose death the battalion remembered.

### What is not known

Neither the 1st Cavalry Division ORLL (AD0512505) nor the II Field Force Vietnam ORLL (AD0511158) carries a contact entry for the 2/8 Cav on February 28, 1970. Single-KIA contacts were frequently not logged at division level. Flashner's specific position, the type of contact, and whether other soldiers were wounded are unknown.

### Next source needed

The **2/8 Cav daily staff journal** (NARA RG 472, 28 February 1970) is the primary outstanding source. It should establish the grid, the contact type, and the circumstances of D Company's position that day.

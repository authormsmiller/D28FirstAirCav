## Operation Masher / White Wing — the Bong Son campaign

> **Context page — operation level.** Built from the 1st Cavalry Division's own after-action
> reports for Masher/White Wing and the Bong Son lessons-learned study (filed with this event). It
> frames the large operation that **2/8 Cavalry — Malec's battalion — fought through in February
> and early March 1966**, immediately before Operation Jim Bowie. Battalion- and brigade-level
> movements are well documented; D Company's hour-by-hour positions are drawn from the firebase
> data and otherwise await the battalion journal.

### What it was

Masher/White Wing (25 January – 6 March 1966) was the 1st Cavalry Division's first large
search-and-destroy campaign on the **Bong Son plains** of northern **Binh Dinh Province** and the
valleys behind them — the Kim Son ("Crow's Foot") and Suoi Ca. It was renamed from "Masher" to the
gentler "White Wing" in early February. The division committed all three brigades in rotation,
with the **22nd ARVN Division** sealing the coast and Highway 1. The fighting peaked in the 2nd
Brigade's **"Iron Triangle"** engagement (17–21 Feb), broken by a B-52 strike.

### Where 2/8 Cavalry — and D Company — fit

Malec's battalion fought in the **1st Brigade** through the valley phases:

- **18–23 Feb** — 2/8 Cav, alongside 1/8 Cav, conducted single- and multi-company search-and-destroy
  sweeps north and southeast of **LZ Bird** and through the **upper Kim Son and Suoi Ca valleys**,
  with "numerous clashes with small enemy groups."
- **24–25 Feb** — 2/8 Cav air-assaulted into the **Noi Go Chai hill mass** to reinforce 1/12 Cav,
  then held blocking positions there.
- **27 Feb** — the 1st and 2nd Brigades (1/8, 2/8, 2/7) extended the search of the **Suoi Ca Da
  Valley**.
- **1–6 March (White Wing / Black Horse phase)** — 2/8 air-assaulted **LZ John** (1 Mar, killing
  two), moved into the **"Bulls Eye"** area, ran company-size patrols, lifted to **LZ Pat** (4 Mar),
  and was pulled back by CH-47 on 5–6 March. The firebase data places **D Company's air assault into
  LZ Rene (BR938958) on 4 March**.

On **3 March**, 2/8 Cav sweeps around Bulls Eye **located a hut "used to manufacture booby traps."**
That detail matters for Malec's story: it is one more sign of how saturated the Kim Son area was
with traps and improvised explosives in the weeks before **Operation Jim Bowie**, the follow-on
operation in which he was wounded on 21 March 1966 — an operation whose casualties were, by the
official record, overwhelmingly from booby traps and punji stakes.

### What's still open

The AARs are written at battalion and brigade level, so D Company's specific daily positions — and
the **name of the 2/8 battalion commander** — are not in them; both await the 2/8 Cav daily staff
journal (NARA RG 472).

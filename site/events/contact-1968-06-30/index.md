## June 30, 1968 — YD288318

> **Research stub.** Built from a single secondary source (a veteran-transcribed
> company daily journal). No US fatality is recorded from this action.

At **1655 hours on June 30, 1968**, **D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry**
received small-arms fire and grenades from an unspecified number of North
Vietnamese soldiers at grid **YD288318**. One American soldier was wounded, and
the company's scout dog was killed. Medevac ran from 1700 to 1852. The journal
gives no enemy casualty count or unit identification for this contact.

This action is recorded here for completeness of the D Company record for
June-July 1968 — it is not tied to any roster KIA. It is part of the journal's own
month-end tally: three US KIA, four WIA, one scout dog KIA, ten enemy KIA, and one
prisoner of war.

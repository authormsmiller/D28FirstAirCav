## Bob Hope's 1970 Christmas show, Long Binh

On **25 December 1970**, **Jim Garvin** was one of four men from his platoon sent to the **Bob Hope USO Christmas show** at **Long Binh** -- the rest of Range Platoon, and most of D Company, spent Christmas in the field. Garvin had earned the trip in a roundabout way: his lieutenant felt he'd been wrongly punished over a booby-trap dispute at FSB Silver and arranged the detail with the CO.

The crowd filled a horseshoe stadium with the stage at the open end -- Garvin's first real sense of the scale of the American presence in Vietnam. Guests included **Johnny Bench**, **Gloria Loring**, **Jennifer Hosten** (the reigning Miss World), the **Gold Diggers**, and the **Ding-a-Lings**. A chopper formation trailing red and green Christmas smoke closed the show.

## Operation Toan Thang III — the campaign frame

> **Context page — division level.** Drawn from the 1st Cavalry Division's Operational Report —
> Lessons Learned for the quarter ending 31 July 1969 (DTIC AD0506273). It establishes what the
> division and the 1st Brigade (2/8 Cav's brigade) were doing, **not** what D Company did on any
> given day. Treat the connection to specific casualties as **highly likely context, not
> established fact**, pending the battalion journal.

**Operation Toan Thang III** ("Certain Victory") was the III Corps offensive umbrella under
which the 1st Cavalry Division operated through 1969. It began before this reporting period and
continued past it; the division's stated purpose was to interdict PAVN/VC infiltration routes
and destroy enemy base areas and forces in War Zones C and D.

Over the quarter the division's weight shifted across III Corps: during the first half of May
1969 it operated in **Tay Ninh, Binh Long, and Binh Duong** provinces; in mid-May it added a
shift south into **Long Khanh and Bien Hoa**; in early June forces moved from Bien Hoa to **Binh
Tuy**; and on 24 June elements shifted north into **Phuoc Long**. As the period closed the
division was concentrating in Tay Ninh, Binh Long, Binh Duong, and Phuoc Long.

The campaign was prosecuted through several named operations (really designated areas of
operation in which daily operations continued), run concurrently by the three brigades and
often reinforced by the 11th Armored Cavalry Regiment:

- **Montana Raider** (War Zone C, 13 Apr – 13 May) — three-brigade effort.
- **Montana Scout** (southern War Zone C, 1 Apr – 23 Jun) — 1st and 2nd Brigades.
- **Comanche Warrior** (War Zone D, 14 May – 1 Jun) and **Creek II** (19 May – 22 Jun) — 3rd
  Brigade.
- **Kentucky Cougar** (initiated 24 Jun) — the period's final operation, shifting north.

**Where 2/8 Cav fit:** the parent battalion was organic to the **1st Brigade**, which operated
in War Zone C / Tay Ninh — under Montana Raider (to 13 May), then Montana Scout, then Kentucky
Cougar. The two War Zone D operations (Comanche Warrior, Creek II) were **3rd Brigade** and did
not involve 2/8 Cav.

**Cumulative results, 1 May – 31 July 1969 (division):** 2,696 enemy killed, 103 prisoners of
war; U.S.: 247 killed, 1,781 wounded (732 requiring evacuation). The report assesses that the
period severely disrupted the 1st NVA, 7th NVA, and 5th VC Divisions in War Zones C and D.

## Summary

In the spring of 1971 the **2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** added a **"Vietnam-style '500'"** to its
recreation program at **FSB Fontaine**. Skytroopers raced **bicycles one lap around the inside of
the firebase berm**, the field broken into **five classes by rank** (E-4 and below, and on up) so
the competition stayed even.

The full item is transcribed from the clipping as the document **[News in Brief: The "500" — FSB Fontaine](/documents/unit/cavalair-500-fontaine/)**.

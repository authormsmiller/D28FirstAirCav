## January 28, 1967 — D Company loses two men

> **Research page — in progress.** Confirmed: two men of D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry were
> killed on 28 January 1967 in Binh Dinh Province during Operation Thayer II. The specific action —
> the landing zone, the nature of the contact, and the enemy unit — is not yet confirmed against a
> unit after-action report.

On **28 January 1967**, **D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** lost two men:

- **CPL Peter Joseph Keller Jr.**, 21, of Detroit, Michigan — Wall Panel 14E, Line 91
- **CPL Donald Francis Yates**, 20, of Round Lake, New York — Wall Panel 14E, Line 94

Both died of **multiple fragmentation wounds**, their names sit three lines apart on Panel 14E, and
they had arrived in-country within two days of each other in July 1966 — together the signature of a
single shared action. Keller died three days before his 22nd birthday.

### Operation Thayer II

By late January 1967 the 1st Cavalry Division was in the final weeks of **Operation Thayer II**
(24 October 1966 – 12 February 1967), the division's pacification campaign in **Binh Dinh Province**
on the central coast, soon to be followed by Operation Pershing. 2/8 Cavalry was a **1st Brigade**
battalion, commanded by **LTC Thomas H. Tackaberry**, and the division's report for the quarter
confirms the 1st Brigade held the **Central and Southern Kim Son Valley** from 3 January 1967 — the
setting for the 28 January contact. The battalion's firebase records narrow it further: in January
1967 D Company held **LZ Minh (BR707760)** in the central Kim Son Valley, with B Company at LZ Ho and
C Company at LZ Chi nearby. As D Company men, Keller and Yates were most likely at or operating from
**LZ Minh** — the leading candidate for the site of their deaths, pending day-level confirmation.

The specific action that killed the two men has not yet been matched to a unit after-action report.
The 1st Cav Division's report for the quarter does not separately narrate it — the 28 January 2/8
contact falls below the division's significant-contact threshold. (The division's named 27 January
fight was a different action: 2/12 Cavalry against the 22d NVA Regiment northeast of Bong Son, not
this Kim Son cluster.) The day-level record — the landing zone, the nature of the contact, and the
enemy unit — awaits the 2/8 Cav daily staff journal (NARA RG 472).

## June 26, 1968 — YD288321

> **Research stub.** Built from a single secondary source (a veteran-transcribed
> company daily journal). No casualties, either side, are recorded from this
> action.

At **0900 hours on June 26, 1968**, **D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry**
took small-arms fire from a single North Vietnamese soldier while moving
south at grid **YD288321**. The company assessed an estimated six to seven
NVA on the same trail. No US casualties were reported, and the journal gives
no enemy casualty count or unit identification.

This action is recorded here for completeness of the D Company record for
June-July 1968.

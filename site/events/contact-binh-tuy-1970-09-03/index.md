## September 3, 1970 — D Company loses a man from Range Platoon

> **Research page — sparse.** Confirmed: CPL Craig Thomas Waterman, Range Platoon,
> D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry, was killed in Binh Tuy Province on 3 September
> 1970. The official record classifies his death as a hostile ground casualty, misadventure.
> The precise location and full circumstances are not yet established.

On **3 September 1970**, **CPL Craig Thomas Waterman** — Range Platoon, D Company, 2nd
Battalion, 8th Cavalry — was killed in **Binh Tuy Province**. He was 20 years old and
had been in-country for 99 days. His name is on the Wall at **Panel 7W, Line 37**.

### Where the battalion was

The 2/8 Cav had been operating from **FSB Powder Ridge** (grid YT807786) through **31
August 1970**, and would establish **FSB Guin** (grid ZT100190), roughly 67 kilometres to
the southeast, on **4 September**. Waterman was killed on the day between — at a temporary
patrol base or staging position during the transition. The two firebases are too far apart
for overland movement; the unit made this kind of shift by helicopter.

### CPL Craig Waterman

Craig Thomas Waterman was from **Rockwell City, Iowa** — a big, easy-going man his friends
called **Water Buffalo**. He began his tour on **27 May 1970** and served in **Range Platoon**
throughout his time in-country.

He is buried at **Saint Francis Cemetery, Rockwell City, Iowa**. In May 2021, his Range
Platoon comrade **Jim Garvin** gathered fellow veterans for a Celebration of Life at the
cemetery.

Jim Garvin has since described what happened that night. It was **pitch black**. Craig had
gotten outside the wire — how or why, no one knows. He **tripped a flare** near the position
of another squad in the same platoon. In the dark, with confirmed enemy activity in the area
that same afternoon, the squad fired. A high school friend who served as pallbearer had been
told Craig died "coming back from patrol into a remote base" — the story had compressed across
a few retellings before it reached Iowa.

Why Craig was outside the wire remains unanswered.

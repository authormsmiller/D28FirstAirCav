## September 3, 1970 — D Company makes contact

On **3 September 1970** at **1420 hours**, D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry engaged an
enemy soldier at a position approximately **2.4 kilometres west of where FSB Guin would stand
up the following morning**. The contact resulted in one NVA soldier killed and an AK-47 captured.
No D Company soldiers were wounded.

The battalion was in the midst of a move into a new area of operations. FSB Powder Ridge,
67 kilometres to the northwest, had closed on 31 August; FSB Guin (grid ZT100190, Binh Tuy
Province) would be established on **4 September**. In the four days between the two firebases,
the company was operating in open terrain in a contested area — no fixed perimeter, new ground,
active enemy presence confirmed.

B Troop, 1st Squadron, 9th Cavalry conducted two significant visual reconnaissance contacts
the same day, resulting in three additional NVA killed in the same general area.

### The following day

On **4 September**, the day FSB Guin opened, D/2-8 Cav made contact again — this time at
grid ZT075214, 100 metres south of the previous day's position — killing two more NVA and
capturing two AK-47s. At 0945 hours that morning, fifteen Hoi Chanh ralliers — one of them
armed with a Soviet SS rifle — surrendered to the 2-8th Cavalry at the newly-established Guin.
The pattern across both days establishes that the battalion was moving into an area of
confirmed, active enemy presence.

### Significance

This contact is documented here for its relationship to a loss on the same day. **CPL Craig
Thomas Waterman**, Range Platoon, D Company, was killed on **3 September 1970** in a friendly
fire incident — the official record classifies his death as a hostile ground casualty, misadventure.
A former comrade recalled being told that he was killed while returning from patrol into a remote
base. A unit that had been in hostile contact with enemy forces that afternoon, in unfamiliar
terrain with no established perimeter, would have every reason to treat any movement near its
position with extreme vigilance after dark. See [CPL Waterman's profile](/soldiers/waterman-craig/)
and the [September 3 KIA page](/events/contact-binh-tuy-1970-09-03/).

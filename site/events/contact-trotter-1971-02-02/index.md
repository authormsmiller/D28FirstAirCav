## 2 February 1971 -- a trail contact after the move from Silver

Days after D Company tore down **FSB Silver** and moved to **Fontaine**, **Jim Garvin** -- now gunner of **Range 3**, a squad rebuilt from the old CP team plus six new replacements -- set up on a **trotter** (a jungle trail). On **2 February 1971** three **VC** came down the trail. Garvin squeezed off a round and his **M-60 jammed on a ruptured shell**; for a moment he and his assistant gunner, **Vince Carbone**, were exposed behind cover. As Carbone remembered it (in a 2020 account), one VC looked straight at them with his AK-47 ready -- then ducked back behind a big banyan tree as other squad members opened up.

The enemy was driven off; the unit captured two weapons, including its first **AK-50** (a folding-stock AK), and a little rice. **Sgt Joe Smith** had a thumb shot off in the fight.

Jack Fink, elsewhere in the platoon, recorded the same contact that day: one VC killed, a round striking a soldier named "Dave" in his ammunition magazine without injury, and a follow-on assault on the mountain that found the VC already gone.

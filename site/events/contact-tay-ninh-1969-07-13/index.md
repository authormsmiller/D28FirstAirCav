## July 13, 1969 — D Company contact, Tay Ninh Province

D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry was on a search and clear mission in **Tay Ninh
Province** when the point element walked into an ambush — mines, machine-guns, and small
arms fire at close range. The initial contact wounded several men in the platoon.

**SGT Clyde Bonnelycke**, acting as Platoon Leader of the Second Platoon, moved forward
to his wounded machinegunner and picked up the weapon. He laid down an effective base of
fire that gave the platoon room to mount a counter-attack. When his ammunition ran out,
he seized grenades and silenced an enemy bunker with accurate throws. Five men were
evacuated. The ambush was broken.

Bonnelycke was awarded the Silver Star with Bronze Oak Leaf Cluster (2nd Award) for this
action. General Orders No. 10412, HQ 1st Cavalry Division (Airmobile), August 26, 1969.

## Operation Lincoln — back to the western highlands

> **Context page — operation level.** Built from the 1st Cavalry Division's Combat After Action
> Report for Operations Lincoln and Mosby I (filed with this event). It is included for **2/8
> Cavalry's operational arc** and is **not** part of any profiled soldier's service window — in
> particular, it falls after SGT Malec's 21 March 1966 wounding and in a different province.

### What it was

As the Bong Son operations (Masher/White Wing, then Jim Bowie) wound down in late March 1966, the
1st Cavalry Division shifted its main effort **west from Binh Dinh back to Pleiku Province** and the
**Oasis** base toward the Chu Pong massif and the Cambodian border, for **Operation Lincoln** (with
the smaller Mosby I).

### 2/8 Cavalry's part

2/8 Cav (1st Brigade) **redeployed from An Khe to the Pleiku / Oasis area**, forming a combat
convoy of **over 450 vehicles** — brigade headquarters, artillery, and logistics — that departed An
Khe at 0704 on March 25 and closed the **Oasis** (ZA115275) at 1521. From there the battalion
swept south and east toward Chu Pong: **LZ Typhus** (YA855198), **LZ Boil** (YA950255), and
**LZ Pox** (YA975291) on March 30; **LZ Dime** (YV916815) on March 31; **LZ Quarter** (YV876766)
through April 2–3; and **LZ Wool** (YV856962, the battalion CP) with **LZ Cotton** (YV843936) and
**LZ Cloth** (YV832942) for the final phase (April 4–7). **D Company** held the battalion CP at
LZ Wool through Phase III. The operation's one 2/8 Cav contact came on **April 7**: four VC engaged
at YV847938, one VCC captured. Lincoln terminated with the battalion airlifting to Pleiku (Lake Bien
Ho) before Mosby I.

This is recorded here to keep the battalion's 1966 arc continuous; **SGT Malec was not present** —
he had been wounded on 21 March in Binh Dinh and evacuated before Lincoln began.

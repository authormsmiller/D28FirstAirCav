## March 7, 1970 — Hau Nghia Province

> **Research stub.** The casualty record is confirmed; the operational circumstances are not.
> This page holds what is known and marks the gap explicitly.

**PFC Thomas Michael Brockmeier**, an infantryman with D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry, died of wounds on **March 7, 1970**, in **Hau Nghia Province**. Official records classify the casualty as hostile, died of wounds — small arms fire, ground casualty. He was 20 years old and had been in Vietnam four months and nineteen days. He is inscribed on the Vietnam Veterans Memorial, **Panel 13W, Line 91**.

### What is known

The date of death, province, unit, and cause are confirmed across all three primary sources. The date of wounding is not recorded — March 7 is when Brockmeier died, not necessarily when the contact occurred.

D Company was operating in the **Dog's Head** area of Hau Nghia Province — the westward bulge near the Cambodian border — during this period. The 1st Cavalry Division ORLL (AD0512505) confirms D Company in that area with a March 18 contact at **XT045833**, eleven days after Brockmeier was wounded. On March 7, C Company was engaged separately at XT178986 in Tay Ninh Province; that action produced ten wounded and no killed, and is unrelated to this casualty.

### What is not known

No contact entry for D Company appears in the ORLL for early March 1970. Single-casualty contacts were frequently not logged at division level. The grid, the date of wounding, and the specific circumstances of Brockmeier's action remain unknown.

### Next source needed

The **2/8 Cav daily staff journal** (NARA RG 472, early March 1970) is the primary outstanding source. A D Company entry in the Dog's Head area (low-easting XT grid) in the March 1–7 window would establish the wounding date, grid, and circumstances.

## Operation Comanche Warrior — War Zone D, 14 May – 1 June 1969

> **Context page — division level** (DTIC AD0506273). This was a **3rd Brigade** operation;
> **2/8 Cav (1st Brigade) was not in it.** It is documented here only to bound the quarter's
> operations and to prevent the May 25 D Co losses from being mis-attributed to it on the basis
> of date overlap.

Comanche Warrior and the overlapping Creek II were conducted by the **3rd Brigade**, roughly in
the same geographic location, both targeted against the **5th VC Division**; the ORLL discusses
them together. The 3rd Brigade's mission was to **interdict the southerly movement of the 5th VC
Division out of War Zone D** toward the heavily populated areas near the **Long Binh–Bien Hoa
complex**, to interdict known crossing points on the **Dong Nai River**, and to locate the
**174th and 275th Regiments** of the 5th VC Division.

Operations were characterized by small-unit patrols and ambushes along enemy lines of
communication. Although few sharp contacts were initiated, the period saw **very large cache
discoveries** — large enough that, even without decisively engaging the 174th and 275th
Regiments, intelligence indicated those and other 5th VC Division units withdrew north into War
Zone D base areas, partly due to food shortages.

**Combined results (Comanche Warrior + Creek II):** 132 enemy killed, 5 prisoners of war, plus
substantial weapons and foodstuff captures.

**Relevance to the cluster:** none directly — this is a different brigade in a different area of
operations (War Zone D). It matters only as a caution: the **25 May 1969 D Co losses fall in the
1st Brigade's War Zone C operation (Montana Scout), not Comanche Warrior**, despite the dates
overlapping.

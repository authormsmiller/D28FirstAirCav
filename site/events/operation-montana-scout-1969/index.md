## Operation Montana Scout — southern War Zone C, 1 April – 23 June 1969

> **Context page — division level** (DTIC AD0506273). This is the ongoing **1st Brigade**
> operation in War Zone C across May 1969 — 2/8 Cav's operation — but the report does not
> describe D Company's specific actions. Treat as **highly likely context, not fact.**

**Mission and form.** Montana Scout was **predominantly interdiction**, with the **1st and 2nd
Brigades deployed along the southern frontier of War Zone C**. Small-unit patrols and ambushes
along enemy lines of communication characterized the operation from **1 April to 23 June 1969**;
the northern half of the Montana Scout AO was covered extensively by aerial reconnaissance and
surveillance.

**1st Brigade activity (2/8 Cav's brigade).** Operating from LZs Grant, Carolyn, St Barbara,
Ike, Jamie, and White, the brigade placed **maximum emphasis on ambush operations** to interdict
enemy movement of personnel, equipment, and supplies. As visual reconnaissance and "usually
reliable intelligence" disclosed enemy concentrations in lower **Base Area 355**, the 1st
Brigade launched a **two-battalion seal-search-and-destroy operation with the 2/8 Cav, 2/12 Cav,
and a tank company** (11th ACR), and ran **saturation ambushes** to interdict movement into Base
Area 355. The report credits the **2/8 Cav with establishing LZ Becky.** In early–mid June, the
1st and 2nd Brigades (with the 11th ACR) attacked elements of the 95C and another regiment of
the **1st NVA Division** near the Crescent and in **AO GOLD**, with B-52 strikes preceding the
commitment of troops.

**Results.** 1,570 enemy killed, 39 prisoners of war; U.S.: 64 killed, 376 wounded, 19 aircraft
and one 105mm howitzer destroyed.

**Why it matters here.** Montana Scout is the **ongoing 1st-Brigade War Zone C operation that
spans the entire May 1969 cluster.** Because Montana Raider ended on 13 May, the **25 May 1969
losses (Karr, Garven, White) most likely fall within Montana Scout** — consistent with 1st
Brigade's Tay Ninh / War Zone C area and with the Tay Ninh province of the men killed days
earlier. This is highly likely context, not confirmation; the battalion journal is needed to
place the specific action.

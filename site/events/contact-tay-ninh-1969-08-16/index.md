## August 16, 1969 — sweep west of FSB Becky, Tay Ninh Province

In the central portion of **War Zone C**, **Company A and Company D, 2nd
Battalion, 8th Cavalry** conducted a ground sweep **west of Fire Support Base
Becky**. Ground contact was established at **0845 hours** and held until
**1000 hours**, when the enemy withdrew. A search of the contact site produced
**25 enemy killed**. A prisoner captured by A Company was identified as a member
of the **95th Regiment, 1st NVA Division** — confirming that unit's presence in
central War Zone C.

Friendly casualties were **1 US killed, 3 US wounded**.

**CPL Michael Dennis Muse**, 21, of Garland, Texas, was killed this day — his
official casualty record gives 16 August 1969, matching the ORLL exactly. He is
the only D Company, 2/8 Cav KIA on record for that date, five days after PVT
Daniel Zuniga died defending the same fire support base.

See **[CPL Michael Muse](/soldiers/muse-michael/)**.

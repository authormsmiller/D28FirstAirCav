  SOURCE: Find A Grave memorial 138857092 (obituary text).
  IDENTITY: Willis Garrett "Gary" Bacon; born 21 Jun 1931 in West Chester (Chester County), PA; died
  10 Nov 2014 in Bexar County (San Antonio), TX; buried West Point Cemetery, Section VIII, Row CC,
  Grave 357. Son of Capt. Robert L. Bacon and Helen Garrett Bacon. Brother: Col. (Ret.) Robert C.
  Bacon. Wife: Connie. Daughters: Elaine Bacon Peveto, Stephanie Bacon. Grandchildren: Taylor Heath,
  Bobby Holbrook, Christian Holbrook, Joshua Peveto.
  EDUCATION: Peacock Military Academy, San Antonio TX (1949); Stanton's Academy Prep School,
  Cornwall NY (1949–50); USMA West Point (1950–54), Class of 1954. Post-service: C&GS College;
  Naval War College; MBA, SMU Dallas.
  SERVICE: Two tours in Vietnam. Army General Staff (Pentagon); military advisory boards; overseas
  assignments. Retired as Colonel.
  COMMAND: CO 2/8 Cav from 16 Feb 1971 (ORLL AD0518422, command roster, period ending 30 Apr 1971).
  Preceded at 2/8 by LTC Robert Moore (took command 7 Oct 1970). Immediately followed by COL Thomas
  Blagg (Blagg was CO at Oct 21, 1971 Nui Ba Mountains contact; command end ~Jul 1971, per Michael).
  NOTE: Before 2/8, Bacon briefly commanded 1/8 Cav from 23 Jan 1971; transferred to 2/8 on 16 Feb,
  with LTC Edward L. Trobaugh succeeding him at 1/8 Cav on 14 Feb 1971.
  RANK NOTE: Hall of Valor lists LTC (Infantry) as his rank at time of Silver Star; he retired as
  COL in 1976 (Hall of Valor / Find A Grave). Standard pattern — held LTC during battalion command.
  HOME: Hall of Valor lists "Brownsville, Texas" as home; obituary places death in Bexar County
  (San Antonio) — may have relocated in later years.
  CIVILIAN: Member, Carrollton-Farmers Branch Rotary Club (also Director, Treasurer, Secretary,
  President); St. Andrews by the Sea Episcopal Church; 2008 Outdoor & Wildlife Preservation Award
  (Laguna Madre / South Padre Island, TX).

    body: >
      2LT Derosier was killed during a search-and-destroy operation near Bong Son, Binh Dinh Province
      (Operation Thayer I), while serving with D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry (Airmobile),
      1st Cavalry Division. At approximately 1500 hours his fifteen-man patrol was pinned down in an
      intense firefight with a reinforced company of North Vietnamese Army regulars, and Derosier —
      the patrol's platoon leader — was killed by small-arms fire. His battalion commander, LTC
      Thomas H. Tackaberry, landed his unarmed command helicopter beside the action, ran through the
      fire to reach the pinned-down patrol, took personal command, and led an assault that drove the
      enemy from their bunkers; for that action Tackaberry received the Distinguished Service Cross,
      its citation recording that "the platoon leader had been killed." 2LT Derosier was 23 years old.

  IDENTIFIED (2026-06-27) as the platoon leader killed in the action for which LTC Thomas H.
  Tackaberry (CO, 2/8 Cav) received his second Distinguished Service Cross. Tackaberry's DSC citation
  (HQ USARV General Orders No. 6537, 28 Nov 1966) describes a search-and-destroy operation near Bong
  Son on 19 Sep 1966 in which a 15-man 2/8 patrol's platoon leader was killed and the patrol pinned
  down by a reinforced NVA company. Date (19 Sep 66), unit (2/8 Cav), and circumstances match, and
  Derosier is the only 2/8 officer killed that day — so he is the platoon leader the citation names.
  PROVINCE CORRECTION: his DCAS casualty record gives Quang Tin (I Corps), but 2/8 Cav was operating
  in Binh Dinh near Bong Son on 19 Sep 66 (Operation Thayer I, begun 13 Sep) and the DSC citation
  places the action there; the Quang Tin entry is a DCAS error. Public page corrected to Bong Son /
  Binh Dinh per the coverage-model convention. Wired to Tackaberry as chain-of-command (Tier 4).
  FULL STORY: the action is documented on the event page contact-bong-son-1966-09-19 (built 2026-06-27,

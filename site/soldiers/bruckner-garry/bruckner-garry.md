    body: >
      Joined the company in the field by log bird (supply helicopter),
      working alongside the 11th Armored Cavalry Regiment. Assigned to a
      platoon and issued field gear. That night, the unit's night defensive
      position (tracks formed in a circle, foxholes dug between them) took
      eight incoming NVA mortar rounds -- none close -- before the unit
      relocated and left a platoon behind in the woodline for an ambush.
      Using a starlight scope, that platoon spotted four NVA soldiers moving
      in the open field the unit had just vacated; a platoon of tanks and a
      platoon of infantry engaged, killing one NVA soldier and capturing two
      AK-47 rifles, with no U.S. casualties.
    source_notice: >
      Sourced from Bruckner's account, "Vietnam: February 2-4, 1970."

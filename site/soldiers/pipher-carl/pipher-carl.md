      bunker complex on a three-platoon front; the fight continued through the night, with
      the enemy staying in their bunkers rather than pressing the attack. Company commander
      CPT Henry Colavita has given two independent firsthand accounts of this fight — a
      2016 oral history and his own memoir, which names Pipher directly. SP4 Pipher was 19
      years old. Full account: events/operation-sheridan-sabre-1969-01-28.

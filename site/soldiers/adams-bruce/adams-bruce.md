    body: >
      With 1SG Ramon "Blinky" Cruz on a 60-day reenlistment leave, Adams
      served as D Company's acting First Sergeant for roughly four months.
      "Old school" and widely disliked by the troops per Colavita, who
      nonetheless found they "got along just fine." During this period,
      Adams handled two perimeter-security incidents personally at LZ
      Rita: waking a sleeping bunker guard by hand, and firing a single
      M-79 warning round at a delinquent listening post that grazed
      soldier Jim Nix's nose. Full account, quoted in full: see the
      document colavita-henry-verbal-account-nix-lp-1968-11. See

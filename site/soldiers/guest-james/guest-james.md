notes: >
  COMMAND DATES: Michael supplied "1966-67" as the span of Guest's D Co, 2/8 Cav
  command with no further precision available yet — used directly for the
  service_record and top-level arrived/departed fields. Neither source gathered gives a
  month/day. The Green Beret Memorial bio adds that he followed the D Co command with
  an Assistant S-3 posting, 1st Brigade (Airborne), 1st Cav Division, during the same
  tour — unclear whether this falls inside or after the 1966-67 window.
  POSSIBLE D CO PREDECESSOR TO SCHOLES — FLAGGED, NOT CONFIRMED: Guest's D Co, 2/8 Cav
  command (1966-67) ends right where CPT Edison Scholes's own D Co command begins
  (1967-68, per soldiers/scholes-edison) — and the two men's careers run strikingly
  parallel up to that point: both are North Georgia College graduates (Guest
  commissioned 1960, Scholes 1961) and both served consecutive-generation postings at
  10th Special Forces Group, Bad Tölz, Germany (Guest 1962-65; Scholes 1963-66) before
  their respective Vietnam tours and D Co commands. This strongly suggests Guest
  directly preceded Scholes as D Co CO, which would push this archive's "Angry Skipper"
  D Co commander chain back a full command tour earlier than previously documented
  (chain was: Scholes 1967-68 -> Grannemann 1968 -> Garner 1968 -> Colavita 1968-69).
  Scholes's own obituary says he "earned the nickname Angry Skipper 6 from his
  commander in the 1st Cavalry Division" — this is more likely a battalion or brigade
  CO than his own company predecessor, so it should NOT be read as confirming Guest
  gave him the nickname. Neither source directly names Guest's successor or confirms a
  Guest-to-Scholes handoff — this is Michael's/this session's inference from adjacent
  dates and parallel career markers, not a sourced fact. A cross-reference note has
  also been added to soldiers/scholes-edison.md flagging this same open question. Worth
  checking the ARSOF Icon and Green Beret Memorial biographies for any other Guest
  contemporary (e.g., a possible connection to Rodney Grannemann or Audis Garner, the
  next two D Co commanders after Scholes) if further sources surface.
  RANK: rank field shows CPT (commissioned 2LT June 1960; by his 1966 Vietnam tour,
  roughly six years commissioned, consistent with company command); retired a Major
  General after 33 years.
  UNIT ASSIGNMENT DISCREPANCY (minor, not resolved): the ARSOF Icon bio names his first
  assignment as "Company D, 1st Airborne Brigade, 502d Infantry, at Fort Campbell, KY,"
  while the Green Beret Memorial bio instead calls it "502nd Airborne Infantry, 101st
  Airborne Division" (no post named). Treated here as the same assignment under two
  different naming conventions (the 1st Airborne Brigade, 502nd Infantry lineage traces
  to the 101st Airborne Division); not confirmed against a third source.
  DEATH: per the ARSOF Icon page, "He passed away on 3 April 2025 at age 86" — no cause
  of death given in either source. Born 12 April 1938, so he died nine days before what
  would have been his 87th birthday; the "age 86" in the source is consistent with this.
  DECORATIONS: full list is sourced entirely from the Green Beret Memorial biography
  page (not independently cross-checked against a citation or DD-214); the ARSOF Icon
  bio does not list decorations at all. Order in the decorations field above follows the
  source's own listing order.

This profile treats D Company as his effective unit per that reasoning and the KIA list's [att] marking; A Company is his administrative parent, noted here for the record, not his D Co service context. NOT organic to any D Co platoon -- inserted into Wild Cat's line for one night only, so platoon is left blank rather than set to Cat. SPELLING DISCREPANCY: Virtual Wall and Wall of Faces both spell the first name 'Elliott' (double-T); site/_docs/d-co-kia-list.md and Colavita's own memoir spell it 'Elliot' (single-T), matching this profile's slug. Keeping 'Elliot' for consistency with the slug and existing archive convention -- flagged, not silently resolved, same treatment as the Derums Karl/Carl and Williams Horton/Morton spelling flags elsewhere in this archive. LOCATION DISCREPANCY: Virtual Wall / Wall of Faces list Tay Ninh Province -- but 2/8 Cav's actual AO during Operation Sheridan Sabre was Binh Long Province (the 2nd Brigade's Fishhook AO, anchored on FSB Rita) -- the same casualty-record quirk already flagged on the Dec 4, 1968 and Jan 28, 1969 D Co KIA clusters (see events/operation-sheridan-sabre-1968-12-04 and -1969-01-28). CAUSE OF DEATH DISPUTED IN THE SOURCE: Colavita's memoir describes a disputed cause -- an explosion in an LZ Rita ammo pit on the night of 20-21 Jan 1969, with competing theories (self-inflicted, accidental or intentional, vs. an enemy satchel charge slung onto the LZ from a bent tree) -- the Army's official 'Artillery, rocket or mortar' classification (matching Virtual Wall's Casualty Detail field) is consistent with the memoir's own account but doesn't resolve which specific cause.

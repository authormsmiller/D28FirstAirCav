      CPL Waterman was killed on 3 September 1970 in Binh Tuy Province — officially
      recorded as a hostile ground casualty, misadventure (friendly fire). A Range
      Platoon comrade who was present described the incident: it was pitch black; Craig
      had gotten outside the wire for reasons no one has been able to explain; he tripped
      a flare near the position of another squad in the same platoon, and was fired on.
      He was 20 years old and had been in-country for 99 days. Why he was outside the
      wire that night is still not known.

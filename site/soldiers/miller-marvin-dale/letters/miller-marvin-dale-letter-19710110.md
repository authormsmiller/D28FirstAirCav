Marvin Dale Miller to his mother, 10 January 1971 — written the same day he went before the promotion board for Specialist 4. Family car talk, the military pay raise in the news, and a quiet stretch in the field: warm nights under a full moon, his buddies talking until one or two in the morning, and a ballgame to fill the afternoon.

---

10 Jan 71

Dear Mom,

Well how's everyone back home? I got a letter from you today and you said Jimmy got a small cut above his eye. I hope it isn't to bad.

So Dan finally decided to get a new transmission for the old Ford. If he could get the suspension fixed up it might not make a bad car. I'd still like to get a new car when I get back if I have enough money. I went in front of a board today to see if I'm going to get promoted to SP4. If I do it will mean a pretty good pay jump. The reason we have to go in front of a board is because rank is so hard to make in the first Cav. I know I'll be lucky if I get promoted so I'm not getting my hopes up.

I wonder if there's any new snow back home. I would think there would be some by now. If you get any big snowfalls make sure you get some pictures of it.

I suppose you've heard about the 7.9% pay raise the military's getting. That ought to help out a little bit. I'm glad to hear Buss got a raise too.

The last few days have been extra hot and it hasn't rained since I got here. I guess the rainy season doesn't start until 3 or 4 months.

There really isn't to much going on around here. The nights have been real pretty because of the full moon. So my buddies and I all get together and talk about things until 1 or 2 in the morning.

Well I guess I'll go play some ball.

Bye bye,
Love Marvin

### Archivist Notes

**The promotion board — written the day of:** "I went in front of a board today to see if I'm going to get promoted to SP4." This letter is dated **10 January 1971**, which directly corroborates the profile timeline's record that Marvin went before a promotion board on 10 January 1971; he was promoted to **Specialist 4 on 1 March 1971**. His own framing is notably modest — "I know I'll be lucky if I get promoted so I'm not getting my hopes up" — and he explains the process: "The reason we have to go in front of a board is because rank is so hard to make in the first Cav." Read against his enlistment date of **24 March 1970**, the promotion came at roughly eleven months of service — a normal first-year progression to E-4 — and his account of a board-controlled system fits a unit that managed promotions deliberately rather than by automatic time-in-grade.

**The "old Ford" and the new car:** "So Dan finally decided to get a new transmission for the old Ford… I'd still like to get a new car when I get back if I have enough money." The car-and-money thread continues from the 26 Dec 70 letter (the new car he wants, the bank loan he means to clear first); here he adds a target down payment in conversation with his brother **Dan**'s work on the family's old Ford.

**The 7.9% pay raise:** "I suppose you've heard about the 7.9% pay raise the military's getting." Military and federal pay were rising in this period as the country moved toward the all-volunteer force, and a pay raise was prominent enough in the news to reach the field. Marvin connects it to home — "I'm glad to hear Buss got a raise too" (his brother Don, on the civilian side).

**A quiet stretch — daily life before the storm:** The standout human detail: "The nights have been real pretty because of the full moon. So my buddies and I all get together and talk about things until 1 or 2 in the morning… I guess I'll go play some ball." A glimpse of ordinary morale in the dry-season lull, more than three months before the late-April combat north of FSB Fanning. The weather note ("extra hot and it hasn't rained since I got here… the rainy season doesn't start until 3 or 4 months") matches the III Corps dry season and echoes the 3 Jan and 28 Feb letters.

**Family:** "Jimmy got a small cut above his eye" — a younger member of the family circle, relayed from home; **Dan** (the Ford, the car authority) and **Buss** (Don, the raise) are his older brothers.

Written from FSB Jeffries after the company came in off the Oct 15–21 operation (per McGrew's calendar). A western-Pennsylvania note dates it neatly: he was "kind of surprised to hear the Pirates won the World Series" — Pittsburgh had taken Game 7 on 17 October 1971. He asks after his brothers Buss and Dan in hunting season and hopes his sister Mary Ellen's baby, due soon, arrives safely.

---

24 Oct. 71

Dear Mom,

Well here it is almost the end of October, it probably will be by the time you get this letter. I can't say I'm sorry to see it go.

It was real nice up until a couple of days ago when it started to rain again. I don't think the rainy season can last much longer. Right around the first of December should be the last of it. All in all, rainy season hasn't been as bad as I expected.

I got a letter from you today. You said the leaves were starting to change color. I'll be expecting some pictures soon.

I was kind of surprised to hear the Pirates won the World Series. I didn't really think they would win. I'm glad they did anyway.

How's Buss and Dan doing in small game season this year. I wonder if they've had any chances at deer with their bows.

I hope Mary Ellen doesn't have any trouble with her baby. She should have it pretty soon shouldn't she?

Well I hope everyone is getting along okay still. I'm doing all right over here.

I guess I'll get this in the mail now.

Love
Marv

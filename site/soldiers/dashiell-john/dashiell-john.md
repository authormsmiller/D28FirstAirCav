notes: >
  COL (Ret.) John Cofer Dashiell, nickname "Jack." Born 18 Mar 1927 at Moonefield
  farmhouse, Isle of Wight County, VA. Son of Harry G. Dashiell and Segar Jordan Cofer
  Dashiell. Raised in Smithfield, VA (Smithfield High School, Class of 1944). Finished
  senior year at Augusta Military Academy before VPI; entered Army as private, 1945.
  OCS → 2LT. Postwar Germany ~1 year. BA Fine Arts, College of William & Mary, 1950 —
  met wife Dorothy Jean "Dot" Smith (W&M Class of 1953) there; married May 1951.
  Korea: wounded Pork Chop Hill, July 1953 (Purple Heart, CIB).
  Vietnam tour: obituary says "1966-67" — arrived in-country 1966, assumed 2/8 command
  7 Feb 1967 (ORLL primary source). Two Silver Stars from Vietnam tour.
  Post-Vietnam commands: stateside brigade (1972-73); Senior US Military Observer
  Palestine (1974-75); Heidelberg Germany (final tour). Retired 1980 as Colonel.
  Post-service: wildfowl carver ("Ducks by Dashiell"), Alexandria VA; moved to
  Smithfield VA 1995 — home overlooking the Pagan River. Wife Dot died 2001 after 50
  years of marriage. Companion Marilyn Conklin Brugh in later years.
  Died 31 May 2017, Smithfield, VA, age 90, brief battle with cancer.
  Burial: Arlington National Cemetery (full military honors).
  Five children: John Jr. (Seattle), Nancy Brandon (Eye, England), Andrew (Denver),
  Paul (Williamsburg), Dorothy Benson (Leesburg). 10 grandchildren, 3 great-grandchildren.
  RANK NOTE: retired as Colonel — previous stub had LTC (his Vietnam rank); updated to COL.
  ARRIVED NOTE: obit says "1966-67"; command assumption per ORLL is 7 Feb 1967.
  Arrived set to "1966" to reflect probable in-country arrival; command date is separate.

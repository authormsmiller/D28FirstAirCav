        Assigned as Radio Telephone Operator (RTO). RTOs were priority targets; Silva
        was wounded by shrapnel when an enemy fighter fired a grenade launcher at him
        during an ambush — awarded Purple Heart. A fellow soldier shot the enemy fighter,
        saving his life.
        Participated in Cambodia Incursion (summer 1970); unit uncovered a massive
        NVA cache complex dubbed "The City."
        Lost two close friends during his tour: John Albert Hancock (friendly fire,
        December 1969; https://www.honorstates.org/profiles/321386/) and Francis "Frank"
        Ware (ambush, March 1970, Tay Ninh Province).

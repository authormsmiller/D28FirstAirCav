  Unit conflict between sources: Honor States lists Unit/Group as "1st Cav Division, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry, D Company" — the same D Co, 2/8 Cav designation used for the May 1972 Chinook batch — while Virtual Wall lists his actual unit as C Battery, 2nd Battalion, 19th Artillery, 1st Cavalry Division, matching his MOS (1193, Field Artillery Unit Commander) and rank (1LT). Published unit field uses the Virtual Wall value as the parent/administrative unit. Working theory (unconfirmed, not a primary source): as an artillery officer he was likely serving as a Forward Observer attached to D Co, 2/8 Cav at the time of his wounding — 2/19 Artillery was the direct-support artillery battalion for 1st Brigade's infantry battalions including 2/8 Cav, per 1st Cav Division Association unit history. This would explain Honor States crediting him to D Co, 2/8 Cav despite his parent unit being C Btry, 2/19 Arty. Needs a primary source (after-action report, VHPA-style incident record, or 1cda.org 2-19 FA history) to confirm the FO attachment before treating as fact.

  KIA 1967-12-11 — Died through hostile action — small arms fire (Virtual Wall: "Hostile,
  died outright; ground casualty; gun or small arms fire"; body recovered), Binh Dinh province,
  South Vietnam. DOB 1944-11-13. Hometown: Independence, Jackson County, MO. Wall: Panel 31E/79.
  Start tour: 1967-05-15 (Virtual Wall). MOS: 11H20 · Infantry Direct Fire Crewman. Grade E4.
  Age at loss 23. Unit (per sources): D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry (Airmobile),
  1st Cavalry Division, USARV.
  Service ID 55985479 — DO NOT PUBLISH (Army-era IDs are SSNs). Honor States profile ID 272764;
  VVMF Wall of Faces 16604.
  Decorations_unconfirmed are Honor States probability-based — move to confirmed when a primary
  source is located. (CIB is plausible for an 11H infantry MOS; Purple Heart is near-certain for
  a KIA.)
  OPERATIONAL CONTEXT: KIA during the Battle of Tam Quan (6–20 Dec 1967), the 1st Brigade's large
  fight against the PAVN 22d Regiment near Tam Quan / Dai Dong on the Bong Son Plain — itself part
  of Operation Pershing (12 Feb 1967 – 19 Jan 1968), the division's year-long Binh Dinh campaign.
  ACTION (per the 1st Brigade Combat Operations After Action Report, Battle of Tam Quan, 30 Dec 1967,
  DTIC AD390613 — IN HAND, filed at events/contact-tam-quan-1967-12-11/sources/): "Ambush elements of
  D 2-8 Cav located at BS 926048 had contact with an unknown sized enemy force at 2215 hours and 2245
  hours. The enemy was moving from north to south attempting to escape." D Co had air-assaulted to
  BS 925996 at 1106 on 10 Dec to block escape into the Cay Giep Mountains, and made the 19 Dec An
  Nghiệp air assault. The "small arms fire" cause fits the night ambush. 2/8 Cav CO at Tam Quan: LTC
  John Stannard; 1st Bde CO Col. Donald V. Rattan (signed the AAR); battle commander LTC Christian F.
  Dubia. The AAR does NOT name friendly casualties; which man fell at 2215 vs 2245 awaits the 2/8 Cav
  daily staff journal (NARA RG 472). See the event page contact-tam-quan-1967-12-11.
  CLUSTER: D Company lost two men this day — SP4 Follett (31E/79) and SP4 John P. Paulson Jr.
  (31E/82; Wall lines three apart). Causes differ (Follett small-arms, Paulson explosive device) —
  consistent with a close night ambush. Both profiles built; both wired to the Tier 1 event
  contact-tam-quan-1967-12-11 and to battle-of-tam-quan-1967.
  REMEMBRANCES on VVMF Wall of Faces (for later enrichment): James Follett (2016, likely family),
  Lucy Micik (2018), Dennis Wriston (2020), John Fabris (2022), Dale Eble ("Before & After," 2022);

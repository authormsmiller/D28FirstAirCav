  casualty under my command" — implying an earlier one. That connection is

  KIA 1968-11-18 — Hostile Died of Wounds, Artillery Rocket Mortar, Ground Casualty, South Vietnam, Tay Ninh province.
  DOB 1948-04-06. Hometown: Harper Woods, Wayne County, MI. Wall: Panel 38W/6.
  Start tour: 1968-07-24 (Virtual Wall). MOS: 11B10 · Infantryman.
  Unit (per sources): D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry (Airmobile), 1st Cavalry Division.
  Service ID 54978439 — DO NOT PUBLISH (Army-era IDs are SSNs).
  Decorations_unconfirmed are Honor States probability-based —
  move to confirmed when a primary source is located.
  CAUSE: casualty record reads "Hostile — Died of Wounds — Artillery/Rocket/Mortar —
  Ground Casualty." So he was wounded by enemy indirect fire and died of those wounds
  on (or recorded as) 18 Nov 1968; the wounding may have occurred slightly earlier.
  OPERATIONAL CONTEXT: a D Co, 2/8 Cav infantryman (11B10). 2/8 Cav was a 2nd Brigade battalion during Operation Sheridan
  Sabre (7 Nov 1968 – 4 Apr 1969), operating in the Fishhook / Saigon River AO. Cromie
  is the earliest archive D/2-8 loss of that operation — 8 days before the Ahern
  artillery case (26 Nov) and 16 days before the Jones/Stoltz/Williams cluster (4 Dec).
  See event operation-sheridan-sabre-1968.
  PROVINCE CAVEAT: DCAS/Honor States list "Tay Ninh Province," but 2/8 Cav (2nd Brigade)
  operated in the Fishhook / Binh Long AO per the 1st Cav ORLLs — so the Tay Ninh entry
  is likely the same DCAS province error seen on the Dec 4 men. Treat Binh Long / the
  2nd Brigade AO as the probable setting pending the 2/8 Cav daily journal (NARA RG 472).

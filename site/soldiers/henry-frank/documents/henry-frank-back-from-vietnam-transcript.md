Army Lt. Col. Frank L. Henry III was in Franklin Tuesday of last week to speak to the children of Cartoogechaye School, where his mother, Mrs. Catharine Henry is a fourth grade teacher, and to the Business and Professional Women's Club, of which his mother is president. He showed slides of Vietnam and gave very interesting and informative talks at both places.

The colonel, son of Mr. and Mrs. Frank L. Henry Jr., has just completed a second tour of duty in Vietnam. This time he was an infantry battalion commander with the First Air Cavalry Division. He entered service in 1952 and holds more than 20 awards and medals, including the Silver Star, the Distinguished Flying Cross, the Bronze Star for valor and the Gallantry Cross awarded by the South Vietnamese government. He is a helicopter and fixed-wing airplane pilot.

His next duty station will be at Ft. Benning, Ga. His wife the former Miss Emma Lee Higdon, of Asheville, and three children Samuel Lee 7, Debora 5, and Frank Joseph 13, who are presently living in Asheville, will join him there when school is out.

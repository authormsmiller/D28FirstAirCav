## New Chief Of Staff Dies

Funeral services for Col. Frank L. Henry, 44, chief of staff of the 101st Airborne Division (Air Assault) will be held Monday at 2 p.m. at Ft. Campbell's Central Chapel by invitation only.

Col. Henry died at Ft. Campbell at 11:55 p.m. Friday of a Berry Aneurysm, a post spokesman said. He was pronounced dead on arrival at Ft. Campbell Hospital's emergency room.

Col. Henry took over as Chief of Staff at Ft. Campbell on June 27 replacing the retiring Col. Ted A. Crozier. Before being appointed as Chief of Staff, Col. Henry was a brigade commander with the Third Brigade at Ft. Campbell. This was his third tour of duty at Ft. Campbell. He began this tour of duty on March 28, 1976.

Col. Henry entered the army in 1954 as a second lieutenant. He served two tours of duty in Vietnam and was awarded the Silver Star, four Bronze Stars, the Distinguished Flying Cross, the Vietnamese Cross of Gallantry with the palm twice, the Vietnamese Cross of Gallantry with the silver star, 15 air medals, the Meritorious Service Medal with one oak leaf cluster and the Combat Infantryman's Badge.

He was a Master Army aviator and a Senior Parachutist.

He is survived by his wife, Ema, of Ft. Campbell; three sons, Joe, a second lieutenant in the Army, and Frank and Samuel of Ft. Campbell; two daughters, Debora and Christa, of Ft. Campbell, and his mother, Catherine B. Henry, of Franklin, N.C., his hometown.

Memorial Services for Col. Henry will be held at 4 p.m. Monday at the Dyer Fieldhouse at Ft. Campbell. Burial will be at 10 a.m. Wednesday at Arlington National Cemetery in Arlington, Va.

Col. Henry will receive a full military funeral.

The family requests no flowers be sent and that any donations be made to the Col. Frank L. Henry Memorial Fund in care of the Chief of Staff Office, 101st Airborne Division (Air Assault), Ft. Campbell, Ky. 42223. The money will go to an orphans' fund begun at one of Col. Henry's old units with the 1st Air Cavalry Division at Ft. Hood, Tex.

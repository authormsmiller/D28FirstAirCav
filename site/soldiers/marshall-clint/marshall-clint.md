  Stub created 2026-06-30 from the 2/8 Cav 4 Nov 1965 AAR (KIA/AA Rpt 4Nov65.pdf) and
  Franklin Trapnell's eulogy in the ASA newsletter, Vol. 1 Issue 8 (June 2008). Clint Roger
  Marshall, born and raised in Snyder, Oklahoma; enlisted 1948 at age 19; ~22 years in the
  Army (82nd Airborne at Ft. Bragg, 101st Airborne at Ft. Campbell, Korea, Germany); retired
  1970. Recon Platoon Sergeant, D Co 2/8 Cav, ~18 months including the unit's first year in
  Vietnam. Married Jo Ann Hines (d. 1987; seven children), later Reba. Died 10 Feb 2008 at
  Snyder, OK, age 79, and buried there with full military honors.
  RANK NOTE: displayed rank is SSG (per archivist, his approximate grade as Recon Plt Sgt in
  1965); the eulogy refers to him at his RETIREMENT rank of MSG (Master Sergeant, retired

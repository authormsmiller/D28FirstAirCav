      Per Colavita's memoir, while D Company held the perimeter at LZ Rita,
      a listening post (LP) went overdue on its scheduled radio squelch
      check. Acting First Sergeant SFC Bruce Adams fired a single M-79
      grenade round toward the LP's position as an alarm; the round burst
      close enough that Nix, one of the soldiers on the LP, came in the next
      morning with "a vertical slice right in the center of the tip of his
      nose." Colavita closes the anecdote with a wry, parenthetical aside —
      "(Good shootin,' Bruce!)" — treating it as a story rather than a
      serious wounding. Exact date not given; placed here as an approximate
      November 1968 marker, during Delta's early weeks holding Rita's
      perimeter. Full account, quoted in full: see the document
      colavita-henry-verbal-account-nix-lp-1968-11. See also
      soldiers/colavita-henry and soldiers/adams-bruce.

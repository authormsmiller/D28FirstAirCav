      SP4 Kmit was killed instantly, walking point, when D Company was ambushed crossing a creek in its AO near FSB/LZ Saint Barbara, Tay Ninh Province — an action the company later nicknamed "Colavita Creek," which also took the life of CPL James Edmonds. His body could not be recovered immediately due to the ongoing situation and a subsequent stay-in-place order; he was recovered two nights later once a secure landing zone could be established. He was 23 years old and nearing the end of his tour. See events/operation-sheridan-sabre-1969-02-05/ for the full account.

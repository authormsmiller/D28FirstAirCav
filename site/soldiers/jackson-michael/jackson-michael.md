notes: >
  KIA 1970-03-17 — hostile, died outright, multiple fragmentation wounds, ground
  casualty. D Co, 2/8 Cav, 1st Cav Div. Panel 12W/13.
  DOB 1949-09-10. Age at loss: 20. Hometown: Simi, Ventura County, CA.
  Burial: Cardwell Cemetery, Cardwell, Dunklin County, MO — buried in Missouri
  despite California home of record; family ties to Missouri likely.
  Start tour: 1969-11-24 (Virtual Wall). MOS: 11C10 (Indirect Fire Infantryman —
  mortarman, not rifleman). Grade E3 per Virtual Wall.
  Induction: Selective Service draft (Honor States).
  Service ID 500523678 — DO NOT PUBLISH (Army-era IDs are SSNs).
  RANK DISCREPANCY: Virtual Wall records grade at loss as E3 (PFC). Honor States
  and VVMF record rank as "Corporal" (E4). Same discrepancy as Flashner (KIA
  Feb 28 1970, same unit). Using Corporal per the two named-rank sources; verify
  against NPRC if primary source becomes available.
  HONOR STATES ERRORS: (1) Lists county as "Sonoma County" — Simi is in Ventura
  County, not Sonoma. (2) Lists tour start as "24 November 1970" — should be 1969
  per Virtual Wall. Both are HS data entry errors; Virtual Wall figures used.
  FIREBASE ILLINGWORTH: FSB Illingworth (XT039792) opened March 17, 1970 — the
  same day Jackson was killed. Source: FSB locations list (sources/fsb-locations),
  noting "2/8th Cav 1Apr70. Open 17Mar-4Apr70." The coincidence strongly suggests
  connection but no contact entry for March 17 appears in the 1st Cav Div ORLL
  (AD0512505) — the log jumps from March 13 to March 18 with no intervening 2/8
  Cav entries. His specific location of death is unestablished. The NARA 2/8 Cav
  DSJ for March 1970 would be the next source to check.
  SP4 Stephen Alsup (D Co, 2/8 Cav, 11C20) was wounded the following day in a
  contact at XT045833 (~7km NE of Illingworth): 14 US WIA, platoon-size enemy
  force from N/NE/S, 19 NVA KIA found next day (AD0512505 entry (n)).
  AO CONTEXT: D Co was in the Tay Ninh Province / War Zone C area under LTC Michael
  Conrad (CO Jul 1969 – Apr 27, 1970). Jackson was the third D Co KIA in a month:
  PFC Henry Rava (Feb 18), CPL Kenneth Flashner (Feb 28), CPL Michael Jackson (Mar
  17). On March 8 the 2-8th Cav had contact NW of FSB Flashner (3 US KIA, 15 WIA
  per AD0511158) nine days before Jackson was killed.
  Decorations confirmed: three service medals (NDSM, VSM, VCM) — standard for
  Vietnam service. Decorations unconfirmed: Purple Heart (near-certain for KIA);
  CIB (11C10 qualifies — mortarman is an infantry MOS); Marksmanship Badge, APUC,
  VGC — Honor States probability-based.

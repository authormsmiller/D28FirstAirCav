WW

I was very saddened to here of Rocky's passing. My thoughts and prayers are with the Colovita family. He was a good man and leader. My father served under Rocky, and was killed in December of 1968. Not too long ago a mutual acquaintance introduced me to Rocky, and we had lunch together. He was troubled by the loss of his men. I believe he genuinely cared for them. I appreciated the time he spent with me...He provided a link to the past, of which I only have a couple (Rocky and one of his Platoon leaders). Only after we met did I find a YouTube video where he mentioned how the death of my father (really all of his men) troubled him. I then realized how much it must have taken to meet with me and face those troubling times again. Thank you Rocky. I pray that you are at peace with the warriors that went before you.

Chuck Williams

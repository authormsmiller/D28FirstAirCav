## Jacob Robinson Weldin

**SGT Jacob Robinson Weldin**, D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry, was killed in
action on **June 6, 1968**. He was from **Wilmington, Delaware**, and had arrived
in-country on December 27, 1967. He is inscribed on the Vietnam Veterans Memorial,
**Panel 59W, Line 17**.

> **Research stub.** Identity, decorations, and service dates are sourced from
> Honor States, Virtual Wall, and VVMF Wall of Faces. The combat narrative below
> is a strong candidate match to a single-casualty action recorded in the D/2-8
> company daily journal — the journal itself never names individuals, so this
> is circumstantial, not confirmed.

### What is known

The D/2-8 Cav company daily journal (a veteran-transcribed daily log covering
3 June-3 July 1968) records a single US KIA on **June 5, 1968**, at grid
**YD325393**: the company received small-arms fire at 1615, a man was wounded at
1745 and died of wounds at 1755, and his body was recovered at 1945 as the company
pulled back. No name is given. This is the only single-casualty D Company action in
the transcribed record close to Weldin's June 6 date of death, making it a strong
circumstantial candidate — matched by date proximity, unit, and casualty count, not
by name.

The one-day offset between the journal's action date (June 5) and the roster date
of death (June 6) fits a pattern seen elsewhere in this source: the daily journal was
kept in the rear off delayed field reporting, so personnel/status dates can lag the
actual date of action. See the event page for [June 5, 1968](/events/contact-1968-06-05/)
for the full reconciliation discussion.

Virtual Wall independently records the casualty location as **Quang Tri
Province** and the cause as hostile fire, died outright, ground casualty —
consistent with the journal's setting and with the short (ten-minute) interval
between wounding and death.

SGT Weldin's confirmed decoration is the **Purple Heart**. Honor States lists
several additional decorations as probability-based awards common to his era and
MOS (Combat Infantryman Badge, service and campaign medals, unit citation, and
foreign decoration); these remain unconfirmed pending a primary source.

### Next source needed

Confirmation of the June 5 action by name requires either the primary NARA 2/8
Cav daily staff journal (RG 472) or a casualty report naming Weldin directly.

      incident date is not recorded in official records; the approximate date of
      September 11 is drawn from the account of his son, Hugh Kent Sarah Wilson, who
      later retraced his father's final weeks. His platoon sergeant, MSG Clyde
      Bonnelycke, was present and later provided a hand-drawn map of the area to Hugh.

    phase: in-country
    type: combat
    tags:
      - { type: c, label: KIA }
    headline: "Died of wounds — September 23, 1969"
    body: >
      SP4 Hugh Henry Sarah died of his wounds on September 23, 1969, in Tay Ninh
      Province — approximately twelve days after the incident near LZ Ike. He was
      24 years old and had been in-country 84 days. Vickie's son, Hugh Kent Sarah,
      was born around the same week. Army Captain Kent Ronhovde, Sarah's closest
      friend from basic training at Fort Dix, escorted the casket back to Michigan
      at Vickie's request and helped her plan the funeral. Hugh Henry Sarah is
      buried at Oakland Hills Memorial Gardens. His name is on the Wall at
      Panel W17, Line 1.

  BIOGRAPHICAL NOTES (War Memorial article, warmemorial.org, accessed 2026-07-13):
  Born Plymouth, MI, 5 August 1945. Graduated Plymouth high school; promising fullback
  at University of Wyoming where he studied science. Degreed scientist and published
  writer. Married Vickie before deployment; she was pregnant when he died.
  Son: Hugh Kent Sarah, born ~late September 1969 (biological son; later Hugh Kent
  Sarah Wilson after Vickie remarried Kendall Wilson, a St. Albans/Georgetown grad).
  OCS friend: Kent Ronhovde — Sarah's "new best friend" from Fort Dix; both discussed
  OCS; Ronhovde went to Army intelligence as a Captain, later J.D. Georgetown + MPA.
  Ronhovde escorted Sarah's casket back from Vietnam and helped Vickie plan the funeral.
  Hugh Sarah Memorial Fund established at Eastern Michigan University (biology dept.
  lab equipment). Funeral: Schrader Funeral Home; service October 6, 1969.
  BONNELYCKE CONNECTION: MSG Clyde Lee Bonnelycke (Range Platoon sergeant) was
  present during the incident. Years later he met with Hugh Kent Sarah Wilson (Sarah's
  son), spent a day sharing war stories, gave the son a copy of a hand-drawn map
  marking the location where SP4 Sarah was wounded. Bonnelycke passed away November
  16, 2024. See: site/soldiers/bonnelycke-clyde/bonnelycke-clyde.md.

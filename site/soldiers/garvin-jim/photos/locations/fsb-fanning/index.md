    caption: >
      Aerial view of FSB Fanning with Garvin's own layout notes. The low perimeter wall was the
      berm, broken intermittently by guard positions, with the line company's hooches just behind
      it. The pie-shaped wedge at upper right held the TOC (Tactical Operations Center) — mess hall
      (the two tents on the far right), radio shack, medic station, quarters and supply points.
      Across the dirt road to the left of the TOC were the mortar platoon quarters and gun pits.
      The two dark areas at upper left and right are the helicopter pads. The six rings on the near
      side are the positions for the six 105mm howitzers (three stand empty — guns had gone out to
      the split base, King). The two yellow squares at upper left are fuel pillows of aviation fuel
      — one night the VC put a tracer into one and set it on fire. The trench at lower left was the
      garbage dump.

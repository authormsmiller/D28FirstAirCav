notes: >
  KIA 1970-02-28 — hostile, died outright, multiple fragmentation wounds, ground
  casualty. D Co, 2/8 Cav, 1st Cav Div. Panel 13W/68.
  DOB 1946-11-21. Hometown: New Orleans, Orleans Parish, LA.
  Start tour: 1969-12-07 (Virtual Wall). MOS: 11B10 (Light Weapons Infantry).
  Induction: Selective Service draft (Honor States).
  Service ID 435689242 — DO NOT PUBLISH (Army-era IDs are SSNs).
  RANK DISCREPANCY: Virtual Wall records grade at loss as E3 (PFC). Honor States
  and VVMF Wall of Faces (filename and page) record rank as "Corporal" (E4).
  Using Corporal per the two sources that explicitly name the rank; however this
  is unusual — E3 is PFC, E4 is CPL/SP4. The DCAS record (Virtual Wall source)
  may reflect grade at time of casualty while Honor States/VVMF may reflect an
  administrative correction. Verify against NPRC records if primary source becomes
  available.
  AO CONTEXT: February 1970 — D Co was in the Tay Ninh Province / War Zone C area
  under LTC Michael Conrad (CO Jul 1969 – Apr 27, 1970). PFC Henry Rava of the same
  unit (D Co, 2/8 Cav) was KIA in the same province just ten days earlier, February
  18, 1970. Flashner and Rava would have served together.
  FIREBASE: FB Mary Gwen (XT526796, coords 11.5755 / 106.3957) was opened by the
  2/8th Cav on February 24, 1970 — four days before Flashner was killed. The
  fragmentation-wounds/ground-casualty coding is consistent with an attack on a
  newly established firebase. Mary Gwen is almost certainly his location of death.
  Coordinate grid XT526796 places it in Tay Ninh Province, consistent with the
  Virtual Wall casualty location. Source: FSB locations list (sources/fsb-locations),
  citing 2/7 Cav DSJ.
  FIREBASE NAMED AFTER HIM: The II Field Force Vietnam ORLL for period ending 30
  April 1970 (AD0511158) records a "Fire Support Base Flashner (XT174 82)" — the
  OCR'd grid is likely XT17482, possibly XT174082 (6-digit). The battalion named a
  firebase after him, consistent with standard practice of honoring recently killed
  soldiers. On 8 March 1970, the 2-8th Cavalry "made contact with enemy forces
  northwest of Fire Support Base Flashner" — resulting in 2 enemy killed, 3 US
  killed, and 15 US wounded. Note: XT174082 is further west than Mary Gwen
  (XT526796), near the Cambodian border in War Zone C, indicating the battalion
  had moved west after Flashner's death.
  ILLINGWORTH: The same ORLL records the 2-8th Cavalry at FSB Illingworth
  (XT039792) on 1 April 1970 — the famous Battle of FSB Illingworth, which
  resulted in 24 US KIA and 54 US WIA. D Company was part of the 2/8 Cav at
  this time. Source: AD0511158 (IIFFV ORLL, period ending 30 April 1970).
  Decorations confirmed: three service medals (NDSM, VSM, VCM) — standard for Vietnam
  service. Decorations unconfirmed: Purple Heart (near-certain for KIA, move to
  confirmed when primary source located); CIB (plausible for 11B10 hostile ground
  casualty); Marksmanship Badge, APUC, VGC — Honor States probability-based.

notes: >
  KIA 1967-12-11 — Died through hostile action — explosive device (Virtual Wall: "Hostile, died
  outright; ground casualty; other explosive device"; body recovered), Binh Dinh province, South
  Vietnam. DOB 1947-08-15. Hometown: Neenah, Winnebago County, WI. Wall: Panel 31E/82. Buried at
  St. Margaret's Cemetery, Neenah, WI. Son of John Paul Paulson and Genevieve Marie Whitman.
  Start tour: 1967-05-14 (Virtual Wall). MOS: 11B20 · Infantryman (Honor States: "Light Weapons
  Infantry"). Grade E4. Age at loss 20. Unit (per sources): D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry
  (Airmobile), 1st Cavalry Division, USARV.
  Service ID 56455710 — DO NOT PUBLISH (Army-era IDs are SSNs). Honor States profile ID 291832;
  VVMF Wall of Faces 39827.
  DECORATIONS CAUTION: Honor States lists Silver Star, Bronze Star, and Air Medal in addition to the
  usual probability set. These are VALOR/service awards that Honor States may auto-populate; the
  Silver Star in particular should NOT be treated as confirmed without a primary citation or General
  Orders. All non-service-medal awards held in decorations_unconfirmed pending a primary source.
  OPERATIONAL CONTEXT: KIA during the Battle of Tam Quan (6–20 Dec 1967), the 1st Brigade's fight
  against the PAVN 22d Regiment near Tam Quan / Dai Dong on the Bong Son Plain — part of Operation
  Pershing (12 Feb 1967 – 19 Jan 1968). ACTION (per the 1st Brigade Combat Operations After Action
  Report, Battle of Tam Quan, 30 Dec 1967, DTIC AD390613 — IN HAND, filed at
  events/contact-tam-quan-1967-12-11/sources/): "Ambush elements of D 2-8 Cav located at BS 926048 had
  contact with an unknown sized enemy force at 2215 hours and 2245 hours. The enemy was moving from
  north to south attempting to escape." D Co had air-assaulted to BS 925996 at 1106 on 10 Dec to block
  escape into the Cay Giep Mountains, and made the 19 Dec An Nghiệp air assault. The "explosive device"
  cause fits the night ambush (mine/grenade/RPG). 2/8 Cav CO at Tam Quan: LTC John Stannard; 1st Bde CO
  Col. Donald V. Rattan (signed the AAR); battle commander LTC Christian F. Dubia. The AAR does NOT name
  friendly casualties; which man fell at 2215 vs 2245 awaits the 2/8 Cav daily staff journal (NARA
  RG 472). See the event page contact-tam-quan-1967-12-11.
  CLUSTER: D Company lost two men this day — SP4 Paulson (31E/82) and SP4 Allan E. Follett (31E/79;
  Wall lines three apart). NOTE: causes differ — Paulson by explosive device, Follett by small-arms
  fire — so the two may have fallen in related but not identical incidents within the same day's
  Tam Quan fighting, rather than a single shared blast. Both wired to battle-of-tam-quan-1967.

    body: >
      As commanding officer of Company D, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry, Capt. Linton directed
      the company's part in the 4 November 1965 action near Position "Cavalair" (grid 979049)
      during the Pleiku Campaign. The fight opened with the D Company Reconnaissance patrol
      (Lt Ward) and drew in successive elements — "Buckeye," "Flanker Right," "Right Guard"
      (A Co), and C Co — against a company-plus PAVN force in a rest area. He prepared and
      signed the battalion's after-action report two days later, recommending decorations for
      his troopers, including posthumous Bronze Stars with "V" for the three D Company men
      killed (SGT Coffey, CPL Hill, PFC Hamill) and a Bronze Star with "V" for the wounded
      senior medic, SP/4 David "Doc" Wilson.

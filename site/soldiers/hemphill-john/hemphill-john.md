notes: >
  Command stub (status: researching) — Bacon/Blagg/Tackaberry model. LTC John A. Hemphill,
  commander of 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry ('Stone Mountain 6'). CONFIRMED as sitting CO 2/8 Cav
  in the 1st Cav Div ORLL dated 15 Aug 1966 (command roster, no change of command logged), and
  independently listed at 2/8 in 1965 on the unit command roster (KIA/Commanding-Officers.txt).
  Because he is named in 1965 AND mid-1966 with no intervening change, he was almost certainly the
  2/8 battalion commander across the entire window of SGT Paul Malec (arrived 20 Sep 1965, wounded
  21 Mar 1966) — closing a CO gap no AAR or the Interim Report could. Linked to Malec as his
  chain-of-command "Served Alongside" (Tier 4 / commanding officer). Relieved by LTC Thomas H.
  Tackaberry on 30 July 1966 (per the 22 Nov 1966 ORLL command roster — the date is faint but
  legible). Source PDFs: sources/orll-1cd-1966/.
  BIOGRAPHY (Wikipedia / Army Executive Biographies, 1985): John Allen Hemphill, b. 19 Dec 1927,
  Boise, Idaho; d. 21 May 2021, Steilacoom, WA; interred West Point Cemetery. USMA Class of 1951.
  Korean War Distinguished Service Cross (Co I, 3/31 Inf, 7th ID, 17 Apr 1953). Wikipedia confirms:
  "As a lieutenant colonel in 1966, Hemphill commanded the 2nd Battalion (Airborne), 8th Cavalry,
  1st Cavalry Division (Airmobile) during his first tour in Vietnam" — a third independent
  confirmation alongside the 15 Aug 66 ORLL and the unit roster. Second Vietnam tour as a colonel,
  CO 3rd Brigade, 9th Infantry Division (1968–69). Retired a Major General (Director of Operations,
  US Readiness Command). Career awards incl. DSC, Silver Star (2), DFC (2), Bronze Star (5),
  Purple Heart (3). Title kept at LTC here to reflect his rank while commanding 2/8 Cav.
  HUMAN DETAIL (from his SECOND tour, not the 2/8 era): during Hemphill's 1968–69 command of 3rd
  Brigade, 9th Infantry Division (as a colonel), a soldier under him — Patrick A. Tiessonniere,
  Silver Star — later recounted (Twilight Tattoo, Fort Myer, 2014; AFBA / Army News Service) that
  Hemphill once came face-to-face with a Viet Cong soldier and thought he was dead, until the enemy
  was shot: "My platoon saved [Hemphill's] life that day." Color on the commander, unrelated to Malec
  or 2/8 Cav.
  CONFIRMATION + IDENTIFIERS: his Legion of Merit (2nd award) citation explicitly names him
  "Commanding Officer, 2d Battalion, 8th Cavalry Regiment, 1st Cavalry Division," action date
  1965–1966 — a fourth, official confirmation of the 2/8 command alongside the ORLL, the roster note,
  and Wikipedia. Army service number ASN 0-64015. DATE DISCREPANCY: Military Times Hall of Valor lists
  his dates as 1 Dec 1937 – 29 Aug 2012, but that birth year is impossible for a 1951 West Point
  graduate who earned a DSC as a first lieutenant in 1953; Wikipedia's 19 Dec 1927 – 21 May 2021
  (interred West Point) is internally consistent and retained here. Sources:
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_A._Hemphill ; https://valor.militarytimes.com/recipient/recipient-7428/

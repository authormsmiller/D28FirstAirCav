        requests for transfer to the rear (most of which Colavita
        describes as "trial balloons" with no real chance of approval).
        Colavita says he rarely needed to interact with him directly day
        to day and didn't know exactly where "the rear" was based (he
        suspected Quan Loi). Due for promotion to 1LT "soon" as of the
        chapter's telling; exact date not given.

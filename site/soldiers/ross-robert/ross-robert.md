## Robert James Ross

**SGT Robert James Ross**, D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry, was killed in
action on **July 1, 1968** — his 21st birthday. He was from **Charlotte, North
Carolina**, and had arrived in-country on October 27, 1967 — over eight months
before his death. He is inscribed on the Vietnam Veterans Memorial, **Panel 54W,
Line 32**.

> **Research stub.** Identity, decorations, and service dates are sourced from
> Honor States, Virtual Wall, and VVMF Wall of Faces. The combat narrative below
> is the strongest of four candidate matches found in the D/2-8 company daily
> journal, though the journal itself never names individuals.

### What is known

The D/2-8 Cav company daily journal records a contact with an estimated 2-3 NVA
soldiers on **July 1, 1968**, at grid **YD293319**: a man was wounded at 0930 and
died of his wounds at 1230. The action also produced one NVA KIA and a captured
AK-47 rifle (serial number 51747). Later that day, at 1315, a separate NVA KIA was
found buried in a bunker nearby at YD296319, attributed to a First Platoon ambush
from four days earlier, June 28.

Virtual Wall independently records the cause of death as hostile fire, gun or
small arms, in Quang Tri Province — matching the journal's account exactly. Of the
four D Company losses reviewed against this journal, this is now the
best-corroborated: exact date, single casualty, matching cause of death, and no
casualty-count discrepancy to reconcile. It remains a candidate rather than a
confirmed identification only because the journal never names individuals.

Robert Ross was born July 1, 1947. If this identification is correct, he was
killed in action on his 21st birthday, after more than eight months in-country.

SGT Ross's confirmed decoration is the **Purple Heart**. Sources differ slightly
on his exact MOS code (Honor States: 11B; Virtual Wall: 11H20), though both
describe an Infantry Direct Fire Crewman role. Honor States lists several
additional decorations as probability-based awards common to his era and MOS;
these remain unconfirmed pending a primary source.

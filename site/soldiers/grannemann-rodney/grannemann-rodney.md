        1968) -> Garner (confirmed in command by 24 Jun 1968, through ~Oct
        1968) -> Colavita (Oct 1968-~1969), each carrying the "Angry
        Skipper-6" callsign in turn.

      As Company Commander of D Company, 2nd Battalion (Airmobile), 8th

      cache (36 bunkers, mortar rounds, rockets, TNT, an AK-47 magazine, and
      NVA gear) was found later the same day, 1710 hours, at grid YD326393.
      Source: the D/2-8 Cav company daily journal (Skipper Journal, 3 Jun-3

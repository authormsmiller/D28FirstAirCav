    body: >
      CPL Edmonds was mortally wounded at a creek crossing in D Company's AO near FSB/LZ Saint Barbara, Tay Ninh Province — an ambush the company later nicknamed "Colavita Creek," which also killed SP4 Chester Kmit. The landing zone was too hot for a helicopter to land, so a jungle-penetrator lift was arranged to evacuate him by cable; during the lift, enemy fire led the medevac crew to cut the cable, and Edmonds fell. A Medical Service Corps colonel formally investigated afterward; company commander CPT Henry Colavita testified the cut was not necessary and has said he believes Edmonds may already have died of his wounds before the fall. He was 20 years old. See events/operation-sheridan-sabre-1969-02-05/ for the full account.

## LZ Betty

**LZ Betty** was a 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry position in **Quang Tri Province**,
I Corps — described only as being "to the west of Quang Tri." No grid coordinate
has been found in any source reviewed so far.

> **Research stub.** Two independent references place the battalion here, five
> months apart. Neither gives a grid. The name itself is disputed for the earlier
> of the two.

### What is known

On the night of **March 15, 1968**, LZ Betty was the site of the mortar and rocket
attack that killed **LTC Howard Petty**, commanding officer of the 2nd Battalion,
8th Cavalry — see [that event](/events/contact-lz-betty-1968-03-15/) for the full
account. A separate Wall of Faces source names the position **LZ Sharon** instead;
the most likely explanation, per the event page, is that the Sharon reference
describes a subordinate company's own position rather than the battalion command
post.

Three months later, the **D/2-8 Cav company daily journal** (the "Skipper
Journal," a veteran transcription covering 3 June-3 July 1968) independently uses
the name **LZ Betty** with no ambiguity, recording the company on base defense
there during an untranscribed gap from **June 10 to June 18, 1968**, before moving
on to LZ Pedro. This second, unambiguous use of the name is a small point in favor
of "Betty" being correct rather than "Sharon" — but it does not resolve the
dispute, and no grid is given either time.

### Next source needed

The **2/8 Cav daily staff journal** (NARA RG 472, March and June 1968) is the
outstanding primary source — it should settle both the name dispute and the
position's actual grid coordinates.

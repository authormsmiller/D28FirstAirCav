## Overview

**FSB Guin** (also: Guinn) was a 1st Cavalry Division fire support base in **Binh Tuy
Province** (Military Region 3, III Corps), established by the **2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry**
on **4 September 1970**. It is documented in the firebase gazetteer under references
AD515205 and AD516259.

Guin followed **FSB Powder Ridge**, located approximately 67 kilometres to the northwest.
The scale of that move — too far to cover on foot, across Binh Tuy terrain — meant the
battalion shifted by helicopter. In the four days between leaving Powder Ridge (31 August)
and standing up Guin (4 September), **CPL Craig Thomas Waterman** of Range Platoon was
killed on **3 September 1970**. See the
[September 3, 1970 contact page](/events/contact-binh-tuy-1970-09-03/) and
[CPL Waterman's profile](/soldiers/waterman-craig/).

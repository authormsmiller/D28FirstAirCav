## Overview

**Gia Ray** was the **village and fixed-wing airstrip** just outside **FB Mace**, at the eastern foot of **Núi Chứa Chan** (Relay Mountain) in **Long Khánh Province**. It was not a firebase but the local air gateway to the Mace area: when D Company, 2/8 Cav had to move in or out in numbers, the men were trucked between Mace and the Gia Ray strip and flown by **C-7 Caribou**. Jim Garvin called Gia Ray "a typical rural village" and "the road [I] would travel many times."

## Location

Gia Ray sits at the foot of Núi Chứa Chan, near present-day **Gia Ray town** in the Xuân Lộc district area, just outside the former FSB Mace. The archive carries no verified MGRS for the strip itself; the coordinate here is **approximate and unverified**, anchored to the modern town beside the mountain.

## What the deck shows

- **14 December 1970** — a Caribou at the Gia Ray strip during the company's arrival lift (it took several planes to relocate the company), and Gia Ray village from the road.
- **17 March 1971** — D Company being divided and loaded aboard Caribous as each plane came in, for the lift to Vung Tau; and a **Cessna O-1 Bird Dog** (L-19) liaison/observation aircraft parked on the strip the same day.

## Related locations

Gia Ray is inseparable from **[FSB Mace](/locations/fsb-mace/)**, the anchor base it served, and sits below **Relay Mountain** (Núi Chứa Chan), the signal installation on the summit above.

## Overview

**LZ Jane** was a landing zone in the **Song Re Valley** corridor, Quang Ngai Province, I Corps, used by **D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** on **17 August 1967** — one day before the redeployment order that sent the battalion back to the Pershing AO. Like its neighbor **[LZ Tom](/locations/lz-tom/)** (BS347482, 18 Aug), it carries no formal name in the firebase gazetteer, appearing there only as "LZ Unnamed."

The position sits approximately 12 km north-northeast of LZ Tom and Ta Ma Airfield. The timing — D Company present on 17 Aug, then at LZ Tom as a pickup zone on 18 Aug — points to LZ Jane serving as a **consolidation or staging point** as the brigade assembled for extraction from the valley. The 3d Brigade's reconnaissance in force ran 1–19 August; both LZ Jane and LZ Tom fall on its final two days.

## Location

The gazetteer fixes LZ Jane at **BS362596 (15.0013°N, 108.5422°E)**, in the upper Song Re Valley corridor north of Ta Ma Airfield. It lies within the same tight cluster of five unnamed August 1967 LZs, all sourced from gazetteer ref 179 and all representing airmobile activity during the 3d Brigade's two-week push through the valley.

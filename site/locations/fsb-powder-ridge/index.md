## Overview

**FSB Powder Ridge** was a 1st Cavalry Division fire support base in **Binh Tuy Province**
(Military Region 3, III Corps), held by **D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** in
August 1970. The firebase gazetteer places the 2/8 Cav here from **11 to 31 August 1970**.

The unit's next position, **FSB Guin**, stood up roughly 67 kilometres to the southeast on
**4 September 1970**. In the four-day gap between the two firebases, **CPL Craig Thomas
Waterman** of Range Platoon was killed on **3 September 1970** — the day before Guin was
established. See the [September 3, 1970 contact page](/events/contact-binh-tuy-1970-09-03/)
and [CPL Waterman's profile](/soldiers/waterman-craig/).

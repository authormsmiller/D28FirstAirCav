## Overview

**FB Gonder** was a 1st Cavalry Division fire support base **inside Cambodia**, established during
the division's **Cambodian Incursion** (1 May – 30 June 1970). The 2/8 Cavalry firebase-by-year
working data places it at grid **XU807383**, occupied from **20 May to 27 June 1970** by **2/5 Cav
and 2/8 Cav**, with **B/2/19th (Airborne) Artillery** (105mm) and, from 29 May, **C/1/30th
Artillery** (155mm) in support. It lay in the same grid square as **Picatinny East** (XU807369),
where C Company, 2/8 Cav worked an enemy weapons cache between 27 May and 2 June — part of the
chain of cache discoveries the incursion overran in the eastern border region.

## The name

The base almost certainly honors **[CPL Kenneth Walter Gonder](/soldiers/gonder-kenneth/)** of
**D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry**, who died on **19 May 1970** — one day before the base
opened. Naming a new fire support base for a recently killed soldier of the occupying unit was
common practice, and the one-day gap, the matching battalion (2/8 Cav held the base), and the
timing all point the same way. This is a **strong circumstantial inference**, not yet confirmed by
a primary naming citation. (Gonder's own death was recorded as **non-hostile** — an accident in
Binh Duong Province — so the base carries his name as a memorial, distinct from where and how he
died.)

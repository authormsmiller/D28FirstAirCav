## Overview

**FSB Fanning** was a 1st Cavalry Division fire support base in the Long Khanh / Binh Tuy area (Military Region 3, III Corps), held by **D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** in the late spring of 1971. It is documented by **Jim Garvin's photo deck** (v06_fanning) -- Garvin served in **Range Platoon ("Range 1")**, and his Fanning chapter runs from his first mention of the base in a letter dated **11 May 1971** to **30 June 1971**.

Fanning followed **FSB Fontaine** in the battalion's chain of moves: Garvin wrote that "the battalion had just made another move to Fire Support Base Fanning," and Fontaine had closed on 8 May 1971 -- the two dovetail. On or about **24 May 1971** the battalion **split Fanning into two firebases** of three 105mm howitzers each, the second being **FSB King**, in the hope that the reduced, reshaped positions would be easier to defend.

## Layout

From Garvin's annotated aerial: the low perimeter wall was the **berm**, broken intermittently by guard positions, with the **line company's hooches** just behind it. The pie-shaped wedge at upper right held the **TOC (Tactical Operations Center)** -- the **mess hall** (two tents on the far right), the **radio shack**, the **medic station**, and quarters and supply points. Across the dirt road to the left of the TOC were the **mortar platoon quarters and gun pits**. Two **helicopter pads** sat at upper left and right. At upper left were two **aviation-fuel pillows** -- one night the VC put a tracer into one and set it ablaze. The near side of the base carried the positions for **six 105mm howitzers** (three stood empty, their guns gone out to King). A trench at lower left served as the **garbage dump**.

## What the deck shows

- **The firebase itself** -- a Sky Crane lifting the dozer out after it built the berm, and Garvin's annotated aerial of the whole layout.
- **Air support** -- close looks at an **AH-1 Cobra gunship**: rocket pods, the 40mm grenade-launcher magazine and chin turret, and the gunship's punch at night.
- **People** -- **Gary Collins** in the rocket-pod frame; Garvin himself present.
- **The road in** -- logging trucks along **Highway 1** as Garvin convoyed up to the base.

## Operation Overlord (June 1971)

In early June 1971, FSB Fanning was the headquarters base for 2/8 Cav's part in **Operation Overlord**, a 1st Australian Task Force (1 ATF) hammer-and-anvil operation against the **3/33 NVA Regiment** in AO Gold (the Long Khanh / Phuoc Tuy border area). An operation-level account records that as the operation opened on **5 June 1971**, "Headquarters 2/8 Battalion [remained] at FSB Fanning," with **three companies in blocking positions along the Suoi Luc** and a new US artillery base, **FSB Hall**, established to support the operation. While 3 RAR and supporting armour drove the enemy from the northwest, the 2/8 Cav and Australian blocks formed the anvil.

Howard McGrew's calendar (a primary D Co, 2/8 Cav source) tracks the same month from inside the unit: **"June 5 - build FSB Hall,"** then **"June 7 - Fanning,"** combat assaults on **9, 13, and 16 June**, and **"June 21 - CA to Fanning and to Vung Tau."** The 5 June "build FSB Hall" matches the 1 ATF D-Day date-for-date, and the entries show Fanning serving as the staging hub between assaults -- the late-June build-up leads straight into the **24 June 1971** Firefight on the Mountain and Bunker Complex sweep already in the archive.

This independently confirms Fanning as **2/8 Cav's primary firebase** in this period -- matching the Stars and Stripes clipping (from Jean "Dizzy" Blais) that calls it "2nd Bn., 8th Cav's primary firebase" -- and fixes the firebase gazetteer's "5-14 Jul 71" Overlord survey as a June date mis-transcribed. The source is secondary (compiled from Peter Scott, *Command in Vietnam*; Mike English, *The Rifleman*; Jerry Taylor, *Last Out*), so it is treated as corroboration of the 2/8 record rather than a primary 2/8 document.

## Namesake

FSB Fanning was named for **[Martin V. Fanning](/soldiers/fanning-martin/)**, killed in action 24 April 1971 — recorded in the firebase gazetteer as "For Martin V. Fanning USA KNBC 24Apr71."

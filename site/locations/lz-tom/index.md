## Overview

**LZ Tom** was a pickup zone in the **Song Re Valley**, Quang Ngai Province, I Corps, used by **D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** on **18 August 1967**. It sits near **Ta Ma Airfield** at grid BS347482 and served as the D Company extraction point on the final day of the **3d Brigade Reconnaissance in Force into the Song Re Valley (1–19 August 1967)** — an operation that carried no formal name beyond that designation in the 1st Cavalry Division's quarterly ORLL.

The operation was a temporary reassignment: 2/8 Cav was placed under **operational control of the 3rd Brigade** and sent north to Quang Ngai Province — an I Corps tasking for an otherwise II Corps battalion. The Song Re was known as a Viet Cong stronghold; this was the first major reconnaissance in force into the valley, and recent reconnaissance aircraft had already taken heavy antiaircraft fire before the operation opened.

The brigade pushed into the valley on 1 August, with 2/8 Cav air assaulting into the area north of the Song Re on **3 August**, then committing into the valley itself on **9 August**. That day's sequence was precisely timed: at **0737 hours**, B Company air assaulted into **LZ Lou** to secure it as the battalion command post and firebase; **C Battery, 2nd Battalion (105mm), 19th Artillery** was operational on Lou by approximately 0900. At **0935**, under C Battery's preparatory fires, A Company air assaulted into **LZ Pat** (BS330477, Hill 450) — and was immediately met with heavy automatic weapons, mortar, and 57mm recoilless rifle fire from an estimated two to three NVA companies. The company pulled back; 46 TAC AIR sorties were called in. Operations continued until **18 August**, when redeployment back to the Pershing AO was initiated. D Company's PZ use at LZ Tom on that day was the extraction — not an insertion. Final elements closed the Pershing AO around **20 August**.

The operation resulted in 42 enemy KIA, 2 POW, and 242 detainees; US losses were 7 KIA and 34 WIA across the brigade.

## Location

The gazetteer fixes LZ Tom at **BS347482 (14.8982°N, 108.5294°E)**, in the upper Song Re Valley roughly 35 km west-southwest of Quang Ngai city, near **Ta Ma Airfield**. The terrain is mountain valley — steep ridgelines flanking a narrow river corridor that was a known infiltration route.

The nearest named landmark in the same source is **LZ Pat** (BS330477, Hill 450), about 2 km to the southwest — the site of a costly combat assault by A/2/8th Cav on **9 August 1967** (11 KIA, 27 WIA, three aircraft shot down). LZ Tom's D Company PZ use nine days later places it in the same operational tempo.

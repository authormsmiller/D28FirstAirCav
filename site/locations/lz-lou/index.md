## Overview

**LZ Lou** (also recorded as **FB Lou** in the firebase gazetteer) was the battalion command post and artillery firebase for **2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** during the opening phase of the **3d Brigade Reconnaissance in Force into the Song Re Valley (1–19 August 1967)**. It sits at grid **BS400425 (14.8472°N, 108.5792°E)** in Quang Ngai Province, I Corps — approximately 7 km east-northeast of LZ Tom in the upper Song Re corridor.

On **9 August 1967**, B Company air assaulted into LZ Lou at **0737 hours** to secure it as the battalion command post and firebase. **C Battery, 2nd Battalion, 19th Artillery (105mm, Towed)** — designated C/2/19th (Abn) Arty — was operational on the position by approximately **0900 hours**. With that artillery cover in place, A Company conducted its own combat assault into **LZ Pat** (BS330477, Hill 450) at **0935 hours** under C Battery's preparatory fires. That assault met immediate and heavy contact from an estimated two to three NVA companies — 11 KIA, 27 WIA, three aircraft shot down — and required 46 TAC AIR sorties before the position was secured.

The firebase remained active after the initial assault. Gazetteer ref 270 records **2/B/29th Artillery** at nearby BS395424 on **12 August**, and **D/2/17th Artillery** at BS395425 on **15 August**, showing artillery presence in the Lou area for at least a week. Redeployment from the Song Re was initiated on 18 August; by that date, D Company was being extracted from LZ Tom (BS347482) to the southwest.

## Location

The gazetteer fixes the primary C Battery position at **BS400425 (14.8472°N, 108.5792°E)**. The 12–15 Aug entries cluster at BS395424 and BS395425, roughly 500 meters to the west-southwest — likely reflecting different battery positions or a minor firebase displacement over the course of the operation.

LZ Lou lies approximately 7 km east-northeast of LZ Tom (BS347482) and about 8 km east of LZ Pat (BS330477). All three sit within the ref 179 / ref 270 cluster of Song Re Valley positions from this operation.

## Overview

**LZ Moberly** (also recorded as **FB Moberly** in the firebase gazetteer) was a **Special Forces CIDG camp** at **Ba To Airfield (Ba To AF)** in Ba To district, Quang Ngai Province, I Corps. It sits at grid **BS558321 (14.7547°N, 108.7265°E)**, approximately 16 km south and 22 km east of LZ Tom in the broader Song Re Valley operational area.

The camp predated the **3d Brigade Reconnaissance in Force into the Song Re Valley (1–19 August 1967)**. The gazetteer records artillery at Moberly as early as **5 August** under the Op Pershing umbrella, indicating it was already an established rear staging and fire support base when the main assault opened on 9 August. During the operation, **D Company, 5/7th Cavalry** used it as a pickup zone on **15 August**.

On **18 August 1967** — the day the battalion's combat operations in the Song Re concluded — the 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry was airlifted to LZ Moberly and spent the night there. The following morning, **19 August**, the battalion was airlifted by **Caribou** (CV-2 / DHC-4 STOL transport) from Moberly's airstrip to **LZ English** at Bong Son in Binh Dinh Province. The gazetteer records **C Battery, 1/77th Artillery (105mm, Towed)** at BS558326 on **19 August** — the same extraction day — confirming artillery elements departed on the same morning.

## Location

The gazetteer fixes Moberly across three nearby coordinates — BS558321, BS558325, and BS558326 — all within roughly 500 meters and all associated with the Ba To Airfield. The tight cluster reflects different unit positions within the same SF camp complex rather than separate locations.

Ba To was a known Special Forces CIDG camp in Quang Ngai Province. The "Ba To AF" designation in the gazetteer confirms a fixed-wing-capable airstrip consistent with Caribou operations.

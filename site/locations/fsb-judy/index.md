## Overview

**FSB Judy** was a fire support base in the Long Khanh / Binh Tuy area (Military Region 3, III Corps), "**down the road from Fanning**" in Jim Garvin's words. **D Company, 2nd Battalion, 8th Cavalry** logged at Judy on **4 June 1971** -- the date on Garvin's two photographs of the base. Unlike Fanning and King, which carried 105mm howitzers, **Judy mounted 155mm howitzers**, a heavier piece.

## What the deck shows

- **A 155mm howitzer** at Judy.
- **The 155mm ammunition storage** on the base.

## Dates & naming

The firebase gazetteer fixes FB Judy at **YT662042** and logs 2/8 Cav there **30 June – 1 August 1971** — a **battalion-level** window. Garvin's deck and McGrew's field calendar both put **Range Platoon** at the base on **4 June 1971** (Garvin's platoon logged there; McGrew recorded "CA to FB Julie"). Platoon and battalion dates are expected to diverge — individual platoons moved on their own schedules — so the 4 Jun platoon date and the 30 Jun – 1 Aug battalion window are not in conflict. On the name: because the gazetteer carries no "Julie" entry and names this position **Judy**, Judy is taken as the accurate name and "Julie" as a calendar mis-transcription.

## Operation Overlord staging (June 1971)

The 4 June 1971 occupation falls **one day before** Operation Overlord's D-Day (5 June 1971, the date Howard McGrew's calendar records as "build FSB Hall"). That timing, together with Judy's **155mm howitzers** — heavier than the 105mm pieces at Fanning and King — strongly suggests Judy was a **staging and artillery pre-positioning step for Operation Overlord**, not a routine platoon resupply: D Company combat-assaulted to Judy on the eve of the operation, and its heavy guns could range the AO Gold blocking area alongside the newly built **FSB Hall**. This reframes the 4 June platoon date as deliberate pre-operation staging — distinct from, and helping explain the gap to, the 30 June – 1 August battalion window. Treated here as a strong inference from the converging dates (McGrew calendar + the 1 ATF Overlord account), not yet a documented operational fact.

Col Peter Scott's Overlord order of battle (*Call to Remembrance*, 2021) names a single US 155mm battery in support — **Battery C, 5th Battalion, 42nd Artillery** — which would tie Judy's heavy guns and the newly built **FSB Hall** to the same US artillery unit backing the operation. (See the [Operation Overlord](/events/operation-overlord-1971-06/) event page and the FSB Hall research note.)
